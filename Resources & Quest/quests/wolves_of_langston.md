## The Wolves of Langston (`Langston/`) - IN PROGRESS

* **Source and scope:** A personal-use, world-adapted version of *The Wolves of Langston: Second Edition*, implemented one chapter at a time. Player-facing prose is rewritten and the mechanics are converted to this game's systems. The working design ledger is `WOLVES_OF_LANGSTON_ADAPTATION_PLAN.md`.
* **Built - Chapter 1, The Adventure Begins:** The player may approach Langston at any time, handles a wolf pack through one of three checks, meets Roi Sunderhammer, reaches Maybelline's funeral at the Pickled Hen, and may inspect Tomlin, Roi, Solaine, and Mayor Tanya Anathram. The town-funded room advances the clock by eight hours through the shared sleep system. Anathram offers 15 Silver Marks plus ordinary room and meals; accepting pays a replay-safe 2 Silver Mark advance and points the player to Tomlin for Chapter 2.
* **Improved branching:** The road failure carries disadvantage into the funeral confrontation. The confrontation supports persuasion, intimidation, or handing control back to Anathram. Every funeral clue is optional and failed checks do not reveal it. Refusing the contract leaves it offered instead of ending the adventure.
* **Not yet built:** world/map entry, Langston hub and shops, Lorebook entries, Chapters 2-6, epilogue, final payment, completion credit, rewards, and aftermath.
* **Key variables:** `wol_quest_stage`, `wol_chapter`, `wol_chapter_1_started`, `wol_chapter_1_complete`, `wol_advance_paid`, the `wol_road_*` route flags, `wol_met_*`, and the individual `wol_tomlin_*`, `wol_roi_*`, `wol_solaine_*`, and `wol_mayor_*` clue flags. State is declared in `startup_parts/51_wolves_of_langston.txt`.
