# The Nine Court & Lyra's Investigation Plan

## 1. Lore & Faction Identity: The Nine Court

* **Who They Are:** A secretive, insular cabal composed of ancient imperial patrician bloodlines entrenched within the Valen Reach. While newer nobility and common folk think of the "Meridian Empire" as the bureaucratic power that collapsed thirty years ago in the Broken Crown War, the Nine Court traces its lineage and rituals back thousands of years to the *First Imperial Dynasty*.
* **The Archaic Disconnect (The "Cleopatra vs. The Pyramids" Principle):** Just as Cleopatra lived closer in time to the modern era than to the building of the Great Pyramids, the late Meridian Empire was millennia removed from its primal origins. The late Empire that fought the Grey Marches was structured, civilized, and viewed the ancient dawn-era gods and rites as bloodthirsty barbarism. The Nine Court rejects late-imperial "softness," covertly practicing archaic purifications, ancestral blood-preservation rites, and devotion to forgotten gods that civilized society would find abhorrent.
* **The Production Line (Compartmentalized Secrecy):** 
  * The Nine Court requires complex, handcrafted components for their archaic ceremonies (e.g., ritual binding vestments, tallow-soaked burial cords, bone-inlaid spindles, or specific astrological tapestries).
  * To ensure total operational security, the Court never allows a single workshop or artisan to see a completed ritual object. They operate a strictly compartmentalized assembly chain.
  * Anonymous brokers distribute small, seemingly mundane tasks to isolated artisans in the poorer quarters (Dredge-End and lower Middle Ward): one spins a specific mineral-treated cord; another dyes flax in a specific noxious mordant; a third braids it into bands. None of the workers know what they are assembling or who commissioned it.

---

## 2. Lyra's Aunt & The Murder

* **The Aunt's Role:** An expert spinner living in the leaning timber tenements of Dredge-End. To keep food on the table for her two young children while Lyra was away, she accepted well-paying piecework from an anonymous middleman: spinning rare, treated fibers.
* **The Fatal Mistake:** Her aunt was too observant for her own good. She noticed that the thread carried an archaic knot-pattern or emitted a distinct resinous scent, and made an off-hand remark or asked a courier a question.
* **The Liquidation:** To the Nine Court, a Dredge-End spinner is expendable vermin. Within days, she was quietly murdered in her bed. Her workbox and remaining spools were stripped, and artificial rent arrears were levied to make her death look like an ordinary, uninvestigated slum tragedy—starvation, fever, and eviction.

---

## 3. The Hask Connection (The Bridge)

* **Hask's Friendship:** Hask, the chandler and fence in Dredge-End, was a close friend of Lyra's aunt for years. He knew her craft, traded with her, and knew her death was no accident.
* **The Bad Blood with Lyra:** Lyra cannot speak with Hask. When Lyra fled the Black Oath three winters ago and took *"something they wanted back,"* she made herself a traitor to the syndicate. If Lyra enters Hask's shop, Hask will order her out or call street muscle.
* **The Player's Role:** Hask will not discuss his friend's murder with just anyone. The player must earn Hask's confidence (through scrap trades, courier tasks, or achieving associate standing). Once trusted, Hask can reveal what he salvaged from the aunt's room before the stewards cleared it—or the description of the strange courier who visited her.

## 4. Quest Structure: *The Broken Spindle* (Working Title)

Following the standard Branch-and-Bottleneck architecture:

### **Beat 1: The Broken Thread (Hask's Revelation)**
* **Requirement:** Lyra's *Five Silver* debt repaid; player has established trust with Hask in Dredge-End.
* **Action:** The player brings Hask an inquiry regarding the aunt. Hask reveals that before she died, she gave him a pawned tin box or a spool of unusual, heavy cord she hid from her handlers.
* **Handoff:** The player takes the clue back to Lyra at the barracks or a quiet lane. Lyra recognizes the material and points out that the dye and oil come from a specialized shop in the Middle Ward.

### **Beat 2: The Intermediary in Middle Ward (The Assembly Trail)**
* **The Setting:** A dye-works, weaver's loft, or apothecary in the Middle Ward that handles the intermediary step in the production line.
* **Tactical Approaches (Archetype Parity):**
  * *Stealth/Finesse (DEX):* Infiltrate the loft rafters or picking the ledger desk after hours.
  * *Social/Streetwise (CHA):* Pose as an agent of the Court or intimidate the dyer into revealing his handler.
  * *Cunning/Investigation (INT/WIS):* Analyze the delivery slates and identify the drop location on the border of Civic Heights.
  * *Combat/Force (STR):* Intercept the courier when they come to collect the finished bundle.

### **Beat 3: The Gathering in Upper Valen (Climax & Discovery)**
* **The Setting:** A private townhouse, embalming hall, or ancestral vault in Upper Wharves or the lower terraces of Civic Heights where the components are assembled into the finished ritual garment.
* **The Discovery:** Lyra confronts the reality of the Nine Court. She learns her aunt was murdered for a passing comment, revealing the terrifying reach of ancient patrician families who treat slum lives as ink to be blotted out.
* **The Resolution / Dilemma:**
  * *Vengeance / The Assassin's Arrow:* Helping Lyra put a bodkin arrow through the patrician handler, avenging her aunt but risking heat from high-society watchmen.
  * *Leverage / Blackmail:* Stealing the completed ritual vestment and proof of the Court's barbaric practices, securing immunity and lifelong funding for her cousins' room and board.
  * *Exposure / The Legal Net:* Handing the evidence over to the Port Watch or Gilded Scales authorities, exposing the archaic cult cell at the cost of drawing their ire.

---

## 5. Narrative & Design Guardrails

1. **No Black Oath Villainization:** Ensure dialogues clearly separate the Black Oath's street crime from the Nine Court's archaic patrician atrocities. The Black Oath are transactional crooks; the Nine Court are an elite, esoteric bloodline cult.
2. **Lyra's Agency:** Lyra remains an archer and a survivor. She should have distinct tactical input during Beat 2 and Beat 3, taking overwatch positions with her recurve while the player operates on the ground.
3. **World Scale:** The quest starts in Dredge-End, crosses Middle Ward, and touches the fringes of Civic Heights, physically reflecting the social ladder of Port Valen.
