## What you have already decided (moved here from The Overdue Peddler.md)

* **Hook:** a request on Ambrose's own blackboard board outside the Still-Room.
* **What he asks for:** 3 shadefern and 2 acorn truffle, in one delivery, no deadline. Those two plants grow only in the Old Grove (Longshade Forest, Room 6), so the player has to find it.
* **Reward:** payment only. Ambrose pays above market price when everything is brought in at once. No tonic and no discount.
* **Where:** it starts and ends in Port Valen, at Ambrose's Still-Room in Herb-Pounder Close, and the player carries the plants back from the forest.
* **Not a Hall job:** it is a favour for a shopkeeper, so no Insignia and no posting on a board.

## What it looks like in play (short version)

1. A blackboard beside the Still-Room door lists what he wants. It can be read at any hour.
2. By day, the player asks him about it, learns why he wants the plants and roughly where they grow, and takes the order on.
3. The sidebar lists it as an active quest and counts the plants as the player gathers them.
4. Back at the Still-Room with 3 shadefern and 2 acorn truffle, the player lays them out and is paid. The blackboard is wiped.

Plants the player gathers beyond the five he asked for stay theirs, and he still buys them at the counter as before (shadefern 2 copper, acorn truffle 3 copper each).

### 2. Asking Ambrose about the blackboard (daytime only, once the blackboard has been read)

**New option in the Still-Room menu:** **"Ask Master Ambrose about the blackboard."** It appears after the player has read the blackboard and before the order is taken.

**Opening prose (first time only):**

>"I saw that you're seeking for shadefern and acorn truffle" you say.

> Master Ambrose looks up from a row of amber bottles with a dropper still in his yellow-stained fingers. His pale eyes go to the door, and then to you. "Ah," he says. "You've read it."

**The player's questions (each appears once and is then gone; they can be asked in any order, and the player can leave at any time):**

**Choice A: "Why those two plants?"**

> "Does no one in the city supply them?" you ask.
>
> He sets the dropper down and pushes his spectacles up his nose. "Hardly, supplies are always unreliable." The voice is dry and calm, the way it is when he talks about anything. "Shadefern cools can cool migraines, but it also sharpens and quickens the mind too. Acorn truffle I use in a restorative that took me twenty years to get right, it strengthens the blood and improves your constitution. Can't grow those in a garden, the best places to buy them are from adventurers, mercenaries and brave gatherers who go into deep forests." 

**Choice B: "Where do they grow?"**

> "Where would I find them?" you ask.
>
> "The nearest deep wood is the Longshade, among the oldest trees so I've been told." He lifts a hand and lowers it, as if drawing a line on the counter. He gives you a look over the spectacles. "I won't draw you a map. I've never been there, and I don't trust what I haven't walked."

**Choice C: "Who brought them to you before?"**

> "Who brought them before?" you ask.
>
> His hands go still on the counter. "A gatherer. A quiet fellow, who came up from the river country twice a season with a pack on his back, all frames and straps." He looks at the amber bottles. "He stopped coming, and I've not heard word of him since. I hope he's okay"

*(See question 3 below: this is where the Aldis Pellam connection would go for a player who finished The Overdue Peddler.)*

**Choice D: "What will you pay?"**

> "What does it pay?" you ask.
>
> "Three silver marks, for the lot." He taps the counter once. "That's above market price, by more than half. I pay more for a delivery I can use the same day, and for a clean cut."

**Then the decision (shown once the player has asked what they want, or immediately if they ask nothing):**

* **"I'll bring them."**
  > "Three of the fern, two of the truffle," you say.
  >
  > "Ensure they're clean." Master Ambrose nods, and for the first time something like warmth crosses his face. "Mind the cuts. Fern bruises if you look at it hard, and truffle spoils in a wet pack. Wrap them separately, in something dry."
  >

* **"That's not for me."**
  > "Not for me," you say.
  >
  > "No." He takes up the dropper again. "It's a lot of danger for a few leaves."

**If the player asks again after declining (described, not quoted):**

> Master Ambrose glances up from his bottles at the question on your face, and nods toward the door. Nothing on the blackboard has changed: three fern, two truffle, three silver marks, the lot at one time.

### The seasonal repeat (built 2026-10-19)

After the first order is done, a new blackboard is posted every season ("WANTED AGAIN", with that season's numbers: 2 to 4 shadefern and 1 to 3 acorn truffle, paid in silver marks as the pieces less two, so at least one). It is asked about in one short, described scene, and the order is gone when the season ends if it has not been delivered (silently; the first order never lapses). Every delivered order counts one toward level progress. The numbers come from the season, not from a die, so the same season always posts the same order.
