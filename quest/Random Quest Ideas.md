# Random Quest Ideas

Rough Quest Idea, these are my own thoughts, or pulled from Dnd worlds that need to be converted to our own that I found woould be fun to turn into quest or short random encounters or used as something that could springboard other quest. Avoid parroting these ideas back to me unless I myself actively mentioned it.

--------------------------------------
Random Encounters. 

HELPING YOUR ELDERS
An old lady (stat block of a Commoner), carrying a heavy
basket tries to pass the road. She is visibly struggling —
but no one stopped to help her so far.
The party can decide to help, and the lady will engage
in conversation — sharing that she is planning to go to
the market, to sell the vegetables in her basket.
She will thank the party at the end of the journey with
a payment of 3 SP.
NELIA CVORZA - HUMAN FEMALE
Hard worker, haunted by the memory of her lost children.
Nobody knows that she kilted them on accident, and she does
not share - but this burden weighs heavily on her

(A 1 time Encounter, doesnt repeat.)

CAUGHT UP IN A WEDDING
The party gets caught up in a wedding celebration —
handed drinks, food and pushed towards a nearby
square, to celebrate together with the happy crowd.
On the event the following things can happen:

ld6

Happening
Drinking contest DC16 Constitution saving
throw or become drunk.
(Passive Perception 13)

A great performance - one of the players gets
handed a lute and is ushered to perform.
(Performance check DC12) On a success, great
ovation and a rain of coins is the reward. (a few silver maybe)

The tripping bride - Players can try catch her
with a DC14 Dexterity saving throw. (Not sure about this one, possible boon)

Food fight— the pastries and creamy sweets
start flying around covering everyone in a
lighthearted food massacre.

Priest's blessing — a priest joins the event
providing prayers and blessings for everyone
involved
(Could come with a boon)

(Could be repeating event)
