Longshade events

ABANDONED CAMP
In a forest clearing the remains of a campfire and some
broken tents can be found. A DC12 Survival check will
identify that this place has been abandoned in the last
couple of days and that it was ravaged by some sort of
predator (panther)
Investigating the ruins (I)C12 Investigation check)
Players can find a pouch of gold.

The campers — the players can try to
follow their tracks, only to realize that they did not
manage to escape the beast — and lie dead about 200 feet
away from their campsite

( not repeatable)

THE SMELL OF A BEAR
A foul stench hits your noses as you approach an
otherwise not noteworthy forest patch. A DC12 Survival
check will lead you to the source — a large Brown Bear
laying on its side, emitting gasses loudly into the air.

It is visibly in pain — (probably need a medicone check for this) reason being it did not consume
enough plants — its diet was too meat oriented in the
past weeks.

In case the party helps the bear any way to get better —
either by providing some plant-based material(ration) or
helping the bear move, it will be grateful and slowly walk
away.

The bear's pain is easiest to iidenitfy with to speak with animals.

THICK Moss
Walking the forest ground yo ur feet touch something
unusually soft. You look down and see that you are
walking on a I-foot-th ick moss cover. It seems oddly
large, oddly overgrown.
With a DC12 Perception check the party can identify
that the color of the moss is slightly different from that
of similar greenery they saw earlier.
With the same check th ey can identify that the moss
grows in an almost perfect circular shape on the ground,
and that its middle is slightly more elevated.
If the party decides to dig, they will find that the same
moss grows even underground, similarly thick, similarly
lush green — but also only within the circular area.
The moss cover is created by an ancient, buried power
source, that is located 15 feet beneath the ground. It is a
Brass cage, with a glowing white gemstone inside,
capable to transmit power to many different purposes —
among others growing plants, providing heat, providing
light.
To get this source of power the party needs to identify
that whatever this circle is, the effect comes from
underneath the ground — and dig for the item

THE LONE CUB
A little brown bear lost and scared — looking for its
parents in the depth of the forest.
The will not be hostile, and players can help it
reunite with its family by passing a DC15 Survival check
and finding the trail. The family will be on the other side
of a nearby river, the cub itself not being able to traverse
— has been left behind.
Players can help the cub through, and in the process
befriend it — DC12 Animal handling checks are required
to make the bear doo certain actions — on these checks
the party has advantage if food is involved

DARK FRIEND
From the bushes a small Panther walks out slowly. It
looks very satisfied and is neutral to the party. It will
jump up a tree and find a comfortable sleeping posi tion.
Players with a DC12 Nature check can identify that the
animal has recently eaten — therefore is extremely
happy and has no care whatsoever of anyone being
around in its territory.

THE TALKING TREE
An old looking tree eerily resembles a face. As soon as
the party would write this off as a coincidence, they can
see the tree open two yellow eyes and turn towards them.
The tree can be used as a "quest giver" a source of
information, or a side plot to find out why he is able to
• The tree speaks common and is friendly to the players.
• It refers to itself as "Bark".
• It has a hushed voice and loves company.
• Not a lot of people come into this part of the forest and
realize that a sentient tree is here. Those who do,
usually run away.
• It does not remember its past, for as long as Bark
remembers it has always been a tree

BARK - TREE
Large, green leafed, healthy looking tree with face like
protrusions on the trunk in 5-foot height. It has yellow eyes and
loves company. Bark has not much to tell but can share details
about how the forest came to be, as it is almost the same age as
all other trees.
Bark mentions that it can communicate with the trees through root
connections — they share general emotions and images with it —
and can relay their needs, worries or happiness through the
connection. 

Recently the trees have been complaining about a dark presen
ce deeper in the woods, that has been posioning one of the water streams near a grove and slowly corrupting the northwest area of the forest. If the party helps end the source of this corruption, Bark promises a reward.

While traveling deep in the forest, the
adventurers happen upon a violent feud
between two tribes of sprites vying to
add a sacred grove to their own territory. While such
disputes among the fey are not uncommon, it is rare for
these hostilities to escalate into warfare.

Coilltean Grove is tended by a dryad caretaker named
Flùràlainn. She has unsuccessfully
tried to negotiate a truce between the Briarvine and
Thornbranch sprite tribes - even offering to evenly
divide her home between them if it would stop the
war. When diplomacy failed, she resorted to using
force to dissuade them from their actions. This
prompted the sprites to set her most beloved tree
ablaze.

The player arrives as the frantic dryad attempts
to douse the flames. The distraught Flùràlainn begs for
assistance (20 gallons of water to extinguish the tree or
it is destroyed in 4 rounds).
