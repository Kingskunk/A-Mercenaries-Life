# Random Quest Ideas

Rough Quest Idea, these are my own thoughts, or pulled from Dnd worlds that need to be converted to our own that I found woould be fun to turn into quest or short random encounters or used as something that could springboard other quest. Avoid parroting these ideas back to me unless I myself actively mentioned it.

INNKEEPER 'S DAUGHTER
Players can meet a lady in love working in the inn,
seemingly in pain and worry. She is the daughter of the
innkeeper, wishing to marry to the son of the tanner
living nearby. She wants the party to help convince her
father, who is very much against the intentions.
If the players agree, they can talk to the father who
will share that he is only afraid to lose his daughter,
fearing that she would grow cold to her family due to the
marriage. This is clearly a false assumption — players
can convince the father with a DC12 Persuasion check or
pay him (50GP) to change his mind.
If players try intimidation, the father will not be open
to discuss any further.
Upon success the lady will thank profusely and will
pay their accommodation within the inn.
Later (in Id12+20 days) a wedding can be observed in
the city — that of the lady and the tanner's son.
KORY KANTAS - HUMAN INNKEEPER
His main value is family and loves his daughter above all else.
He has simple values, simple life, no real intention to cause
harm to anyone.
Roll for 2 Flaws, and i Appearance in the NPC setup tables.
IRINA KANTAS - HUMAN DAUGHTER
She is friendly but soaked in worry and sadness. If her
problem is resolved, she will be your best friend in the city,
helping wherever she can.
Roll for 1 Flaw, 1 Strength, and 1 in the NPC setup
tables
