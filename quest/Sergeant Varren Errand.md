# Sergeant Varren's Errand

## Quest Overview

* **Questgiver:** Sergeant Varren (Iron Carrion Armory Shed)
* **Locations:** Carrion Compound, Middle Ward (TrunkRoad), Civic Heights (Cathedral of the True Sun), Patrician Quarter (Avir Manor).
* **Format:** Designed specifically for **one player character**. Encounters, damage output, and investigation checks are scaled for one character, and failed checks fail forward. The only way to die is to be defeated in the final fight with Trent.

---

## THE HOOK: SERGEANT VARREN'S ERRAND

The player finds **Sergeant Varren** in the **Armory Shed** at the Carrion Compound, sitting by the grindstone, mending shield straps with an awl.

### Varren's Dialogue & Briefing
> Varren punches the awl through a strip of oiled leather and pulls the waxed cord tight with his teeth. He doesn't look up from the bench. Tells the player the issue, that the issue with the serial killer, the watch has been trying to catch him but resources are thin, the people are in fear all leads they have tried so far have been dead ends, it's almost like someone is trying to throw them off the scent, ever lead they get vanishes into thin air. He points the player to Bronner Trent, who may have more answers and are looking for travelers or adventuers, fixers to help solve this issue. If the Iron Carrion solves this issue, it would bring much goodwill, softpower and reputation to the company.

## ACT 2: INVESTIGATION & THE CATHEDRAL

Before walking into a patrician's estate, the player has two local leads: street talk in Conduit Square, and the wounded Kenny at the Cathedral.

### 1. Street Talk (Conduit Square)
Rumors or talk around the communal bathhouse or the *Wagoner's Rest* taproom requires or any place in the city that has rumors a to gather infomation **DC 11 Charisma (Persuasion or Streetwise)** check.

* **Success (11+):** A wagon driver and a baker confirm two facts:
  1. **Ulrich wasn't in town:** Ulrich of Avir was upriver in Alderford on timber business during the nights of the 5th, 6th, and 7th murders. He only returned yesterday.

### 2. The Wounded Survivor (Cathedral of the True Sun)
On Civic Heights, (We don't yet know his name, but I assume we get this person's name from the Deacon themself.) Kenny lies in the quiet side chapel of Saint Althea. **Deacon Harl Vey** tends the cots.

#### The Situation 
Kenny has a fractured skull, cracked ribs, and heavy blood loss from crossbow bolts. He is wrapped in clean linen, breathing in shallow, fevered rasps. He cannot easily be cured on the spot.

#### Gathering the Clues:
* **The Deacon’s Testimony:** Deacon Vey speaks plainly:
  > *"The boy was half-drowned and bleeding out when the Watch dragged him in. I set the ribs and stitched the lung, but the blow to his head keeps him in the dark. In his fever last night, he kept thrashing—screaming about Captain Trent sending them to die."*
* **Kenny’s Possessions (Automatic Clue):** 
  Deacon Vey points to Kenny's mud-stained haversack under the cot. Inside is:
  * A crumpled, blood-stained map of Avir Manor, written in **the exact same handwriting** as the warrant Trent just handed the player.
  * A note from Trent promising Kenny 10 Silver Marks if he *"leaves the study window unlatched."*
* **Attempting to Question Kenny (DC 11 Intelligence / Medicine or Wisdom / Perception):**
  * **Success:** Using smelling salts or pressing a damp cloth with cold spring water to Kenny’s neck brings him around for twenty seconds. He wheezes:
    > *"Trent... set us up. Said the desk had proof... but the guards were standing right inside the door. He wanted us dead..."*

    (we obviously don't have smelling salts or cold spring water, I am assuming these items are nearby and the place noticies them, that could be the medicine check noticing these items and being able to use them on him succesfully.)

  * **Fail-Forward (<11):** Kenny groans and coughs blood, slipping deeper into delirium without speaking. However, his violent coughing fit dislodges something hidden in his boot lining, the it's a handwritten, wax-sealed **Search Warrant** identically to the one that was given to you by trent.

## ACT 3: AVIR MANOR (PATRICIAN QUARTER)

If all else fails and the player goes directly to the Avir Manor in the patrician quarter, The player must cross the **Inner Gate** to reach Ulrich’s estate on the Avenue of Estates.

### Passing the Inner Gate
* **Legal:** Buy a **Day Writ (1 Silver Mark)** at the Gilded Scales Head House on Civic Heights from Registrar Ashe. The player can attempt to show the to the guard, refuse the writ, and says it does not have the required seals to enter, and that they will need a writ from the Registrar to pass.

### The Infiltration (Solo-Balanced)
Avir Manor is an elegant limestone mansion with manicured gardens and high slate roofs. Four private house-guards patrol in pairs.

#### Approach A: The Stealth Run (DC 12 Stealth & DC 12 Sleight of Hand / Lockpicking)(this DC is 16 during the daytime)
Because there is only one character, sneaking is fast:
* **If Successful:** The player bypasses the courtyard guards, slips through the second-floor balcony, and enters Ulrich's private study.
  * **What They Find in the Study:**
    1. Financial notes and receipts from the Factors Hall proving Ulrich was staying at the in Alderford during the murder nights.
    2. **Ulrich's Jounral log one of it's last entries:**
       > *"Third trespass this month. Intruders carried forged militia orders signed by Captain B.Trent. Trent bears a grudge after Lord Ulrich refused to fund his armed rabble."*

#### Approach B: Caught or Confronting Ulrich Directly
If the player fails a stealth check, gets spotted, or simply knocks on the front door:
* **The Fail-Forward Capture:** The guards level crossbows and order the player to the ground. **Do not run a lethal 4v1 combat.** The guards disarm the player and bring them straight to Lord Ulrich in his ground-floor parlor.
* **Ulrich's Reaction:** Ulrich is a sharp-featured noble in velvet robes, sipping wine. He is not frightened—he is completely exasperated:

## ACT 4: CONFRONTING BRONNER TRENT

The player returns to the Middle Ward to corner Bronner Trent at his militia post in the middleward (Trunkroad)

### The Reveal
When confronted with the matching warrants, Kenny’s testimony, or Ulrich’s alibi, Trent realizes his plot has failed, he's taken too many risk, told too many lies, and there is no way out other than escape.

He sets his mug down, his expression hardening into cold, vicious contempt:
> *"Merchants like Ulrich look down his nose at men like me from his stone mansion. Men like that and the poor folk whining about bread and coal while better men bled for this city... while my own men died during the war. 
> 
> Betty Fairweather screamed for three minutes before she went quiet. Taking a life is the only real thing left in this rotten part of the world. You should have died in Avir Manor like the rest of them.*

Trent grabs a heavy oil lantern from the wall, smashes it onto the timber floorboards to ignite a wall of flame, and bolts out the door into the alleys.

(I am still slightly unsure of having this in a police like station in the middle ward is the best place, maybe a more private setting would help bypass some loopholes and logic questions.)

### The Chase (Dynamic Solo Encounter)
To catch Trent before he vanishes into Dredge-End and engage him in a fight, the player must succeed on **2 out of 3 chase challenges**:

1. **The Flaming Exit:** Vaulting the burning doorway and dodging collapsing timber.
   * **DC 12 Dexterity (Acrobatics) or Athletics.** 
   * *Failure:* Take 1d6 fire damage and lose ground.
2. **The Crowded Lane (Lantern Lane):** Trent overturns barrels of brine and rope spools to block the lane.
   * **DC 11 Wisdom (Perception) or Dexterity (Acrobatics)** to spot the shortcut through Hollis's shop yard.
   * *Ranged Alternative:* A successful ranged weapon or spell attack (AC 14) clips Trent and slows him down automatically.
3. **The Canal Edge (The Drainage Cut):** Trent tries to jump across a 10-foot sluice gate into Dredge-End.
   * **DC 12 Strength (Athletics)** to tackle him before he leaps, or **DC 12 Charisma (Intimidation)** to draw the attention of nearby people who block his escape.
