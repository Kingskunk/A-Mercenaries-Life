Vane's office is smaller than the compound outside makes it look: a trestle desk crowded with ledgers and transit vouchers, one shuttered window letting in a blade of grey morning light, and the company's raven banner nailed flat to the stone behind him instead of hung loose from a pole. He doesn't look up until the door latches shut behind you.

"Sit if you want. This won't take long." He doesn't wait to see whether you do, closing a ledger under one hand.

"We marched out of the Black Sinks with four hundred banners and doubled that taking Alderford's contract. Half of what's wearing Carrion colors now has never held a line under me before, and every one of them still needs feeding, housing, and someone to answer for them when the Watch comes asking. That's not a problem shields fix."

He leans back, studying you the way he'd study a ledger column that didn't add up right.
