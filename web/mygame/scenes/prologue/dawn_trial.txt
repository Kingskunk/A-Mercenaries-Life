The third horn cracks through the freezing pre-dawn mist before you've truly slept, dragging you bolt upright out of your damp bedroll. Around you, groggy soldiers curse and scramble for their gear in the pitch dark.

"UP, YOU MAGGOTS! ON YOUR FEET!"

You stumble out into the freezing dark, falling into a ragged line of shivering soldiers beside the dying watch-fires. A grizzled veteran with a notched ear and a wooden muster board under one arm walks the line, matching each face to a name and each name to a mark in the book.

He stops in front of you, runs a charcoal-blackened thumb down the roll, and finds you.

"${name}," he reads. "${class_name}. Right. Quartermaster's cart, third from the end. Check your breach issue and report to your division."
