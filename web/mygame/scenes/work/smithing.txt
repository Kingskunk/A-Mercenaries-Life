*label smith_work_halda_inquiry
*if (smith_halda_accepted)
  Halda glances from the glowing bar in her tongs to you. "You know the terms. Come back when you are ready to put your back into them."
  *return

You wait until Halda has set her hammer down before you speak. She gives you the look of someone measuring a length of iron for flaws.

"I do not hire people to admire a forge," she says. "I hire them to move when told and stop before they make expensive noise. What are you after?"

*choice
  # "A start. I can carry coal and follow a mark."
    Halda studies your hands, then grunts. "That is a more useful answer than most."
    *goto smith_work_halda_terms
  # "I want to learn the trade, in time."
    "In time is right," Halda says. "First you learn to keep a clear floor and fetch the right tool without being asked twice. The trade comes after you prove you can do the small things."
    *goto smith_work_halda_terms
  # "What work would you put me to?"
    "Coal, water, scrap, deliveries, and whatever else keeps my hands on the iron," Halda says. "Nothing glorious. All of it necessary."
    *goto smith_work_halda_terms

*label smith_work_halda_terms
"You would start as a casual hand," Halda says. "You show up, take the work I give you, and do it cleanly. No promised lessons and no wandering off once the day has started. Keep that up long enough, and we can speak about hammer and heat."

She points the hammer at the yard door. "When I have work fit for an extra hand, ask me here."

*set smith_halda_accepted true
*page_break "Understood."
*return

*label smith_work_halda_result
*if (smith_work_task = "haul")
  *if (check_success)
    You learn the yard's rhythm quickly: coal before the fire wants it, finished work off the rack before it blocks the next order. By the end, Halda has stopped needing to point.
  *else
    The loads leave your shoulders aching, and you lose time twice finding the right rack. Halda corrects you with a short word and keeps the work moving.
*elseif (smith_work_task = "sort")
  *if (check_success)
    You read Halda's marks, separate usable pieces from the bad iron, and keep the tools she calls for close at hand. The work goes on without her having to break stride.
  *else
    Halda has to send you back through the scrap once and correct the tool in your hand. You remember both corrections before the next order comes.
*elseif (smith_work_task = "tend")
  *if (check_success)
    You keep the bellows steady, the water close, and the floor clear under a rain of scale. The heat never gets ahead of you.
  *else
    The heat wears you down before the work is done, and Halda calls you back twice before a tool goes where it belongs. You finish the shift with soot in every crease of your hands.
*elseif (smith_work_task = "steady")
  You carry, sweep, fetch, and hold what Halda puts before you. The work is plain, but the forge keeps moving because someone does it.
*elseif (smith_work_task = "forge_stock")
  *if (check_success)
    You move iron and finished work before the next pair of hands needs it. The yard stays clear enough that even the cart driver stops asking where his order has gone.
  *else
    You lose the thread of two orders and have to shift a stack twice before the yard runs cleanly again. Halda notices, says little, and puts you back on the right path.
*elseif (smith_work_task = "forge_finish")
  *if (check_success)
    Under Halda's eye, you take the burrs from a run of iron pieces and leave a thin coat of oil where it will keep rust away. She checks one, then lets you finish the rest.
  *else
    Halda catches a rough edge before it leaves the bench and sends it back to you. You work slower after that, feeling each pass of the file before you set the piece down.
*elseif (smith_work_task = "forge_orders")
  *if (check_success)
    You learn to read the marks at a glance and put each order where the work will reach it in time. Halda's hammer never has to stop while she waits for the next piece.
  *else
    Two marks look alike until Halda taps the difference with one blackened finger. You reset the pile and keep the corrected order in your head for the rest of the day.
*elseif (smith_work_task = "assistant_draw")
  *if (check_success)
    You bring each pin out evenly, checking the marks before the iron loses its give. Halda tests the first against its hinge plate and sets the rest aside without correction.
  *else
    The first few pins run heavy at the shoulder, and Halda makes you heat them again. You adjust your hammer work and finish the batch closer to the marks.
*elseif (smith_work_task = "assistant_true")
  *if (check_success)
    You work the hatchet edges until they lie straight from heel to point. Halda runs her thumb along one, then gives the rest a small nod.
  *else
    One edge wanders as you bring it true. Halda shows you where the metal has pulled, and you correct the rest with slower, smaller blows.
*elseif (smith_work_task = "assistant_heat")
  *if (check_success)
    You learn the heat by color and time, turning each bar before its skin burns. Halda's hammer meets iron that is ready every time she reaches for it.
  *else
    You bring one bar too cool and another too bright. Halda sends both back into the fire, then has you name the color you should have waited for.
*else
  You keep the routine work moving: sweepings out, water filled, coal close, and no finished piece left where a boot can find it.

*if (((not(smith_work_task = "steady")) and (not(smith_work_task = "forge_steady"))) and (not(smith_work_task = "assistant_steady")))
  *if (check_success)
    *set smith_work_progress_earned 1
    *if (d20_roll = 20)
      *set smith_work_progress_earned 2
