Rain beats steadily against the black canvas of the encampment, turning the red clay of the river valley into a miserable sea of mud.

Smoke from damp pine fires stings your eyes. All around you, the Iron Carrion—four hundred hardened sellswords, deserters, and scoundrels—prepare for tomorrow's march into the contested borderlands. The sound of whetstones scraping against iron fills the air, rhythmic and cold as a death knell.

Before the horns sound tomorrow, you are just fresh meat in a company that buries three rookies for every one that survives the winter in the Grey Marches.

You sit on an upturned ale cask beneath a sagging leather awning, picking dried clay from your boot heels, when squelching footsteps approach through the muck.

A rat-faced company clerk in a moth-eaten wool mantle ducks under your awning. He clutches a tallow candle inside a pierced-tin lantern, shielding a cracked leather ledger from the drizzle. At the top of the damp vellum page, the date is scratched in dark iron-gall ink: [b]14th of Scorpio, 428 IR[/b]. He dips an iron stylus into a horn of lampblack gall-ink and glares at you with watery, impatient eyes.

    *set combat_cast_result 1
  *set hp_current + combat_cast_result
  *if (hp_current > hp_max)
    *set hp_current hp_max
  *set combat_cast_locked true
  *set locked_combat_cast_page_id choice_page_id
*return

*label resolve_dissonant_whispers
*comment Call after the caller's own roll_d20_check against a target DC --
*comment check_success standing in for "the target failed its save" (full
*comment damage); on a failure the target "saved" for half, rounded down.
*comment Spends one bard_spell_slots regardless of outcome (RAW: the slot is
*comment spent on casting, not on the save result). Deals 3d6 psychic,
*comment applied straight to combat_enemy_hp. Reports combat_player_dmg_dealt.
*if (not(combat_dissonant_whispers_locked)) or (not(locked_combat_dissonant_whispers_page_id = choice_page_id))
  *set bard_spell_slots - 1
  *temp dw_d1 0
  *temp dw_d2 0
  *temp dw_d3 0
  *rand dw_d1 1 6
  *rand dw_d2 1 6
  *rand dw_d3 1 6
  *temp dw_total ((dw_d1 + dw_d2) + dw_d3)
  *if (check_success)
    *set combat_player_dmg_dealt dw_total
  *else
    *set combat_player_dmg_dealt ((dw_total - (dw_total modulo 2)) / 2)

    *set combat_enemy_hp 0
  *set combat_dissonant_whispers_locked true
  *set locked_combat_dissonant_whispers_page_id choice_page_id
*return

*comment ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
*comment REUSABLE ENGINE PRIMITIVE: CURRENCY MATH
*comment Pure arithmetic, no locking of its own -- callers gosub this from
*comment inside their own replay-safety guard (see resolve_wager and
*comment buy_tavern_item below for the pattern), the same way every other
*comment *set stat +N mutation in this game needs one.
*comment ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
*label currency_add
*comment Input: currency_add_amount, a signed integer in copper-equivalent
*comment units (10 copper = 1 silver, 10 silver = 1 gold). Adds (or, if
*comment negative, spends) that amount against gold/silver/copper, carrying
*comment and borrowing across denominations so the three stats stay sane.
*temp ca_amount currency_add_amount
*temp ca_sign 1
*if (ca_amount < 0)
  *comment A bare "*set ca_sign -1" is ChoiceScript's decrement shorthand
  *comment (ca_sign - 1), not a literal assignment -- it would leave
  *comment ca_sign at 0, not -1. "0 - 1" sidesteps the shorthand the same
  *comment way ca_amount's own negation two lines below already does.
  *set ca_sign 0 - 1
  *set ca_amount 0 - ca_amount
*temp ca_gold (ca_amount - (ca_amount modulo 100)) / 100
*set ca_amount ca_amount - (ca_gold * 100)
*temp ca_silver (ca_amount - (ca_amount modulo 10)) / 10
*set ca_amount ca_amount - (ca_silver * 10)
*temp ca_copper ca_amount
*if (ca_sign > 0)
  *set gold + ca_gold
  *set silver + ca_silver
  *set copper + ca_copper
  *if (copper > 9)
    *set copper - 10
    *set silver + 1
  *if (silver > 9)
    *set silver - 10
    *set gold + 1
*else
  *set copper - ca_copper
  *if (copper < 0)
    *set copper + 10
    *set silver - 1
  *set silver - ca_silver
  *if (silver < 0)
    *set silver + 10
    *set gold - 1
  *set gold - ca_gold
*return

*comment ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
*comment REUSABLE ENGINE PRIMITIVE: GAMBLING WAGER
*comment One shared subroutine for every gambling table in the game (dice,
*comment cards, knuckle-bones, whatever a future town needs) instead of
*comment hand-rolling a new ante/payout/lock trio per location. A caller
*comment just needs its own *create'd house-bankroll stat (so the NPC can
*comment eventually run dry and stop offering the game) plus these four
*comment inputs, set right before *gosub_scene startup resolve_wager:
*comment   wager_bankroll_var  - string name of that table's bankroll stat
*comment   wager_ante_copper   - cost to play, in copper-equivalent units
*comment   wager_win_copper    - gross payout on success (capped to
*comment                         whatever's left in the bankroll)
*comment   check_stat/check_dc/check_skill - as usual for roll_d20_check
*comment Outputs: check_success (as always) and wager_bankroll_busted (true
*comment once the bankroll hits 0, so the caller can hide the option and/or
*comment print a "the house is tapped out" line on the next visit).
*comment The ante debit and the win payout are two separate mutations that
*comment can both fire on the same page (ante, then the check, then a
*comment conditional credit) -- exactly the "two mutations, one breath"
*comment problem *rand and *set stat +N run into everywhere else in this
*comment game, so each half gets its own dedicated lock rather than sharing
*comment one, the same way damage_roll_locked is kept separate from
*comment roll_locked.
*comment ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
*label resolve_wager
*if (wager_bankroll_var = "")
  *set wager_bankroll_var "taphouse_dice_bankroll"
*if (not(wager_ante_locked)) or (not(locked_wager_ante_page_id = choice_page_id))
  *set currency_add_amount 0 - wager_ante_copper
  *gosub currency_add
  *set {wager_bankroll_var} + wager_ante_copper
  *set wager_ante_locked true
  *set locked_wager_ante_page_id choice_page_id
*gosub roll_d20_check
*set wager_bankroll_busted ({wager_bankroll_var} <= 0)
*if (check_success)
  *if (not(wager_win_locked)) or (not(locked_wager_win_page_id = choice_page_id))
    *temp wager_payout wager_win_copper
    *if (wager_payout > {wager_bankroll_var})
      *set wager_payout {wager_bankroll_var}
    *set currency_add_amount wager_payout
    *gosub currency_add
    *set {wager_bankroll_var} - wager_payout
    *set wager_win_locked true
    *set locked_wager_win_page_id choice_page_id
  *set wager_bankroll_busted ({wager_bankroll_var} <= 0)
*return

*comment ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
