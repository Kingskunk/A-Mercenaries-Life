*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Permanent room geography and smell.
*label pc_gb_place
*if (gb_first)
  The Green Branch is one long room under a low beamed ceiling, with thick pale walls and a floor of worn red tiles. Plank tables and benches run down the middle, rubbed smooth and pale by years of elbows. A wide hearth takes up the far wall, and a clay oven with an iron door is built into its side. Along the near wall, a long counter holds a stack of bowls, a row of lidded jars, a bowl of dark olives and a tall pitcher of water. A ladder in the corner climbs to a loft closed in with hanging curtains. The room smells of warm bread, olive oil and wood smoke.
*else
