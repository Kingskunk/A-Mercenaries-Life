*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Fixed geography only.
*label pc_mk_place
*if (mk_first)
  The market lines a widening of Palmcote's packed-earth lane between low drystone terrace walls and pale stone houses under red clay tile roofs, with grey-green olive trees rising up the slope behind. Rough timber stalls roofed with weathered canvas awnings face each other across the dirt. On the trestles sit sacks of grain, crates of carrots and cabbages, loaves of bread, bundles of split firewood, folded bolts of coarse cloth and a counter scale, beside wicker baskets of green and black olives and lidded clay jars of honey. The air smells of warm dust, baked crust, split wood and crushed leaves.
*else
  The market lines the widening of the lane between the drystone walls and the stone houses: canvas-awning stalls of grain, vegetables, bread, firewood, cloth, olives and honey, with the olive terraces rising behind.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, then ground state.
*label pc_mk_sky
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain patters on the stretched canvas awnings and runs in thin brown threads down the sloping lane.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Grey mist settles across the olive terraces and blurs the upper cottages into pale shapes.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow slides off the canvas awnings and collects in the stones along the terrace walls.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (time_period = "Dusk")
    Low light slants across the hillside, turning the stone walls and canvas awnings warm gold.
  *else
    Bright sun pours down the slope and picks out every pale stone in the terrace walls.
*else
  Flat grey light sits over the canvas awnings and the grey-green trees behind them.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The packed earth between the stalls is dark and churned soft, with water pooled between the stones.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Damp patches darken the earth between the trestles from the last rain.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 3: STREET ── Current market activity.
*label pc_mk_street
*if (time_period = "Dusk")
  Sellers pack their remaining loaves and jars into crates and lash the canvas edges down while lamps come on in the stone houses.
  *return
*if (day_of_week = "Marketday")
  The lane is busy. Villagers and road carters crowd between the awnings, testing the weight of grain sacks, counting out bread and haggling over baskets of olives.
  *return
*if (time_period = "Morning")
  Stallholders are still setting out crates of carrots and cabbages beside the grain sacks and checking the counter scale before the sun climbs.
  *return
*if (mk_vignette = 1)
  A seller in a dusty apron weighs a basket of black olives on the counter scale and chalks the tally on a board.
*elseif (mk_vignette = 2)
  A woman stacks split firewood beside a trestle and trades two loaves of bread for a bundle of greens.
*elseif (mk_vignette = 3)
  A carter ties a lidded clay honey jar into a wicker hamper while a stallholder counts out copper bits.
*else
  A villager runs a thumb along a folded bolt of coarse cloth, shakes her head once, and moves on to the vegetable crates.
*return
