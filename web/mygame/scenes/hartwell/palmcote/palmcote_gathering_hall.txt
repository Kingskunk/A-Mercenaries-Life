*comment ── TALKING WITH NORA GREER ── Roleplay pass: steady, diplomatic, never names House Ferrand.
*label pc_gh_talk
*set pc_gh_asked_village true
*set minutes_to_pass 5
*set time_advance_call_id "pc_gh_talk_village"
*gosub_scene calendar advance_time
You step up to the trestle table. "${name}," you say. "What can you tell me about Palmcote?"

Nora rests her palms flat on the closed register and gives a slight, polite nod. "${name}, then. It's a quiet place, mostly. We live by the terraces: olives, mostly, pressed down at the yard when the harvest comes in, though folks keep a few goats and kitchen plots along the lower wall. Odda at the Green Branch keeps a warm kitchen and a few beds if you're staying the night, and the market at the foot of the lane has oil, salt and whatever the carters bring up from Hartwell."

She glances toward the slatted rack on the back wall. "If you're looking for honest coin, check the board. Hartwell's clerk sends down road and escort notices when they're short-handed, and villagers pin their own errands up beside them. As long as you mind the terrace walls and pay your way, folks here will treat you fairly."

*label pc_gh_talk_house
*set pc_gh_asked_house true
*set minutes_to_pass 5
*set time_advance_call_id "pc_gh_talk_house"
*gosub_scene calendar advance_time
"I couldn't help but notice the house on the hill coming into the village," you say. "Who owns it?"

Nora's expression doesn't change, though her fingers settle quietly over the corner of the village register, smoothing the page flat. "An old family from the Viscounty. They've held the upper ridge and the big groves since before my father's time. Most folk here just call it the big house, or the house on the hill."

"We have an arrangement that works well enough," she goes on evenly. "Their estate steward handles the pressing-yard leases and sends down the carts for the harvest; I handle the village register, the lower terrace walls, and whatever Hartwell sends up the road. They don't trouble the village, and the village doesn't trouble their gate."

She tilts her head slightly, watching you with calm politeness. "They hire through the yard when the harvest is heavy, if that's why you're asking, though for anything up by the wall itself, you'd have to speak with their estate steward, not me."
*goto pc_gh_front
