*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Fixed geography only. The long description prints once. Nothing here changes with the season or the hour.
*label pc_place
*if (pc_first_hub)
  Palmcote climbs a south-facing hillside on a single steep lane, with the houses stepping up one above another. Low stone walls hold the earth in terraces around it, and every terrace is planted with olive trees, their trunks thick and knotted and their leaves grey-green on top and pale underneath. Houses of pale stone sit close to the lane under roofs of red clay tile, each with a bench by the door and a wall of drying baskets.

  Near the foot of the lane, a long building with a wide porch has a notice board nailed up beside its door. A low inn sits just above it with a green branch hung over the doorway. Where the lane bends, a round stone well stands under a small tiled roof, with a rope and wooden bucket hanging from its beam. Past them, a flat yard of beaten earth holds stacked baskets, a row of wooden carts and a small stall with jars on it. At the top of the hill, behind a high wall and a line of dark trees, stands a large house of grey stone with a great many windows. The air smells of dust, warm leaves and crushed fruit.
*else
  Palmcote climbs its single steep lane up the hillside through the olive terraces, with the long hall and its notice board near the foot, the inn with its green branch just above, the roofed well at the bend, the basket-stacked yard beyond, and the great grey house high behind its wall.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, followed by the ground's recent-weather state. No people here.
*label pc_sky
*temp pc_dark false
*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *set pc_dark true
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain patters on the clay tiles and the leaves, and runs down the lane in a thin brown stream between the terrace walls.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  Wind rips up the hillside and flips the pale undersides of the leaves in long ripples. Rain hammers the tiles, and the lane runs like a ditch.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Low cloud sits on the slope, and the trees a few rows up fade into grey. The lane vanishes into white a short way above you.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow clings to the terrace walls and weighs down the olive branches, and the clay roofs show white against the dark trunks.
*elseif (weather = "Blizzard")
  Blowing snow fills the lane and buries the lower terraces. The upper hill is lost behind a wall of white.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (pc_dark)
    Stars crowd the sky over the hill, and the pale walls show clearly along the lane.
  *elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
    The low sun lights the slope orange, and the long shadows of the tree rows stretch across the terraces.
  *else
    Strong light fills the hillside, hot on the stone and sharp on the silver-green leaves.
*else
  *if (pc_dark)
    A thick, dark overcast hides the stars, and the lamps in the windows are the only light on the slope.
  *else
    Flat grey cloud sits over the hill and turns the leaves a dull green.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Water still runs down the lane in the gaps between the stones, and the terrace soil is heavy and dark.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The lane is damp and dark from recent rain, with a few puddles in its low spots.
*return
