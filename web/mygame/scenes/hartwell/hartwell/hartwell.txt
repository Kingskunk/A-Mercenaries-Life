*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Fixed geography only. The long description prints once. Nothing here changes with the season or the hour.
*label ht_place
*if (ht_first_hub)
  Hartwell packs tightly behind its modest limestone curtain wall on a gentle rise above the surrounding Vale fields. Just inside the broad cart gate, low arched stone press-houses sit stacked with banded oil casks and wicker olive baskets, sending a warm, earthy scent of crushed olives and lamp oil drifting down the cobbled street.

  Timber-and-stone storehouses, tiled-roof merchant houses, and iron bracket lamps crowd both sides of the climbing lane around a carved stone water conduit at the town's center. Above the clustered gables on the upper crest rises Hartwell Keep, a compact, square-towered stone castle flying House Falwick's banner above the roofs. The air smells of pressed olive oil, horse harness, baking bread, and woodsmoke.
*else
  Hartwell climbs behind its modest stone wall from the arched olive press-houses near the cart gate past crowded timber-and-stone storehouses and the central water conduit toward Hartwell Keep on the upper crest.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, followed by the ground's recent-weather state. No people here.
*label ht_sky
*temp ht_night_sky false
*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *set ht_night_sky true
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Steady rain slicks the grey cobbles, streams from the tiled eaves, and darkens the limestone battlements of Hartwell Keep above the street.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  Driving rain and hard wind sweep across the rooftops, rattling iron street-lamp brackets and forcing empty handcarts tight under the press-house arches.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Thick Vale mist settles inside the town wall, softening the clustered roofs and hiding the upper tower of Hartwell Keep in pale cloud.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow coats the stone battlements, the tiled gables, and the rim of the central water conduit, turning the cobbles slick underfoot.
*elseif (weather = "Blizzard")
  Driven snow pours across the curtain wall and swirls blind between the storehouse fronts, blotting out the keep tower overhead.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (ht_night_sky)
    Clear starlight sits over the keep tower, while warm iron street lamps lay bright amber pools along the cobbled rise.
  *elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
    The setting sun lays long copper bars across the tiled roofs and limestone wall of Hartwell Keep while bracket lamps flicker to life below.
  *else
    Bright open daylight warms the pale limestone wall, the stacked oil casks by the gate, and the banner snapping cleanly atop Hartwell Keep.
*else
  *if (ht_night_sky)
    Low cloud hides the stars above the keep, leaving the bracket lamps along the cobbled street to cast a steady warm glow between the storehouses.
  *else
    Flat grey cloud sits over the Vale, muting the pale stonework and keeping the cobbled lane in even daylight.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Rainwater lingers between the uneven cobbles and runs in thin trickles along the gutter stones.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The cobbles remain damp from recent rain, catching pale reflections beneath the eaves.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 3: STREET ── Current town activity. Daytime work follows the season; the rotating vignette follows the clock.
*label ht_street
*temp ht_vignette (((campaign_day * 24) + clock_hour) modulo 6) + 1
*temp ht_is_night false
*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *set ht_is_night true
*if ((street_life = "empty") and (not(ht_is_night)))
  The cobbled street stands nearly deserted. Shop shutters are barred, wagons sit wedged beneath the press-house eaves, and only two watchmen huddle inside the shelter of the gate arch.
  *return
*if (ht_is_night)
  *if (ht_vignette = 1)
    Two watchmen in cloaks walk unhurriedly up the cobbled rise, a shuttered lantern swinging between them as they check storehouse bolts.
  *elseif (ht_vignette = 2)
    The low creak of a late-arriving wagon echoes under the cart gate, followed by the soft clink of harness chains settling for the night.
  *elseif (ht_vignette = 3)
    Warm light and muted laughter spill briefly from an upper window before a wooden shutter pulls neatly shut.
  *elseif (ht_vignette = 4)
    A liveried rider in House Falwick's colors walks a tired gelding slowly up the lane toward the keep gate.
  *elseif (ht_vignette = 5)
    The lingering, warm smell of pressed olives drifts down from the banked press-houses beside the quiet water conduit.
  *else
    Footsteps ring crisp across the cobbles and fade into a side alley, leaving only the steady drip of the central conduit.
  *return
*if (time_period = "Dusk")
  Press-hands wipe oil from their forearms as they lock the arched timber doors near the gate, carters unhitch tired teams for the evening, and iron lamps brighten along the climbing street.
  *return
*if (day_of_week = "Hallowday")
  The olive presses stand silent behind bolted arches and the storehouse doors remain shut for the rest day. Townsfolk in brushed wool stroll unhurriedly past the central conduit, stopping in small knots to trade news from the surrounding villages.
  *return
*if (day_of_week = "Marketday")
  The street is packed from gate to square. Olive carts from Palmcote, hide wagons from Oxworth, and grain-and-loaf drays from Millstow edge past each other while clerks with ink-stained cuffs shout weigh-bill counts from the storehouse steps.
  *return
*if (current_season = "Spring")
  Fresh whitewash brightens the merchant facades along the lane, and wagons roll steadily out the gate carrying lamp oil and barreled provisions toward the northern roads.
*elseif (current_season = "Summer")
  Storehouse doors stay propped wide to air the stone cellars, and townsfolk pause by the central conduit to splash cool water over their wrists in the warm Vale air.
*elseif (current_season = "Autumn")
  Wicker baskets heaped with harvested Palmcote olives crowd every yard beside the gate, and the heavy beam presses groan steadily from dawn onward.
*else
  Crisp woodsmoke hangs above the tiled roofs, wool-cloaked carters stamp warmth into their heels beside the gate braziers, and oil casks stack high inside the stone storehouses.
*if (ht_vignette = 1)
  Two broad-shouldered press-hands roll a newly bunged cask of golden olive oil out of an arched doorway onto a waiting handcart.
*elseif (ht_vignette = 2)
  A drover with dust on his shoulders leads a string of pack mules past the water conduit, heading down toward the gate.
*elseif (ht_vignette = 3)
  A clerk in a neat dark coat stops beside a grain wagon to inspect a wax-sealed waybill before waving the driver up the street.
*elseif (ht_vignette = 4)
  Two guards in House Falwick's livery descend from the keep road, trading a brief nod with the watchmen at the gate arch.
*elseif (ht_vignette = 5)
  A baker's boy weaves quickly between two parked drays carrying a wicker tray of warm Millstow barley loaves.
*else
  A cooper hammers a fresh iron hoop tight around an oil barrel beside a storehouse door, the sharp rings carrying cleanly up the lane.
