*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Fixed geography only.
*label ht_mk_place
*if (mk_first)
  Hartwell Market spreads across a broad cobbled square just off the main street, hemmed by timber-and-limestone storehouses and the tiled roofs of the merchant quarter beneath the distant tower of Hartwell Keep. Rows of sturdy timber bays roofed in canvas awnings hold banded casks of pressed olive oil, tied grain sacks from Millstow, rolled Oxworth hides, waxed cheese rounds, folded wool bolts, earthenware jugs, and everyday ironware. Beside the central weighing post hangs a heavy iron beam-scale stamped with House Falwick's inspection mark. The air carries the rich scent of olive oil, warm barley bread, tanned leather, and dry straw.
*else
  Hartwell Market fills the cobbled square beneath its canvas-awning bays, with banded oil casks, grain sacks, wool bolts, and the House Falwick beam-scale set out between the storehouse fronts.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, then ground state.
*label ht_mk_sky
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain drums across the taut canvas awnings and runs in slick streams between the cobbles around the weighing post.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Pale Vale mist hangs over the rooftops, softening the storehouse gables and clinging damp around the awning poles.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow slides off the sloped canvas and gathers along the stone curbs while the cobbles turn slick underfoot.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (time_period = "Dusk")
    Low copper sunlight slants across the limestone facades and lays long shadows beneath the timber trestles.
  *else
    Clean daylight warms the cobbled square, glinting off glazed oil jugs and the bright brass weights beside the scale.
*else
  Overcast cloud sits even over the roofs, leaving the canvas bays muted cream against the pale stonework.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Rainwater pools in the hollows between the cobbles and darkens the bases of the timber posts.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Damp patches linger across the paving stones around the trestle legs from the last rain.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 3: STREET ── Current market activity.
*label ht_mk_street
*if (time_period = "Dusk")
  Sellers lash down canvas corners, roll bunged oil casks back under the stone arcades, and count their day's coin as the bracket lamps brighten around the square.
  *return
*if (day_of_week = "Marketday")
  The square is packed shoulder to shoulder. Carters off the Dunmow and Langston roads bargain briskly beside village growers from Palmcote, Oxworth, and Millstow while market clerks check weigh-bills by the central scale.
  *return
*if (time_period = "Morning")
  Porters heave fresh casks of pressed oil and sacks of flour onto the trestles while stallholders balance the brass weights on the central beam-scale.
  *return
*if (mk_vignette = 1)
  An oil merchant taps the wooden bung of a fresh cask with a mallet and lets a buyer sample a drop on a crust of barley bread.
*elseif (mk_vignette = 2)
  A drover from Oxworth haggles sharply with a town tanner over a bundled stack of salted raw hides beside the weighing post.
*elseif (mk_vignette = 3)
  A clerk in a dark wool coat checks the wax seal on a crate of Palmcote olives before waving a porter toward the press-house lane.
*else
  Two townswomen compare bolts of undyed wool under the nearest awning while a baker's boy threads past with a tray of warm loaves.
*return
