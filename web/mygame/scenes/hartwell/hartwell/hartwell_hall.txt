*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Fixed geography only.
*label ht_hall_place
*if (hl_first)
  Hartwell Hall opens into a high-raftered timber-and-stone public chamber floored in worn grey flags laid with a long patterned runner down the center. On the left wall, beneath House Gault's deep-blue banners bearing the golden horse, hangs a broad timber Contract Board ruled neatly into columns above low waiting benches. Along the right wall stretches a long oak clerks' counter backed by pigeonholed shelves stacked with tied parchment rolls, brass weights, wax seals, and open ledgers beneath tall diamond-leaded windows. The room smells of gall ink, sealing wax, dry parchment, and lamp oil.
*else
  Hartwell Hall stretches across its flagged chamber between the ruled Contract Board beneath House Gault's banners on the left and the long oak clerks' counter backed by parchment shelves on the right.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── One muffled indoor weather line plus damp threshold memory.
*label ht_hall_sky
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain patters lightly against the tall leaded windows on the right and dims the light across the oak counters.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Pale Vale mist presses against the high glass, leaving the wall lanterns beside the Contract Board burning warm.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Cold grey light through the tall casements catches a thin wet smear just inside the street doors.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  Bright daylight slants through the tall right-hand windows and lays crisp pale squares across the stone floor and benches.
*else
  Even overcast daylight fills the high windows, keeping the parchment shelves and clerks' desks evenly lit.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Damp footprints lead from the oak doors across the runner toward the nearest clerk's counter.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  A few wet tracks mark the threshold stones inside the doorway.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 3: STREET ── Steward Harland Kemp introduces himself once on first visit.
*label ht_hall_street
*if (hl_first)
  *set met_ht_steward true
  Behind the central oak counter stands a lean, brisk human man in his early fifties with silver-streaked dark hair combed flat, observant pale-blue eyes, and an ink-stained right thumb. He wears a neatly cut slate-blue wool coat fastened with House Falwick's brass clasp, while two junior clerks sort tied parchment rolls beside him.

  He glances up from a wax-sealed weigh-bill and gives a crisp, professional nod. "Harland Kemp, Town Steward for House Falwick. If you are looking for road postings or courier dispatches, the Contract Board hangs on the left wall. If you have Viscounty business, step up to the counter."
  *return
*elseif (hl_vignette = 1)
  Steward Harland Kemp checks an oil-press weigh-roll with a junior clerk, marking clean figures in red ink down the margin.
*elseif (hl_vignette = 2)
  A merchant factor waits at the oak counter while Steward Harland Kemp presses House Falwick's brass seal into warm wax on a road charter.
*elseif (hl_vignette = 3)
  Steward Harland Kemp walks over to the Contract Board, unpins an outdated courier slip, and returns briskly behind the counter.
*else
  Steward Harland Kemp reads through a sealed village dispatch by the light of the tall windows, tapping his quill lightly against the blotter.
*return
