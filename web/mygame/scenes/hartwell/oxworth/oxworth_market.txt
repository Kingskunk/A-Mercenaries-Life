*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Fixed geography only.
*label ox_market_place
*if (mk_first)
  The market fills a trampled yard right beside Oxworth's main split-rail cattle pens. In the middle, a heavy iron beam-scale hangs from timber uprights on a plank platform, with scarred butcher's blocks and trestle tables ranged around it under stretched canvas awnings. The tables hold joints of beef wrapped in waxed paper, rounds of yellow cheese, butter crocks, baskets of eggs, tubs of rendered fat and folded raw hides bound with cord. Stacked salt-casks stand against the outer hurdles, and the brick rendering flues of the salting shed rise just behind the stalls. It smells of raw meat, brine, tallow and warm cattle.
*else
  The market stands beside the cattle pens as before: the iron beam-scale on its platform, the butcher's blocks and trestles under canvas awnings, the salt-casks by the hurdles, and the salting shed's flues behind.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, then ground state.
*label ox_market_sky
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain drums on the stretched canvas awnings and runs in dark rivulets between the plank platform and the pens.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Mist rolls off the open down and blurs the far hurdles and salt-casks into grey shapes.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow slides off the canvas awnings and turns to grey slush around the blocks and trestles.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (time_period = "Dusk")
    Low light slants across the pens, turning the canvas awnings and stacked salt-casks gold.
  *else
    Bright open-country light pours down across the yard and glints on the iron beam of the scale.
*else
  Flat grey light sits over the awnings and the dark hurdles behind them.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The packed earth between the trestles is churned to mud, and water stands in every hoofprint.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The yard is dark and slick between the stalls from the last rain.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 3: STREET ── Current market activity.
*label ox_market_street
*if (time_period = "Dusk")
  Sellers wash down the butcher's blocks and lash canvas over the casks, while the scale-keeper locks the iron weights into a stout wooden chest.
  *return
*if (day_of_week = "Marketday")
  The yard is packed. Carters from the Hartwell road back wagons up to the hurdles, buyers test the heft of hides and beef quarters, and the beam-scale creaks without pause.
  *return
*if (time_period = "Morning")
  Only half the stalls are laid out yet. A thick-shouldered man in a salt-stained canvas apron unlocks the weight chest at the scale platform while sellers prop up their awnings.
  *return
*if (mk_vignette = 1)
  The scale-keeper in his salt-stained apron hooks a folded hide onto the beam-scale, waits for the bar to settle, and chalks the weight on a tally board.
*elseif (mk_vignette = 2)
  A buyer haggles over a tub of rendered fat, walks off toward the pens, and comes back two paces later.
*elseif (mk_vignette = 3)
  A farmwife sets baskets of eggs and wrapped butter pats along a trestle edge and counts coppers into her apron pocket.
*elseif (mk_vignette = 4)
  A butcher wipes his cleaver on a coarse cloth and wraps a dark red joint of beef in waxed paper.
*elseif (mk_vignette = 5)

*else
  A yard dog trots under the trestles, noses at a dropped scrap, and ducks clear of a seller's boot.
