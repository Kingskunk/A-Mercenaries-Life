*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Fixed geography only. The long description prints once. Nothing here changes with the season or the hour.
*label ox_place
*if (ox_first_hub)
  Oxworth sits squat on the open down, built around a wide, rutted cattle lane rather than a tidy green. Heavy split-rail cattle pens flank both approaches to the village, their turf worn down to packed earth and straw, with broad timber gates and stone troughs along the fence lines. Low cottages of rough fieldstone and dark timber huddle under deep thatch against the downs wind, each with a stout chimney and a small walled yard.

  At the head of the lane stands a timber-and-turf hall with a mud-scraped porch and a wide notice board nailed beneath its eaves. Across the trampled yard beside the main pens, open trestles, butcher's blocks and an iron beam-scale sit under canvas awnings, while a long salting shed with brick rendering flues and stacked salt-casks backs onto the outer hurdles. Near the crossroads, a low stone inn hangs a carved wooden yoke and horn from an iron bracket above its door. Wood smoke, damp hide, brine and warm cattle hang heavy in the air.
*else
  Oxworth spreads along its wide cattle lane between the split-rail cattle pens, with the village head's hall at the head of the road, the market trestles and scale beside the main hurdles, the salting shed with its brick flues beyond, and the inn's yoke-and-horn sign near the crossroads.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, followed by the ground's recent-weather state.
*label ox_sky
*temp ox_dark false
*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *set ox_dark true
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain darkens the split-rail pens and drips steadily off the low thatch, turning the trampled cattle lane into slick mud.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  Wind sweeps unimpeded off the open down, driving rain sideways across the pens. Canvas awnings snap over the market blocks, shutters bang, and cattle crowd to the leeward hurdles.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Cold mist rolls off the downs and settles into the pens, hiding the far pastures and leaving only the nearest roofs and hurdle posts in view.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wind-driven snow clings to the windward side of the pens and coats the thatch, while the open down beyond fades to pale grey.
*elseif (weather = "Blizzard")
  Blowing snow swallows the outer pens and the downs road alike. Even the village head's hall at the head of the lane is half lost in white.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (ox_dark)
    Clear starlight lies wide over the open down, throwing sharp shadows from the hurdle posts across the packed earth.
  *elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
    Low light stretches long shadows from the cattle pens across the lane and turns the smoke over the salting shed to pale gold.
  *else
    Bright, clean light pours over the open down, showing every roof and pen for half a mile around.
*else
  *if (ox_dark)
    A heavy, starless sky presses down on the downs, with only the inn's lit windows and a lantern by the pens breaking the dark.
  *else
    Flat grey light sits over the down and dulls the rough stone of the cottages.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Water stands in the hoof-pocked ruts of the cattle lane, and the turf inside the pens is churned to deep mire.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The cattle lane is dark and greasy from recent rain, with shallow puddles along the fence lines.
*return
