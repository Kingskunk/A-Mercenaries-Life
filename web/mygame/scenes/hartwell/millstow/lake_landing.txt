*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Matches Wallpaper/Millstow_Lake_Landing_Day.jpg.
*label ml_landing_place
*if (ld_first)
  A gravel track drops down from the village lane to Azure Lake's southeastern bank. To the left, thick reed beds border a broad expanse of open water reaching toward low wooded hills across the bay, and a weathered landing stage on stout pilings juts out into the shallows with two flat-bottomed skiffs tied to posts by hemp lines.

  To the right sits a tarred-timber boatwright's shed under a pitched shingle roof, its wide arched bay open to the breeze. Outside the doorway, a clinker-built boat rests keel-up on sturdy trestles beside sawhorses stacked with seasoned oak planks, a neat coil of rope on the gravel, and banded pitch barrels. The air smells of pine resin, fresh oak shavings, wet hemp, and cold water.
*else
  The gravel track opens beside Azure Lake's reedy bank: the weathered landing stage and its two tethered craft to the left, and the tarred boat-shed with its keel-up hull on trestles, plank sawhorses, coiled rope, and pitch kegs to the right.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ──
*label ml_landing_sky
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain dimples the broad water past the pilings, patters steadily across the overturned hull, and runs off the shed's shingle eaves.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Pale mist hangs low over the reeds and hides the far wooded hills, leaving only the moored boats and nearest posts clear.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow coats the landing planks, the overturned hull, and the stacked oak lumber while the lake lies leaden beyond the rushes.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (time_period = "Dusk")
    Low copper light spreads across Azure Lake and throws long shadows from the pilings and boat trestles across the gravel.
  *else
    Bright daylight warms the weathered landing planks and glints off the ripples moving past the tethered craft.
*else
  *if (time_period = "Dusk")
    Dusk settles cool and grey over the water, deepening the shadows inside the open work bay.
  *else
    Overcast daylight spreads evenly over the slate-blue expanse, the reed beds, and the tarred siding of the shed.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Shallow rainwater sits in the grass verge beside the reeds, and the packed ground feels sodden underfoot.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Dampness lingers on the pilings and turns the gravel around the sawhorses a richer brown.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 3: STREET ── Orik introduces himself on first contact.
*label ml_landing_street
*if (ld_first)
  *set met_orik true
  A broad-shouldered half-orc man in his mid-forties stands beside the upturned skiff, running a thumb along a freshly caulked seam. He has weather-beaten olive-grey skin, blunted lower tusks, short black hair silvering at the temples, and steady brown eyes. He wears a sleeveless oiled-leather vest over a coarse grey linen shirt with rolled sleeves, his forearms streaked with pine pitch and a worn canvas tool-roll belted at his hip.

  He glances up from the hull and gives a short, level nod. "Orik. Boatwright here. Watch where you step around the oak stack, and keep clear of the pitch kegs if you don't want tar on your heels. You need something?"
  *return
*elseif (ld_vignette = 1)
  Orik stands beside the upturned skiff with a wooden mallet and caulking iron, driving oakum cleanly into a lower seam.
*elseif (ld_vignette = 2)
  Orik crouches by the sawhorses, sighting down the edge of a seasoned oak plank with one eye shut before marking it with charcoal.
*elseif (ld_vignette = 3)
  Orik walks out along the wooden jetty to check the hemp mooring lines on both skiffs, testing each knot with a firm tug before striding back to the shed.
*else
  Orik leans over a pitch barrel beside the open bay, scraping hardened resin off a broad iron ladle before setting it neatly on his workbench.
*return

      "How long have you been building boats here?" you ask.

      "Twelve years," Orik says, resting one calloused hand on the upturned planking. "Spent ten before that in the Port Valen shipyards, hanging ribs on salt-water merchant hulls. Too many foremen, too much shouting, and brine eating every nail you drive."

      He taps the flat bottom of the skiff. "Out here it's honest oak and pine pitch. I build and patch skiffs for Millstow, Oatcroft, and Reedmere, flat-bottomed so a fisherman can nose into the reeds without splitting a keel. Good wood, tight seams, no fuss."

      *goto ml_landing_talk_menu
  *if (not(ml_ld_asked_lake))
    # "What should a traveler know about Azure Lake?"
      *set ml_ld_asked_lake true
      *set minutes_to_pass 5
      *set time_advance_call_id "ml_ld_talk_lake"
      *gosub_scene calendar advance_time
      "What should a traveler know about Azure Lake?" you ask.

      Orik squints out across the water toward the distant wooded shore. "Looks tame enough in the morning, but afternoon winds swing hard out of the northwest and chop up the middle fast. Shallow reed beds choke both the north and south banks, too. Drag a loaded hull in there and you'll be wading up to your knees to push free."

      "If you ever cross by skiff and see cloud stacking behind the far hills, don't gamble on open water. Hug the east shore back to Millstow's jetty."

      *goto ml_landing_talk_menu

      Orik's jaw tightens a fraction. He points a thumb toward a freshly patched strake low on the upturned skiff. "See that fresh oak? Second hull in ten days brought in from the southern cove with deep waterline gouges. Not rock scrapes, either. Clean angled cuts, too wide for a submerged branch."

      "And three nights back, someone walked off with six lengths of seasoned oak planking and a small keg of pine pitch straight off my drying rack after sundown. You don't steal dry oak and pitch unless you're patching boats somewhere out of sight."

      *goto ml_landing_talk_menu
