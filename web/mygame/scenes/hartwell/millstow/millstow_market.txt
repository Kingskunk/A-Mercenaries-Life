*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Fixed geography only.
*label ml_mk_place
*if (mk_first)
  The market fills a wide gravel-and-earth yard between a row of fieldstone cottages and a low drystone wall opening onto Azure Lake, with the two stone windmills turning on the grassy point beyond the water. Timber stalls roofed with patched canvas awnings line the cottage fronts, their trestles laid with folded bolts of undyed cloth, earthenware bowls, forged ironware, crusty barley loaves, rounds of waxed cheese, straw baskets of eggs, braided onions, and piled root vegetables. Near the center of the yard, an iron beam-scale hangs from a heavy timber post beside oak barrels, wooden crates, and tied burlap sacks of grain. The air carries the mixed scent of warm bread, milled flour, lake breeze, and damp earth.
*else
  The market fills the gravel yard between the fieldstone cottages and the lake wall, its canvas-awning stalls and hanging iron beam-scale looking out toward Azure Lake and the two windmills on the point.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, then ground state.
*label ml_mk_sky
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain drums across the stretched canvas awnings and beads along the iron chains of the beam-scale, turning the gravel yard muddy-brown.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Pale lake mist drifts over the low wall, blurring the distant windmills and hanging damp around the awning posts.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow slides off the sloped canvas and gathers along the top of the drystone wall while the lake lies leaden beyond.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (time_period = "Dusk")
    Low golden light slants off Azure Lake, warming the fieldstone fronts and casting long shadows beneath the trestles.
  *else
    Bright open light spreads across the yard, picking out the pale linen bolts and the slow turn of the mill vanes across the water.
*else
  Flat overcast cloud hangs over the yard, leaving the canvas awnings a muted cream against the slate-blue lake.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Water pools in the hollows of the packed gravel between the barrels, and the earth feels heavy underfoot.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Damp patches cling to the gravel around the trestle legs from the last rain.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 3: STREET ── Current market activity.
*label ml_mk_street
*if (time_period = "Dusk")
  Sellers pack remaining loaves, cheese rounds, and cloth bolts into wooden crates and cinch down the canvas corners as cottage lamps blink on.
  *return
*if (day_of_week = "Marketday")
  The yard is lively. Villagers, lake boatmen, and carters off the Hartwell road crowd between the trestles, hefting grain sacks, tapping waxed cheese, and bargaining beside the beam-scale.
  *return
*if (time_period = "Morning")
  Stallholders are setting out fresh barley loaves and baskets of eggs beside the cloth tables while checking the balance of the hanging beam-scale.
  *return
*if (mk_vignette = 1)
  A flour-dusted baker slides three crusty barley loaves across a board and counts copper bits into a tin cup.
*elseif (mk_vignette = 2)
  A fisherman sets a damp creel beside the beam-scale post and haggles over a sack of milled grain with a farmer.
*elseif (mk_vignette = 3)
  A woman tests the weave of an undyed wool bolt with her thumb before trading a braided string of onions for two clay bowls.
*else
  A carter hoists a tied burlap sack onto his shoulder and steps past the barrels toward the lakeside lane.
*return
