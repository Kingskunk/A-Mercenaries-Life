*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Fixed geography only. The long description prints once. Nothing here changes with the season or the hour.
*label ml_place
*if (ml_first_hub)
  Millstow strings itself along the southeastern bank of Azure Lake on a single gravel-and-earth lane. On the water side, a rough grass verge and a line of leaning timber fence posts drop gently down to reed beds and a wide stretch of slate-blue lake reaching toward a distant wooded bank.

  On the landward side sit cottages of fieldstone and weathered timber under moss-patched thatch and shingled roofs, divided by drystone walls, kitchen gardens, stacked firewood, oak barrels, and a plain handcart resting against the masonry. Ahead on a turf knoll above the roofs rise two stone-and-timber windmills, their lattice sails angled toward the lake breeze. Thin chimney smoke hangs over the eaves, and the air smells of cold water, woodsmoke, milled grain, and baking barley.
*else
  Millstow stretches along the southeastern bank of Azure Lake, its gravel lane running between the reedy shallows and a row of fieldstone-and-thatch cottages beneath the two windmills on the turf knoll.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, followed by the ground's recent-weather state. No people here.
*label ml_sky
*temp ml_dark false
*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *set ml_dark true
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain dimples the broad surface of Azure Lake, drips steadily from the thatched eaves, and turns the packed gravel muddy-brown.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  Gale wind drives whitecaps over Azure Lake and whips the reeds down against the bank. Rain hammers the shingles and stonework, and the windmills on the knoll hold their sails reefed bare against the gusts.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Thick lake mist rolls inland off the shallows, hiding the far bank and swallowing the upper mill towers in pale cloud.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow dusts the thatched roofs, the drystone garden walls, and the leaning posts by the reeds, while the lake lies leaden beside the whitened shore.
*elseif (weather = "Blizzard")
  Driven snow sweeps unchecked off the open lake, burying the grass edge and blotting out the mills and the far bank behind a moving wall of white.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (ml_dark)
    Stars show bright above the knoll, and a pale sheen of moonlight lies over the calm expanse of Azure Lake.
  *elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
    The setting sun lays long copper streaks over Azure Lake, and the shadows of the roofs and fence posts stretch far up the lane.
  *else
    Bright open daylight spreads over the lake and the turf knoll, warming the stonework and catching the slow turn of the mill vanes.
*else
  *if (ml_dark)
    Heavy cloud hides the stars, leaving Azure Lake a sheer black void beside the warm lamplight of the cottage windows.
  *else
    Overcast cloud sits over the lake and turns the surface a cool slate-blue beneath the pale sky.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Puddles linger in the grass beside the reeds, and the gravel track feels heavy and sodden underfoot.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The track is damp from recent rain, and moisture clings to the fence rails and garden masonry.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 3: STREET ── Current village activity. Daytime work follows the season; the rotating vignette follows the clock.
*label ml_street
*temp ml_vignette (((campaign_day * 24) + clock_hour) modulo 6) + 1
*temp ml_night false
*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *set ml_night true
*if ((street_life = "empty") and (not(ml_night)))
  The village track is empty. Shutters are fastened tight, handcarts rest pulled close under the eaves, and not a single skiff moves on the lake.
  *return
*if (ml_night)
  *if (ml_vignette = 1)
    Water laps softly through the reeds below the bank, and a lone lantern bobs briefly near the dwellings before vanishing indoors.
  *elseif (ml_vignette = 2)
    The heavy timber brake on one of the windmills groans once in a shift of breeze, then falls still.
  *elseif (ml_vignette = 3)
    Hushed voices and the scrape of a bench carry faintly from a doorway before a latch clicks shut.
  *elseif (ml_vignette = 4)
    A dog barks twice behind a garden hedge, answered by the rustle of waterfowl settling in the rushes.
  *elseif (ml_vignette = 5)
    The faint, sweet smell of banked bakehouse embers drifts down the track on the cool air off the lake.
  *else
    A timber shutter rattles once in a passing gust off the shallows, and the village settles back into quiet.
  *return
*if (time_period = "Dusk")
  Lamps are coming on behind the casements, flour-dusted hands head down the slope from the mills, and a shallow-draft skiff glides into the reeds for the night.
  *return
*if (day_of_week = "Hallowday")
  The mill vanes hang tied still above the roofs and the handcarts sit empty by the walls. Villagers in clean wool sit on the garden walls facing the lake, talking unhurriedly while children skip smooth pebbles out into the shallows.
  *return
*if (day_of_week = "Marketday")
  Extra grain carts wait on the grass edge and travelers from the Hartwell road walk the lane asking after flour, loaves, and fresh lake fish, while sacks are hoisted onto shoulder-boards near the cottages.
  *return
*if (current_season = "Spring")
  Garden plots behind the drystone walls are newly turned, and the spring breeze keeps both pairs of mill vanes turning briskly over the thaw.
*elseif (current_season = "Summer")
  Warm air carries the scent of cut grass and sun-heated rushes, and doors stay propped open to catch the breeze off Azure Lake.
*elseif (current_season = "Autumn")
  Sacks of newly threshed grain sit stacked under tarred cloth against the walls, and the mills turn steadily from morning onward to keep ahead of the harvest.
*else
  Woodsmoke hangs over the shingled eaves, thin crusted frost clings to the shaded rushes, and villagers move briskly between doors in heavy wool.
*if (ml_vignette = 1)
  A flour-dusted hand pushes a creaking handcart loaded with tied grain sacks down from the knoll.
*elseif (ml_vignette = 2)
  Two fishermen walk up from the reedy bank carrying a wicker creel between them on an oar.
*elseif (ml_vignette = 3)
  A woman leans over a garden wall to shake flour from an apron, sending a pale cloud drifting toward the shore.
*elseif (ml_vignette = 4)
  Far out on the slate-blue lake, a single skiff moves steadily past the reed line under short oar strokes.
*elseif (ml_vignette = 5)
  A carter pauses beside the fence posts to tighten the rope over a load of wrapped barley loaves bound for the Hartwell road.
*else
  A boy sits on a garden wall whittling a cork net float while watching the mill vanes turn overhead.
*return
