*comment ====================================================================================
*comment THE HORSE DEALER (the first mount). One library scene, entered with *goto_scene from two places that each set horse_place first: "bc" is the rail of the pens at the Barleycross common pasture (30 silver marks,
*comment 27 on a good bargain) and "mw" is the stalls of the Wagoner's Rest stable yard in Port Valen's Middle Ward (36, 32 on a good bargain: the yard feeds and shoes it, so it asks more). hd_buy is the sale, hd_sell the
*comment sale back (always 15 silver marks, at either place, so no one can buy at one and sell at the other for a profit). Not in *scene_list: compile.js lists it with verifyFileName.
*comment
*comment Who sells and which horse is for sale come from the five-day block's seed (horse_seed, stored under the page's lock so the hours the page costs cannot change the answer on a replay); each place has its own
*comment offset, so the two never show the same person. The pen's horse is pen_*, the seller sel_*; buying copies the pen's horse into horse_*, the one the player owns. The player may try to bargain once (a check).
*comment Owning a horse is has_horse. It is ridden automatically on the road (overworld-data.js, calendar.txt travel_step) and stabled in every settlement (calendar.txt horse_upkeep). Nothing here explains a trade: a
*comment person has a horse and a price.
*comment ====================================================================================
