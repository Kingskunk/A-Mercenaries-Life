*comment NAMES: the name generator's one entry point. The lists live in web/mygame/names-data.js.
*comment
*comment How a quest uses it:
*comment   *set name_race "human"        (so far only "human")
*comment   *set name_gender "f"          ("m", "f" or "any")
*comment   *set name_region "port"       ("port", "marches" or "any": which surnames are in play)
*comment   *set name_seed rj_pest_block  (any whole number; the same four values always give the same name)
*comment   *gosub_scene names name_make
*comment   ...then name_first, name_last and name_full hold the name until the next call.
*comment
*comment Keep the SEED (one number), not the name. Ask again whenever the name is needed: on the posting, at the
*comment door, at the reward. Nothing here is random, so a page refresh cannot change a name. Copy the result into
*comment *temp variables before the next name_make call if two people are on one page.
*comment
*comment Names of characters already in the game are never generated (RESERVED in names-data.js).
*comment ====================================================================================
