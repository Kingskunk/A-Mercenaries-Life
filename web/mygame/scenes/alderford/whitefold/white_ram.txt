An arch beside the stair leads to a walled stable yard with a covered trough, hitching rails and four dry stalls. The room smells of mutton broth, peat smoke and straw.

[b]◈ YOUR ROOM: THE WHITE RAM[/b]
*line_break
The room under the slate roof holds a plank bed, straw mattress, folded wool blanket and a tin basin beside a jug of water. Its shuttered window looks across the stable yard toward the pale quarry face. The room is plain, dry and warm enough once the door is barred.

*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  The yard lamp shows as a narrow line beneath the shutter, and an animal shifts in one of the stalls below.
*elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
  Last light rests on the quarry face before the yard falls into shadow.
*else
  Daylight comes through the shutter gaps and lies across the foot of the bed.

*if (weather_severity >= 3)
  Wind worries the shutter and rattles the slate overhead.
*elseif (weather = "Rain")
  Rain taps steadily above the bed and splashes in the yard below.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Cold clings to the window wall, though the room itself stays dry.

*if ((time_period = "Morning") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))

*elseif (time_period = "Night")
