[b]◈ THE QUARRY YARD[/b]
*line_break
The Quarry Yard lies below the lowest pale face of the Coldspine. Stone blocks sit in ordered ranks beneath open sheds, each marked in charcoal with its cut and measure. A hand crane stands over the loading bed, and a broad gate leads toward the stepped quarry beyond. White dust coats the cart wheels, the packed ground and the hems of every work coat.

*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain turns the dust to chalky slurry along the cutting beds.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Meltwater runs from the quarry steps and freezes in the deepest cart ruts.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  The pale face disappears upward into fog, leaving only the lower ledges visible above the sheds.
*elseif ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  Work lamps burn under the sheds, but the gate toward the quarry itself is barred.

*if (wf_yard_first)
  A middle-aged human woman stands by the weigh platform with a slate tucked against her hip. Her face is broad and weather-reddened, a pale seam cuts through one eyebrow, and chalk has settled into the creases of her dark work gloves. She glances from the cart marks to you.

  "Hessa Vail," she says. "If you need stone, wait until I finish weighing this load. If you need trouble, the mountain has enough of its own."
*else
  Hessa Vail marks another block on her slate beside the weigh platform.

      Hessa taps two chalk marks with one finger. "Cut blocks when a buyer can pay for the carting. Lime whenever the kilns have run long enough. The stone goes to walls, mills, weirs, hearths. The lime goes wherever somebody needs mortar or wants sour ground made useful."

      You look over the waiting carts. "And the wool?"

      "The folds carry that side of the village," Hessa says. "We share a road. That is enough."
