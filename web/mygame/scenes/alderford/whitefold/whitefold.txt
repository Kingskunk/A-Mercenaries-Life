*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ──
*label wf_place
Whitefold stands on hard, pale ground at the southern foot of the Coldspine. It is not one tight street but a spread of low stone steadings, fold yards and small barns gathered around the common and climbing the sheltered lower slopes. The quarry rises immediately behind the western end of the village in stepped white faces, with dark drill holes and narrow ledges under the mountain's broken slope. Slate roofs are weighted against the wind, and every household keeps some walled ground for vegetables, hens or a milk goat. The broader cart road comes in from Limestone Cut past a long trough where the teams drink. A narrower foothill road leaves the common northwest toward Redvein, running between the lower folds and the first broken slopes.

The Quarry Yard fills the foot of the face: cutting beds, stacked blocks under lean-to roofs, a weigh platform and low carts waiting by the gate. Beyond it, the lime kilns sit in a row of white-stained domes, their fuel sheds and ash pits kept apart from the cottages. But the greater part of Whitefold looks to the flocks: dry-stone walls divide the rough pasture, lambing sheds stand open behind the houses, and narrow lanes run between family pens toward the grazing above. Across the lane stands the Fleecefold, a broad shared weighing and baling barn of pale stone and dark timber, with open pens beside it. The White Ram faces the small common at the road end, its ram-horn sign creaking above a pair of stable doors. Chalk dust, lanolin, wood smoke and wet sheep hang in the cold air.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY AND GROUND MEMORY ──
*label wf_sky

*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain darkens the quarry face and turns the chalk dust in the yard to pale paste.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  Wind tears across the open foothill and rattles every loose gate in Whitefold.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Fog fills the lower ground below the village, leaving the quarry face as a pale wall above it.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow catches in the folds and lies in the quarry steps, turning the lanes to grey slush.
*elseif (weather = "Blizzard")
  Blown snow erases the far edge of the common and hides the Coldspine behind a white moving screen.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (wf_dark)
    Starlight catches on the quarry's pale ledges, while the kiln mouths show as dull red points below.
  *elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
    The last light holds on the upper quarry face while the cottages and folds settle into shadow.
  *else
    Clear light picks out the pale stone, dark sheep backs and the broken southern wall of the Coldspine.
*else
  A low overcast flattens the village into stone grey, wool grey and the white of the quarry face.
