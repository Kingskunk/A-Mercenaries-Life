[b]◈ THE FLEECEFOLD[/b]
*line_break
The Fleecefold is a long barn of pale stone and smoke-dark timber, its open side facing a yard of rail pens. Fresh fleeces sag over racks, each marked at the neck with a daub of dye, while older bales wait bound in sacking along the wall. A beam scale hangs above the hard-packed floor. Beside it, a low door stands open on a small room with a notice rail and a long, scarred table. Lanolin, damp wool and sharp lye hang in the warm air.

The Common pays one yard guard to sit by the weighing floor while bales and payment boxes are open. Their ash staff stays across their knees unless a cart goes through the loading door; the village does not keep a street patrol for it.
