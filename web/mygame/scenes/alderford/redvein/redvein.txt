*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ──
*label rv_place
Redvein occupies a fold in the southern foothills of the Coldspine, where the ground has been cut open in long red scars. Low cottages of rough grey stone stand on the lower slope, their roofs held down with stones against the wind. Above them, spoil heaps rise in uneven ridges, grassed over in places and raw where fresh loads have been tipped. The Eastcut Road enters between two of the heaps and bends south toward Alderford.

The Eastcut lies on the higher side of the village. A packed work yard runs before timber shelters built over the mine mouths, with sorting tables, empty ore carts and a tall wheel for lifting loads. A long shed beside the road holds spare beams, rope and shovels. Below the yard, wagons wait at a broad scale platform before taking their loads down the ridge.

The Gather House faces a small open space near the cottages, a square stone building with a bell over its door and benches under the eaves. The Red Lantern stands on the road side of the same space, its lower walls built of stone and its upper floor of dark boards. Behind it, a walled yard holds hitching rails, a trough and three covered stalls. The air smells of cold stone, damp earth, smoke and the sharp iron scent of fresh-cut ore.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ──
*label rv_sky
*temp rv_dark false
*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *set rv_dark true
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain runs in thin dark lines down the spoil heaps and gathers in the wheel tracks below the Eastcut.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  Wind sweeps loose grit across the open yard, and the bell above the mine gate has been tied still.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Fog fills the lower folds of the hills, leaving the nearer spoil heaps and pit sheds as dark shapes above it.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow catches on the heap tops and turns the road through the village to red-grey slush.
*elseif (weather = "Blizzard")
  Snow races along the exposed slope and hides the far end of the work yard behind a moving white screen.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (rv_dark)
    Starlight catches on pale stones in the heaps, while the pit sheds stand black against the slope.
  *elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
    Low light deepens the red cuts in the hills and leaves the cottage roofs dark below them.
  *else
    Clear light picks out the red earth in the cut slopes and the dark wall of the Coldspine beyond the village.
*else
  A low overcast lies across the hills and flattens the colour of the spoil heaps and road.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Water still lies in the deeper wheel tracks, and the lower edge of the work yard remains soft underfoot.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The road is drying from the centre outward, though dark patches remain beneath the heaps.
*return
