[b]◈ THE EASTCUT MARKET[/b]
*line_break
The work yard lies below a raw red slope where several dark mine mouths have been cut into the hill. Thick timber frames hold the ground above their entrances, and a bell rope hangs beside the nearest gate. The packed ground before the shelters is Redvein's market: carters' wagons and trestle stalls line its edge, miners' wives sell bread, roots and wool between the long tables of broken stone, and a raised scale platform takes the weight of each loaded wagon before it goes down the ridge. A large wooden wheel turns slowly at the far end of the yard, winding rope over a sheltered shaft.

*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain darkens the cut slope and taps steadily on the timber roofs over the mine mouths.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Fog drifts through the work yard and blurs the upper ends of the spoil heaps.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow gathers on the timber frames and melts where it touches the churned red ground.
*elseif ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  A few covered lamps burn near the gate, the stalls stand bare under their tarpaulins, and the work faces beyond the frames are black.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The yard stays soft around the cart tracks, with water darkening the ground beside the scale platform.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Red mud has dried in a thin crust along the cart ruts.

*if (rv_cut_first)
  The work follows a careful order: stone broken underground is brought up in baskets and carts, pale waste is thrown aside, and the red, heavy pieces are sorted into waiting wagons, while the stalls along the edge do a quieter trade of their own. The people at the tables keep their hands clear of one another's hammers and do not look up for long.
*elseif ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  Open sorting and trading have stopped for the moment, though the wheel and the guarded lamps show that work below has not entirely ended.
*else
  Stone, rope, wagon wheels and shoppers move through the yard in separate lines, each leaving space for the next.
