[b]◈ YOUR ROOM: THE RED LANTERN[/b]
*line_break
The room beneath the roof holds a plank bed, straw mattress, folded wool blanket and a tin basin beside a jug of water. A shuttered window looks across the stable yard to the red slope above the village. The room is plain and dry, with the smell of smoke caught in the boards.

*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  The yard lamp shows as a narrow line beneath the shutter, and a horse shifts somewhere below.
*elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
  Last light lies along the stable roof while the red slope beyond it darkens.
*else
  Daylight comes through the shutter gaps and falls across the foot of the bed.

*if (weather_severity >= 3)
  Wind worries the shutter and rattles the roof boards overhead.
*elseif (weather = "Rain")
  Rain taps steadily above the bed and splashes in the yard below.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Cold clings to the window wall, though the room itself stays dry.
