[b]◈ JANNA'S SIDE DOOR[/b]
*line_break

A narrow side door sits behind the smithy, tucked beside the chimney where the mill-race noise fades into the river below. A little warmth reaches the latch from the stones behind it.

*if (weather_severity >= 3)
  The weather presses hard against the outer wall, but the chimney gives off a steady heat.
*elseif (weather = "Rain")
  Rain ticks against the eaves and gutters along the smithy's rear wall.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Fog has softened the mill-race to a low, steady rush beyond the buildings.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Meltwater taps from the eaves into the dark behind the smithy.

*choice
  # Knock and tell Janna you came to see her.
    You knock twice on the side door. When the bolt draws back, you say, "I came to see you, if this is a good time."

    Janna opens the door only wide enough to stand in it at first. The forge soot has been washed from her face, though a dark smudge still shadows one thumb. At your words, her mouth curves. "You picked a better time than shouting over Torvald's hammer. Come in, then."

  # Knock and ask whether you have caught her at a bad time.

    Janna studies you for a moment, then unhooks the door farther. "You would not be standing here if you wanted to leave. Come in. If I needed the door shut, I would say so."

  # Knock, then decide not to interrupt her evening.
    You knock twice on the side door. When Janna opens it, you give a small shake of your head. "I only wanted to say I was thinking of you. I will let you keep your evening."

    Janna leans on the frame. "That is kind. Come back when you want to talk, not only when you think you should." She waits until you have turned toward the street before closing the door.

"Torvald taught me more than he says aloud," she tells you. "The forge is good work, and I am proud of it. But I want a bench with my own mark cut into the iron. Not because his work is lacking. Because I want to know what I can build under my own name."
