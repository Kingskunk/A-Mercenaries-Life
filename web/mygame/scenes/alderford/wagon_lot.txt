*comment Port Valen is made up. It plays the same part the wagon lot plays in Timbermouth and the wagon yard outside the River Gate plays in Port Valen: the master makes the offer here, the player signs here, and
*comment the line musters here the next morning. The offer, the signing, the muster and the road are the escort scene's own labels; this scene is only the PLACE (place layer, sky layer, a rolled lot-life layer)
*comment and the choices that lead into them. The master is the same kind of generated person, seeded from the five-day block plus 7000 (es_master_make), so nobody else's job hands out the same face.
*comment
*comment The lot is part of the Lower Wharf Market, so it follows the Market's hours (af_ok_market, decided in alderford.txt af_hours). The town menu shows a shortcut to it (af_lot_show) only while a line is
*comment making up or the player holds a job that sets out from here. The line makes up on one day of the week (es_ctx_dow, set by es_set_line; the prose never names it).
*comment

*comment ====================================================================================

[b]◈ THE CARTERS' LOT[/b]
*line_break
Beside the Lower Wharf Market's uphill gate, a long lot of trodden earth has been fenced off with rails and left open to the lane that climbs to the palisade. Wagons stand in it nose to tail, with ox yokes and spare wheels hung from the rails and a picket line of beasts running down one end. Casks of brine-cured fish, bundles of sawn boards and sacks of grain wait along the fence under tarps, and the ground is churned to a grey mud wherever the carts turn. The roar of the weir carries across the lot, and the air smells of brine, green timber, dung and wet straw.

*gosub af_lot_sky

*if (weather_severity >= 3)
  The oxen have been led under the lean-to at the end of the lot and stand with their backs to the weather, and the hands have crowded in beside them. Every wagon is sheeted down where it stands.
*elseif (al_state = "muster")
  Four wagons stand nose to tail along the rails with their tarps lashed down over their goods. Oxen shift in their yokes, a hand walks the column tapping each hub with a mallet, and a lead horse stamps at the lane end.
*elseif (time_period = "Morning")
  *if (al_life = 1)
    Hands rake straw under the lean-to while a boy carries water to the oxen in two buckets on a yoke.
  *elseif (al_life = 2)
    A wagon stands with a wheel off while a wheelwright and his apprentice argue over a split felloe, and a hand keeps the ox from wandering.
  *else
    Casks are rolled up a plank into a wagon bed, and a hand chalks a mark on each head as it goes past.
*elseif ((time_period = "Midday") or (time_period = "Afternoon"))
  *if (al_life = 1)
    A hand shoes an ox in a sling at the end of the lot, with the animal's foot tied up and its eyes rolling.
  *elseif (al_life = 2)
    Two hands grease an axle with a paddle from a bucket of black fat, and a third lies asleep in the straw with his hat over his face.
  *else
    A wagon rolls in down the lane from the gate, its team steaming from the climb, and the hands come out to meet it and unhitch the beasts.
*else
  *if (al_life = 1)
    Teams are led in off the lane one by one and rubbed down, and a cookfire is lit under the lean-to.
  *elseif (al_life = 2)
    A hand walks the rails with a lantern and counts the carts under it, touching each one as he passes.
  *else
    A carter sits on an upturned bucket mending a harness strap by the last of the light, while an ox noses at his pocket.

  A caravan is being made up. The wagons stand along the rails with their goods already roped, and the caravan master walks their length, checking ropes and wheels.

*comment ── The sky: weather and light only, no people. ──
*label af_lot_sky
*temp al_night false
*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *set al_night true
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain drums on the tarps and the lean-to roof and turns the lot to a field of grey puddles.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  A gale tears at the rails and drives the rain flat across the lot, and spray from the weir comes with it.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Mist hangs over the lot, blurring the far rails and the roofs of the curing lofts below.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow settles on the tarps and the rails, and melts in the ruts.
*elseif (weather = "Blizzard")
  Snow drives across the lot in a freezing gale and piles against the rails.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (al_night)
    Starlight silvers the rails and the wet edges of the ruts.
  *elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
    The low sun lays the long shadows of the wagons across the lot.
  *else
    Sunlight lies across the lot and dries the top of the mud.
*else
  *comment Overcast
  A flat grey sky sits low over the lot and drains the color from the tarps.

*comment ── WEATHER MEMORY (calendar ground_state, strictly no people) ──
*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  After days of rain the ruts stand full of grey water, and the lot is a churned mire from rail to rail.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The ruts still hold puddles from the last rain.
*return
