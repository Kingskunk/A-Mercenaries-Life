[b]◈ ON THE ROAD[/b]
*line_break
Four soldiers come up the street toward you at a walk, close to being in step. Each wears the Carrion raven on the shoulder. The one in front, a broad woman with a split lip healing at one corner, raises two fingers when she sees you.

"You're the one off the roster," she says. "Captain's asking for you. We're to bring you in."

"Am I in trouble?"

"Nobody said. Nobody said to put a hand on you either, so I'd guess not." She tips her head up the street, the direction they came from. "Compound."

They fall in around you, two ahead and two behind. Carters take one look at the five of you and pull their wagons to the side of the street, and the walk to the compound goes quicker than it would alone.

[b]◈ THE CONTRACT OFFICE[/b]
*line_break
The soldier with the split lip knocks once on Vane's door, opens it for you, and waits outside in the corridor.

Vane is at the trestle desk where you last saw him. Two sheets of paper and a scrap folded small lie in a row in front of him. He finishes the line he is reading before he looks up.

"Sit." He waits until you do.

"I sent you to see Ysolde, climb Kestrel's tower and hear Varren out. You did all three, and each of them has been in this room since, about you." He lays his palm flat on the first sheet.

*if ((rotten_rib_resolution = "blackmail") and (rotten_rib_strikes = 0))
  "Ysolde. The timber orders moved the same week you laid that waybill on her table. Brant has his oak back, and Elric is still in his office, doing as he's told." He taps the page. "Nobody threw a punch and nobody threw you out. That's how I like paper handled."
*elseif (rotten_rib_resolution = "blackmail")
  "Ysolde. The timber orders moved, and Brant has his oak back." He taps a line lower down. "She also writes that Elric's draymen walked you out of his office once before you had what you were after. You climbed back up and finished it. Next time, finish it the first time. A man who's thrown you out once knows your face."
*else
  "Ysolde. You brought her the waybill, and it's worth having." He turns the sheet face down. "The oak's gone. The ship slid down the ways on green wood, and our timber is still sitting in the Scales' sheds. Elric's men put you out of his office twice, and after the second throw he stopped worrying about you."
*if (rotten_rib_ledger_clue)

  His finger finds a line near the bottom. "She noticed you read the chalk on that pillar before you walked in. Good."

He moves the folded scrap to the middle of the desk.

*if (silt_gate_face_seen)

  "Kestrel has both names, and the sergeants on the far bank are polite to her now. Nobody down at the gates could pick you out of a crowd, by her count. That's worth as much as the names."

The third sheet he leaves where it lies.

*if (slasher_resolution = "arrested")

He cut past two militiamen in the rush out of his own post, and they're lucky to be walking. You found him, which is more than anyone else in this city managed. But he's out there now with a grudge, and he knows our name."

*if (vd_clean = 3)

"When I need you again, I'll send for you." He draws the next dispatch toward him. "Anything else?"
