*comment rope-and-oil house after dark. Hask puts the player forward; the woman in the room above gives the job.
*comment This scene returns to the Dredge-End market hub and is intentionally outside the main district file so

*comment
*comment THE THREE-OPTION BLOCK AT THE END IS AN ESCALATION LADDER, not three parallel checks. All three
*comment approach rolls land on night_list_check; a pass walks straight through, and a failure sets
*comment combat_enemy_advantage and hands the player a fight (night_list_fight). Winning that fight pays
*comment exactly what a clean pass pays. Losing it -- or fleeing, or being "rescued" at 1 HP -- lands on
*comment night_list_failed, whose prose was always written as a fight that went badly. A failed roll is
*comment therefore never a dead end: it is a second route to the same errand, priced in blood.
*comment ====================================================================================================

*comment night_list_repeat (a later run of the list, after the first was resolved) and night_list_repeat_trouble (the roll that decides whether a repeat run meets trouble) are stats created in startup.txt, not scene temps: the district enters this scene AT A LABEL

  Someone below waves you up in silence. The steps end at a low room above the shop, warmer than the street and close with the smell of lamp oil, wet hemp, and old wax. Coils of rope sit against the far wall. A slate table holds a small leather wallet and a short strip of paper marked with neat columns.

  A woman in a plain dark coat sits at the table, counting its contents before she knots it shut. She looks up only after finishing.

  "Hask's runner. Sit, or don't."

  *choice
    # Tell her Hask said you would run the list, stay quiet, and bring the payment home.
      "Hask said there was work," you say. "He said I could carry a list and not ask questions."
      *goto night_list_offer
    # Ask who she is.
      "Who are you?"
      "I handle the work here. That is enough for tonight."
      *goto night_list_briefing
    # Leave the room.
      She gives a small nod and resumes her count. The person below is waiting when you come down.
      *goto_scene port_valen/port_valen_dredge_end cut_market_page
*else
  The person below recognizes you and steps aside. On the upper floor, the woman has another marked strip on the table and a fresh pair of purses next to it.

  *if ((black_oath_night_list_count >= 3) and (short_purse_stage = "unstarted"))
    She pushes the marked paper aside before you reach the table, her dark eyes measuring you in the candlelight.

    "I have the usual list," she says. "Or I have a matter that needs an outside hand."

    *choice
      # Hear what needs an outside hand.

      # "I'm only here for a night run."
        *goto night_list_offer
      # Leave the room.
        She gives a small nod and pulls her papers closer.
        *goto_scene port_valen/port_valen_dredge_end cut_market_page
  *else
    "You want a list?" she asks.
    *goto night_list_offer

*label night_list_offer

She pulls the marked strip closer and puts a finger over each line as she speaks.

"First, the boat-sheds. Leave this shop by the waterside. Go past the scrap yard's driftwood fence and find the net-maker's shed just beyond it. Knock twice on the back plank. Two tied leather purses will be waiting."

Her finger moves to the next mark. "Then head toward the alley shrine, but do not go through its arch. Follow the waterside and go down the steps below it. Someone will be waiting. Give them the first; they will give you a wrapped parcel."

"Last, Lamp Stair. Go up by the shrine and follow the seawall with it to your left until the red lamps start. Use the service passage behind the cookshop. Parcel for the other. Bring it all here."

*choice
  # Accept the list and make the run.
    *set night_list_repeat false
    *if (black_oath_night_list_stage = "resolved")
      *set night_list_repeat true
    *set black_oath_night_list_stage "active"
    *set black_oath_night_list_resolution "none"
    *set black_oath_night_list_paid false
    *goto night_list_route
  # Ask what is in the packet.
    "What is in the packet?"
    "Not your concern. If you can carry it without knowing, carry it. If not, leave it on the table."
    *goto night_list_offer
  # Decline the work tonight.
    "Not tonight," you say.
    "That's fine. I'll get someone else."
    *goto_scene port_valen/port_valen_dredge_end cut_market_page
