Lyra sits on the edge of her end cot in the Vanguard barracks, her dark wool cloak folded over her knee and a leather satchel resting beside her. Her recurve bow is cased and strapped tight against the wall pegs.

She looks up as you approach down the aisle, her expression tighter than usual. "${name}," she says quietly, glancing toward the empty bunks nearby before dropping her voice. "Got a minute?"

You pull up an empty crate beside her cot. "What happened?"

"I've been digging into my aunt's death since I squared up with you," she says, resting her forearms on her knees. "Asking around the lower terraces, checking what was left of her things, following up on who was bringing her work before she died." She pauses, her jaw setting. "I found some troubling stuff, ${name}. Whatever she got tangled up in, it isn't ordinary slum trouble, and it's bigger than one street crew."

She meets your eyes squarely. "I can't do this alone. Not without getting myself cornered. I need another pair of hands, someone I can trust to watch my back."
