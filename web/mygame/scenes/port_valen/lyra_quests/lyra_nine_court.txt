*label nine_court_hook
*set nine_court_hook_seen true

Lyra sits on the edge of her end cot in the Vanguard barracks, her dark wool cloak still damp at the hem and dried canal mud crusted up the heels of her boots. Her recurve bow lies unstrung across her knees while she rubs an oiled rag slowly down one limb.

She hears your footsteps on the floorboards and looks up. "You're back," she says, setting the rag aside. "I tried Dredge-End."

You pull up an empty crate beside her bunk. "Any luck?"

"None." She folds the rag over her knee, her jaw tight. "The tenants along the lower terraces shut their doors the second I start asking about my aunt's last week. Half of them are scared of their own shadows, and the other half pretend they never saw her."

She glances down the barracks aisle to make sure the nearest bunks are empty, then drops her voice. "There's one man who knows. Hask. He runs the scrap yard down by the boat-sheds on the canal bank."

"You know him?"

"He and my aunt went back twenty years," Lyra says quietly. "Traded copper, swapped favors, looked out for the kids when winter hit hard. If anyone saw who was bringing her piecework, or what was in her workbox before someone cleared it out, it's him." Her fingers tighten on the unstrung bow. "I walked up to his fence this afternoon. Before I got three words out, he picked up a rusted iron bar off his sorting bench and told me to clear off his yard."

"Because of the Oath."

"Because I walked out and took what wasn't mine." She meets your eyes squarely. "He won't hear a word from me, ${name}. You, though, aren't on his blacklist. If you go down to the boat-sheds and ask about his old friend, he might actually answer."

*label nine_court_hook_choice
*choice

    *set nine_court_stage "active"

    "I'll head down to the scrap yard and see what he knows," you say.

    Some of the tension leaves her shoulders, though her gaze stays sharp. "Don't lead with my name," she warns, reaching for her bowstring. "He'll shut down on the spot if he thinks I sent you. Ask about the spinner who died in the lower tenements, his old friend. Once he starts talking, see if he kept anything from her room, or saw who paid her."

    She slips the bowstring loop over the top tip and sets the wood aside. "I owe you another one for this. Find me when you have something."
