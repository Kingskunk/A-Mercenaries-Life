[b]◈ THE GRAIN GREEN[/b]
*line_break
The Green is a long, open strip of packed earth beside the High Grey Road. A beam scale stands under a roof of rough boards, its iron hooks black with use. Around it, simple timber frames hold canvas awnings when people are selling, and low stone blocks give buyers somewhere to rest a sample sack. The storehouses rise behind the fence with their loading doors above head height and their lower walls rubbed pale by carts passing close.

*if (marlcross_milling_fair_today)
  *if (marlcross_milling_fair_indoors)
    The fair's tables and judges have been moved into the Cross Hall, but the Green still holds the abandoned outline of its work: tied-down canvas, benches carried in against the fence, and a line of disappointed carts along the road.
  *elseif (marlcross_milling_fair_phase = "morning")
    For the Milling Fair, the scale shed has become a judges' table, with a hand-painted board naming ${marlcross_milling_fair_pie_description} and ${marlcross_milling_fair_prize_description}. Pie sellers claim the awnings in a row, and volunteers are laying long boards across barrels for the contestants' table.
  *elseif (marlcross_milling_fair_phase = "contest")
    The scale is draped with red cloth and the Green is ringed three deep with onlookers. A long table stands beneath the awnings, with rows of ${marlcross_milling_fair_pie_description} waiting behind it and the judges poised beside the prize board.
  *elseif (marlcross_milling_fair_phase = "afternoon")
    Crumbs and flour mark the long contest table. The prizes are still displayed beneath the scale shed while the remaining sellers do brisk trade with visitors who have stayed to hear the result.
  *else
    Only the last pie sellers remain. The contest table has been cleared, though its floury outline and the trampled straw around it show how full the Green was earlier.

*if (weather_severity >= 3)
  The awnings have been stripped down and the scale beam is tied fast. Rain or snow lashes through the empty frames, while the storehouse doors remain barred.
*elseif (weather = "Rain")
  Rain runs from the awning edges and darkens every sackcloth cover left on the Green.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Fog hangs between the awning frames and hides the far end of the storehouse line.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Slush fills the low places around the scale, and flakes gather on the broad beam above it.
*elseif ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  The awnings are furled and the Green lies empty under a single lamp by the scale shed.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The ground beneath the scale is still soft enough to hold the sharp print of every boot and hoof.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Dry chaff has begun to cling to the dark tracks left by the last rain.

*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  The market is shut, but the road remains open along its edge.
*elseif (mc_market_first)
  Sacks stand in small groups beneath the awnings, each tied at the neck with cord or a strip of cloth. People lift handfuls of grain into their palms, compare colour and weight, then carry their sacks toward the scale or back to their carts.
*else
  Grain moves through the Green in a slow exchange of lifted sacks, weighed loads and carts leaving room for one another along the road.
