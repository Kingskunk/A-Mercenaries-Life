*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ──
*label mc_place
Marlcross stands where the High Grey Road meets three farm tracks on the eastern high ground. The meeting place is a broad triangle of pale, hard-packed marl, cut deep with wheel ruts. An old stone cross stands on a stepped base at its centre, its arms worn blunt by weather and passing hands. The Cross Hall faces it from the west side: a low timber building with a bell frame over its door and a line of benches beneath the eaves.

The Grain Green opens along the north side of the road, a fenced strip of packed earth with a beam scale, empty stall frames and a stone trough. Beyond it stand long raised storehouses on short stone feet, their shuttered loading doors looking out toward the cart track. On the ridge, a round windmill turns above the roofs. Its sails are visible from every open part of the village, and the sound of its gears carries down when the wind is right.

The southern track passes a wainwright's yard where bare wheel rims, wagon tongues and stacks of seasoned planks lean beneath open sheds. Farther out, an ox common lies behind a post-and-rail fence, with a pond at its lowest corner and enough cropped grass for teams stopping on the road. The air smells of dry straw, flour dust, animal sweat and axle grease.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, followed by the ground's memory.
*label mc_sky
*temp mc_dark false
*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *set mc_dark true
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain taps on the storehouse shutters and gathers in the wheel ruts, turning the pale ground slick and brown.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  Wind snaps at the empty market awnings and the mill's sails have been tied off against the gusts.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Fog fills the folds of the high ground, leaving the road marker and the mill as pale shapes above it.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow clings to the cart roofs and piles in the corners of the stone cross's base.
*elseif (weather = "Blizzard")
  Snow races across the open rise and smears the road into a white, shifting track. The mill is only a dark bulk behind its furled sails.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (mc_dark)
    Starlight catches on the road marker and the mill sails stand black above the roofs.
  *elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
    Low light turns the marl and the dust on the road a warm red-brown, while the ridge begins to darken behind the mill.
  *else
    Clear light lies open across the rise, and the grainfields show in long bands beyond the last barns.
*else
  A flat overcast hangs over the ridge and dulls the pale ground around the crossing.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Water remains in the deepest ruts, and the path between the Green and the storehouses is still soft underfoot.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The marl has dried pale again except where carts have pressed dark tracks across it.
*return

  *elseif (marlcross_milling_fair_phase = "morning")
    Fair carts crowd the roadside and strips of red and cream cloth have been tied to the market frames. The smell of pastry comes warm from the Green while people carry benches and pie tins across the crossing.

    The crossing hums with the noise of a gathered village. Every few moments a cheer rises from the Grain Green, then breaks apart into laughter and shouted guesses about the contest.
