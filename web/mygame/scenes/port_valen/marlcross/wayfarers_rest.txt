An arch beside the house leads to a walled yard with hitching rails, a covered trough and a stable range under a hay loft. Wagon tongues stand propped beneath a lean-to beyond the stalls. The air smells of woodsmoke, cooked onions, straw and warm animal hide.

[b]◈ YOUR ROOM: THE WAYFARER'S REST[/b]
*line_break
The room beneath the low roof holds a plank bed, a straw mattress, a folded wool blanket, and a tin basin beside a jug of water. A shuttered window looks over the stable yard, where the roofline of the hay loft cuts across the view. The room is plain, dry and close enough to the road that wheel noise reaches it through the wall.

*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  The yard lamp sends a narrow bar of light under the shutter, and an animal shifts somewhere below.
*elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
  The last light lies along the stable roof while the yard grows busy below.
*else
  Daylight enters through the shutter gaps and falls across the foot of the bed.

*if (weather_severity >= 3)
  Wind presses at the shutter and rattles the roof boards overhead.
*elseif (weather = "Rain")
  Rain taps steadily above the bed and splashes in the yard below.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Cold rests against the window wall, though the room itself remains dry.
