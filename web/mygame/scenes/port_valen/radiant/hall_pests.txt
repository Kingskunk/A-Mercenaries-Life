*comment ====================================================================================
*comment VERMIN IN THE HOUSE: the first radiant Hiring Hall job (Gilded Scales page)
*comment
*comment One job with two tables. WHERE it happens is a Middle Ward house most of the time and, rarely, a Patrician
*comment Quarter house. WHAT is in the walls is a pack of giant rats or a giant snake. The posting on the wall only says
*comment "vermin in the walls" and names the household, so the creature is a surprise on arrival.
*comment
*comment Wired in (2026-10-06):
*comment   - The wall posting and the signing are in port_valen_civic_heights.txt (pv_ch_roll_read, pv_ch_roll_sign). Signing
*comment     needs an insignia, which the Seals window sells (pv_ch_seals_buy). Signing calls rj_pest_select and
*comment     rj_pest_describe, sets rj_pest_stage "active" and rj_pest_until (three days).
*comment   - The house is reached from a button in the Middle Ward hub or the Quarter avenue hub, while the job is open.
*comment   - A Quarter job counts as admission at the Inner Gate (the two writ checks in port_valen_upper_wharves.txt).
*comment   - compile.js lists the file with verifyFileName. Not in *scene_list.
*comment
*comment Names: the patron is a generated person (names.txt, web/mygame/names-data.js). The five-day block is the seed, so the
*comment wall, the hub button, the door and the payment all show the same name, and nothing about the name is stored.
*comment The posting gives the patron and the kind of house, never a street.
*comment
*comment Rules this file follows: the patron pays at the door (no return trip to the Hall); the counter is
*comment hall_jobs_scales (one per government, Port Valen's page); a failed roll never hands back the thing it was
*comment gambling for (a failed surprise only loses the surprise, a failed clean solve starts the fight with the creature
*comment first); Guidance does not help the clean-solve check (check_no_guidance).
*comment
*comment Numbers (all tunable): 4 silver for the job, stealth DC 12, clean solve DC 10 plus proficiency bonus.
*comment Fight stats are not balance-simmed: two library mire-rats, or one beast named Giant Snake with
*comment 9 HP, AC 12, +4 to hit.
*comment ====================================================================================
