*comment ====================================================================================
*comment THE COURIER JOB (radiant): carry a sealed letter, a sealed contract or a small parcel to a named person at another place, on foot, on the free map. The sixth radiant job, and the first that does not
*comment move the player anywhere: it is taken at the Hiring Hall board and delivered from the map card of the destination, so the player walks wherever and whenever they like inside the term.
*comment
*comment Design (the user agreed, 2026-10-11): delivery is on the OVERWORLD MAP CARD ("Deliver ..." beside the tile's other buttons, offered by overworld.txt ow_here), not inside each village, so a
*comment new town, village or fort needs no edit here. Pay is about half a copper bit a mile with a floor; every third delivery (cr_credit counts them) adds one to the Insignia's page (hall_jobs_scales); a late parcel simply lapses,
*comment no penalty and no message (radiant_hub.txt rh_check). Letters, sealed contracts and small parcels only: more valuable work is for the higher Insignia ranks (not built), and a posting already carries
*comment a grade (always 1) for that.
*comment
*comment The board is Overworld.courierPostings (overworld-data.js): a pure function of the origin tile and the day, so a refresh shows the same three postings; the recipient is made from names-data.js
*comment (race by the destination's setting, surname by its region, a look phrase), seeded by the posting, so the same person is always at the same place. The postings are held in crp1_* to crp3_*; a taken
*comment one is flagged for the day (crpN_taken). The satchel holds three parcels, in crs1_* to crs3_*: no arrays, three fixed slots, taken first-free.
*comment
*comment Replay: the take and the delivery each change state once, under a page-id lock (cr_take_locked, and the currency lock for delivery); both pages print from values the lock does not change.
*comment
*comment EVERY settlement has a board (the user, 2026-10-11): the origin is cr_origin (a tile id), and the board is the same generator for any of them. Port Valen's is printed inside the Hiring Hall board page
*comment (port_valen_civic_heights.txt pv_ch_roll_read, which sets cr_origin "pv" and calls cr_board_print, with choices only). Every other settlement's existing notice option (the Factor's Halls of Timbermouth and Alderford,
*comment the Cross Hall, the Tide House, the Gather House, the Longhouse's board of cords, Barleycross's notice post, Whitefold's notice rail in the Fleecefold) ends by setting cr_origin and going to cr_read, which prints that
*comment board's postings and offers them, and goes straight back (cr_back) if there is nothing to take. cr_back is the one table of where each place returns to; a new settlement is one line there, one line in
*comment COURIER_TILES (overworld-data.js) and a call to cr_read from its own notice option.
*comment
*comment Not in *scene_list: compile.js lists it with verifyFileName. Wired in: the notice options named above, radiant_hub.txt (lapse, "Your posting"), overworld.txt (the Deliver options) and
*comment overworldmap.js (their buttons), startup.txt variables.
