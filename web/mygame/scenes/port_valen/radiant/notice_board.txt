*comment ====================================================================================
*comment SETTLEMENT NOTICE BOARD: single shared renderer for every settlement's local quest
*comment notices, origin-specific courier contracts, and (for towns with a yard) wagon-lot
*comment link. Every non-Port-Valen settlement board sets cr_origin and cr_inline true and
*comment goes to nb_read here. cr_origin identifies the physical board; the scene never makes
*comment boards share postings or state. A bespoke quest adds a notice only through its own
*comment eligibility block here (using cr_can for the board's Insignia page gate), while its
*comment quest scene owns acceptance and resolution.
*comment ====================================================================================

*label nb_read
*gosub_scene port_valen/radiant/hall_courier cr_board_prepare
*temp nb_peddler_open false
*if ((((cr_origin = "tm") or (cr_origin = "fv")) and (peddler_stage = "none")))
  *set nb_peddler_open true
  *set pd_origin cr_origin
*temp nb_forge_open false
*if ((cr_origin = "fo") and (ahf_stage = 0))
  *set nb_forge_open true
*temp nb_mare_open false
*if ((cr_origin = "bm") and (bm_mare_stage = 0))
  *set nb_mare_open true
*temp nb_rv_tunnel_open false
*if ((cr_origin = "rv") and ((rv_st_stage = 0) and ((character_level >= 2) and ((rv_hall_seen) and (rv_lantern_seen)))))
  *set nb_rv_tunnel_open true
*temp nb_any_quest ((((nb_peddler_open) or (nb_forge_open)) or (nb_mare_open)) or (nb_rv_tunnel_open))
*temp cr_lot_here false
*if (((cr_origin = "lg") or ((cr_origin = "tm") or (cr_origin = "af"))) and (cr_lot_link))
  *set cr_lot_here true
*temp cr_any true
*if (not((crp1_open) or ((crp2_open) or ((crp3_open) or ((crp1_full) or ((crp2_full) or (crp3_full)))))))
  *set cr_any false
*if ((not(nb_any_quest)) and ((not(cr_lot_here)) and (not(cr_any))))
  *if (cr_inline)
    *set cr_inline false
    *page_break Step back…
  *goto_scene port_valen/radiant/hall_courier cr_back
*temp cr_where "Among the notices"
*if ((cr_origin = "tm") or (cr_origin = "af"))
  *set cr_where "Among the postings on the board"
*elseif (cr_origin = "fv")
  *set cr_where "Among the scraps on the post"
*elseif (cr_origin = "fo")
  *set cr_where "Beside the board of cords"
*elseif (cr_origin = "wf")
  *set cr_where "Beside the notice rail"
*elseif (cr_origin = "lg")
  *set cr_where "Below the forecast"
*elseif (cr_origin = "nv")
  *set cr_where "Under the heading for House and Village work"
*elseif (cr_origin = "bm")
  *set cr_where "Under the heading chalked for Hall work"
*elseif (cr_origin = "fm")
  *set cr_where "On the pins under the chalked heading"
*elseif (cr_origin = "rn2")
  *set cr_where "Under the chalked heading beside the back door"
*elseif (cr_origin = "pc")
  *set cr_where "Pinned along the wooden notice rack by the back wall"
*elseif (cr_origin = "rv")
  *set cr_where "Beside the hauling lists on the rail"
*temp cr_head "THE NOTICE BOARD"
*temp cr_seal "the city's"
*if (cr_origin = "lg")
  *set cr_head "LANGSTON HALL: THE CONTRACT BOARD"
  *set cr_seal "the Hall's counterseal"
*if (((((cr_origin = "nv") or (cr_origin = "bm")) or (cr_origin = "fm")) or (cr_origin = "rn2")) or (cr_origin = "pc"))
  *set cr_head "THE VILLAGE HEAD'S HALL: THE CONTRACT BOARD"
  *set cr_seal "the village head's countersign"
*if (not(cr_inline))
  [b]◈ ${cr_head}[/b]
  *line_break
*set cr_inline false

*if (nb_peddler_open)
  One notice is newer than the rest, in a careful, uneven hand:

  [i]"WORD WANTED. My husband Aldis Pellam went into the Longshade to gather plants, with two hired guards, and did not come out. We held his funeral without him, and I would still know what happened. Eight silver for the telling. The last cottage on the Lane, Barleycross. Merel Pellam."[/i]

*if (nb_forge_open)
  A folded sheet is pinned beside the knotted cords with a heavy iron nail, written in thick, square ink:

  [i]"FORGE BURNED. Someone put a torch to my smithy roof last night. Forty copper bits to whoever brings me the name and proof, or brings the bastard to my door. Durgan, the smithy off the work track."[/i]

*if (nb_mare_open)
  A card pinned beneath STRAYS AND BRANDS carries a dark blue wax seal bearing a horse, written in a tidy clerk's hand:

  [i]"STRAYED. Spotted black and white mare out of the Remount Yard, Countess's brand on the left shoulder, wearing shoes with our forge mark. Four silver marks for her return unharmed. Apply at the Remount Yard gate. Sgt. Dagny Marren."[/i]

*if (nb_rv_tunnel_open)
  A heavy card pinned under the wooden slats bears the green wax mark of the Gilded Scales, written in bold, hurried block letters:

  [i]"EASTCUT CLEARANCE. Beasts or worse broke into the Old South Tunnel three days back and killed two haulers. Eight silver marks on proof the passage is clear. Licensed bearers only. Apply at the Eastcut gate. Overseer Berek."[/i]

*if (cr_any)
  ${cr_where}, a few sheets are ruled like forms, each headed with a posting number and sealed twice, the patron's in wax and ${cr_seal} pressed beside it.
*elseif (not(nb_any_quest))
  No courier sheet is left on the board for you.

  *if ((nb_peddler_open) and (cr_can))
    # Press your insignia to Merel Pellam's notice and take the missing-gatherer job.
      *goto_scene timbermouth/barleycross/overdue_peddler pd_sign
  *if ((nb_peddler_open) and (not(cr_can)))
    # Take Merel Pellam's missing-gatherer job.
      *goto_scene timbermouth/barleycross/overdue_peddler pd_need
  *if ((nb_mare_open) and (cr_can))
    # Press your insignia to the stray-mare notice and take the job.

  *if ((nb_mare_open) and (not(cr_can)))

  *if ((nb_rv_tunnel_open) and (cr_can))
    # Press your insignia to Overseer Berek's mine-clearance notice.
      *goto_scene alderford/redvein/singing_tunnel rv_st_notice_take
  *if ((nb_rv_tunnel_open) and (not(cr_can)))
    # Take Overseer Berek's Eastcut tunnel notice.
      *goto_scene alderford/redvein/singing_tunnel rv_st_notice_need
  *if ((crp1_open) and (cr_can))
    # Press your insignia to the posting and take the courier job to ${crp1_to_name}.
      *set cr_pick 1

  *if ((crp1_open) and (not(cr_can)))
    # Take the courier job to ${crp1_to_name}.

  *if (crp1_full)
    # Take the courier job to ${crp1_to_name}.
      *goto nb_cr_full
  *if ((crp2_open) and (cr_can))
    # Press your insignia to the posting and take the courier job to ${crp2_to_name}.
      *set cr_pick 2
      *goto nb_cr_sign
  *if ((crp2_open) and (not(cr_can)))
    # Take the courier job to ${crp2_to_name}.
      *goto nb_cr_need
  *if (crp2_full)
    # Take the courier job to ${crp2_to_name}.
      *goto nb_cr_full
  *if ((crp3_open) and (cr_can))
    # Press your insignia to the posting and take the courier job to ${crp3_to_name}.
      *set cr_pick 3
      *goto nb_cr_sign
  *if ((crp3_open) and (not(cr_can)))
    # Take the courier job to ${crp3_to_name}.
      *goto nb_cr_need
  *if (crp3_full)
    # Take the courier job to ${crp3_to_name}.
      *goto nb_cr_full

    # Walk over to the wagon lot, where the line is making up.
