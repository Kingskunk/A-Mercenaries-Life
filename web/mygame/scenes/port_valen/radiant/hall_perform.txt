*comment ====================================================================================
*comment MUSIC FOR AN EVENING: the fourth radiant Hiring Hall job (Gilded Scales page). A bard-for-hire evening at a different place each time.
*comment
*comment The venue is a seeded roll from the posting's week: 1 the Wagoner's Rest (Middle Ward inn), 2 the Cleaved Keel (harbor tavern), 3 a private supper,
*comment 4 a wedding feast (Middle Ward houses), 5 a gala in the Patrician Quarter (rare; the posting is admission at the Inner Gate). The inn and the tavern are run by
*comment the keepers the player already knows (Oswin, Maret); the hosts of the supper, the wedding and the gala are generated people (names.txt).
*comment
*comment Three sets, one hour each. A set is one Performance check, and the player chooses the song: 1 a loud crowd-pleaser, 2 a tender ballad, 3 a clever comic number.
*comment The room has a taste, hinted in the prose and never shown as a number: its preferred song is DC 10, its disliked one DC 14, the third DC 12 (+2 at the gala). The
*comment taste shifts across the night, so reading the room pays. A lute adds +1 (check_task_type "perform", dice_engine.txt); without one the house lends a battered lute and
*comment the job still plays, since carried goods unlock options and never gate the path.
*comment
*comment Pay is a venue fee plus a tip for each set that landed: inn or tavern 3 + 4 each, supper 8 + 3, wedding 12 + 4, gala 20 + 6 (copper), then a hot meal.
*comment
*comment Rules this file follows: roll first, then the page's one time advance, so a cast-ahead Guidance (one minute) still counts. A refresh replays a page from its start on
*comment top of that page's END state, so a page must not read a value it changes itself: each set's number is a constant assigned at the top of its own page
*comment (rj_perf_set1..3), and each result is stored by assignment (rj_perf_good1..3). The hints show the ability but never the DC, so the room's taste cannot be read
*comment off a number.
*comment
*comment Wired in: wall posting and signing in port_valen_civic_heights.txt (pv_ch_roll_read, pv_ch_perf_sign); reached through the posting hub (radiant_hub.txt rh_hub) from the
*comment Trunk Road, the Harbor Quayside or the Patrician Avenue, open at Dusk only; compile.js lists the file. Not in *scene_list.
*comment ====================================================================================

*comment ── the room: the crowd, the mood, and a clue to what it wants (tastes shift across the night) ──
*label rj_perf_room
*if (rj_perf_venue = 1)
  *if (rj_perf_cur = 1)
    Carters stamp the cold off their boots and crowd the benches, calling for the next round before the last has landed. Somebody is already banging a cup on the board in time to nothing.
  *elseif (rj_perf_cur = 2)
    The ale has done its work. A drover is telling a long story to a friend who is not listening, and the whole table is laughing at the wrong parts.
  *else
    The fire has burned down to coals. The carters who are left sit with their chins on their fists, looking at nothing, and the hall is quiet enough to hear the wheel creak outside.
*elseif (rj_perf_venue = 2)
  *if (rj_perf_cur = 1)
    A porter at the bar is telling a joke that has been told before, and the whole bench knows the end of it and is waiting to be let in on it again.
  *elseif (rj_perf_cur = 2)
    A boat crew has come in off a long shift, stamping and shouting, wanting noise to match their own.
  *else
    The room is thinning, and the ones who are left have ale left in their cups and a grin for anything with a punch in it.
*elseif (rj_perf_venue = 3)
  *if (rj_perf_cur = 1)
    A dozen guests are settling at the long table, talking low and politely over the soup. ${name_first} glances at the window, and wants something to warm the room without breaking it.
  *elseif (rj_perf_cur = 2)
    The wine has loosened collars. A man at the far end tells a story that is a little too sharp, and the table pretends not to hear and then laughs.
  *else
    The candles have burned down to stubs. An old couple at the end of the table sit with their hands together, and nobody is saying very much.
*elseif (rj_perf_venue = 4)
  *if (rj_perf_cur = 1)
    The couple are at the head of the table with their glasses raised, and the speeches have started. Someone's mother is already weeping into a napkin.
  *elseif (rj_perf_cur = 2)
    The benches have been pushed back and the floor is bare, and half the room is already on its feet and looking at you.
  *else
    The dancing has spent itself. The couple sit together at the end of the table, tired and glowing, and an old aunt asks, quietly, whether you know anything from home.
*else
  *if (rj_perf_cur = 1)
    The salon is all silk and low talk, and a long table of silver. Nobody is eating much. The room is waiting to be moved, and would rather not be told so.
  *elseif (rj_perf_cur = 2)
    The glasses are being refilled between courses, and a man near the middle is holding a wine-stem like a pointer, hungry for something clever.
  *else
    The candles are down by a third and the talk has gone soft and private. Guests lean toward each other, and the quiet is the kind that is waiting for a particular sort of song.
*return

*comment ── the song resolver: the room's taste sets the DC, then roll first and advance the clock after ──
*label rj_perf_play
*gosub rj_perf_taste
*set rj_perf_dc 12
*if (rj_perf_song = rj_perf_pref)
  *set rj_perf_dc 10
  *set rj_perf_fit 1

  *set rj_perf_dc 14
  *set rj_perf_fit 3
*else

  *set rj_perf_dc + 2
*set check_task_type "perform"
*gosub_scene dice_engine roll_skill_check rj_perf_dc "performance" "" "" "" "" ""
*comment The engine clears the task type itself, but skips that when a page replays, so clear it here and both runs end the same.
*set check_task_type "none"
*set rj_perf_ok check_success
*set minutes_to_pass 60
*set time_advance_call_id ("rj_perf_set_" & rj_perf_cur)
*gosub rj_perf_time
*gosub rj_perf_mark
*temp crowd "the room"
*if (rj_perf_venue = 1)
  *set crowd "the carters"
*elseif (rj_perf_venue = 2)
  *set crowd "the porters"
*elseif (rj_perf_venue = 3)
  *set crowd "the guests"
*elseif (rj_perf_venue = 4)
  *set crowd "the wedding guests"
*if (rj_perf_song = 1)
  *if (check_success)
    You hit the first chord hard and the whole room lifts with it. The chorus comes round and ${crowd} are on it, banging and stamping, shouting the line back before you can sing it.
  *else
    You come in too hard and a string buzzes. The chorus falls apart in the middle, and ${crowd} pick it up wrong and drop it again.
*elseif (rj_perf_song = 2)
  *if (check_success)
    You bring it down to a murmur and let the lute carry the line. By the second verse nobody is talking, and by the third nobody is breathing loudly.
  *else
    You mean it, and it shows in the first line and nowhere after. A cough spreads along the room, and the second verse goes to nobody.
*else
  *if (check_success)
    You start the story straight-faced, a man and a mule and a wager, and by the third verse ${crowd} can see where it is going and are begging you not to.
  *else
    The joke is a verse too long, and the punchline lands on a silence. Somebody at the back laughs a beat after the rest, alone.
*if (rj_perf_fit = 1)
  *if (check_success)
    It is exactly what the room wanted.
  *else
    It was the right song, and you dropped it.
*elseif (rj_perf_fit = 3)
  *if (check_success)
    It is not what they wanted, and you carried them anyway.
  *else
    It is not what they wanted, and it shows.

*if (check_success)
  [b][🎵 The set lands: a tip in the cup][/b]
*else
  [b][🎵 The set falls flat: no tip][/b]

*elseif (good = 0)
