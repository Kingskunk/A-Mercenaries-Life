*comment ====================================================================================
*comment THE POSTING HUB: the one place that lists the Hiring Hall jobs the player has signed and sends them to the right job file.
*comment
*comment Reached from a single "Your posting" button on the Trunk Road (Middle Ward) and on the Patrician Avenue (Quarter), each of
*comment which first calls rh_check so the button only shows while a job is open. The entry sets rh_from ("road" or "avenue") so the
*comment way back, and the wording when a job's house is in the other district, follow where the player stood.
*comment
*comment To add a job: put its "open" test in rh_check, and one block of buttons in rh_hub (a direct button when the player is in the right
*comment district, otherwise the travel line), routing with *goto_scene to that job's own first label. The job file keeps its own
*comment stage and clock; this file only reads them.
*comment
*comment Not in *scene_list: compile.js lists it with verifyFileName.
*comment ====================================================================================

*comment Sets rj_has_job: any signed job still open.
*comment Every signed job has a term. When it runs out the job undoes itself: no penalty, nothing to report, and it simply is not there any more
*comment (the board's pool puts a fresh posting up in its own time). Each job's lapse goes here, one line each.
*label rh_check
*if ((rj_pest_stage = "active") and (campaign_day > rj_pest_until))
  *set rj_pest_stage "none"
*if ((rj_haul_stage = "active") and (campaign_day > rj_haul_until))
  *set rj_haul_stage "none"
*set rj_has_job false
*if ((rj_pest_stage = "active") and (campaign_day <= rj_pest_until))
  *set rj_has_job true
*if ((rj_haul_stage = "active") and (campaign_day <= rj_haul_until))
  *set rj_has_job true
*return

*label rh_hub
*gosub rh_check
*if (not(rj_has_job))
  *comment Nothing open (the job closed or lapsed behind the player): back to where they stood.
  *goto rh_back
[b]◈ YOUR POSTING[/b]
*line_break
You hold the insignia flat in your palm, and the terms you signed for run across its face in thin lines of light.

*temp rh_here "middle_ward"
*if (rh_from = "avenue")
  *set rh_here "quarter"

*comment A job whose house is in the other district sends the player to the travel menu; one travel button covers all of them.
*temp rh_far false

*if ((rj_pest_stage = "active") and (campaign_day <= rj_pest_until))
  *temp rh_days ((rj_pest_until - campaign_day) + 1)
  [b]Vermin in the Walls[/b]: ${rj_pest_place}. Four Silver Marks, paid at the door. Open @{(rh_days = 1) one more day|${rh_days} more days}.
  *line_break
  *if (not(rj_pest_where = rh_here))
    *set rh_far true
*if ((rj_haul_stage = "active") and (campaign_day <= rj_haul_until))
  *temp rh_hdays ((rj_haul_until - campaign_day) + 1)
  [b]A House to Be Moved[/b]: ${rj_haul_place}. A copper bit an hour, a little over for each piece delivered whole, and a meal. Open @{(rh_hdays = 1) one more day|${rh_hdays} more days}.
  *if (not(rh_here = "middle_ward"))
    *set rh_far true

*choice
  *if (((rj_pest_stage = "active") and (campaign_day <= rj_pest_until)) and (rj_pest_where = rh_here))
    # [b]The house[/b]: ${rj_pest_place}. [~10 min]
      *goto_scene port_valen/radiant/hall_pests rj_pest_arrive
  *if (((rj_haul_stage = "active") and (campaign_day <= rj_haul_until)) and (rh_here = "middle_ward"))
    # [b]The move[/b]: ${rj_haul_place}. [~10 min]
      *goto_scene port_valen/radiant/hall_haul rj_haul_arrive
  *if (rh_far)
    # [Travel: Head to the district the house is in]
      *goto_scene port_valen/port_valen port_valen_travel
  # Put the insignia away.
    *goto rh_back

*label rh_back
*if (rh_from = "avenue")
  *goto_scene port_valen/port_valen_upper_wharves pq_avenue
*goto_scene port_valen/port_valen_middle_ward mw_gate_road
