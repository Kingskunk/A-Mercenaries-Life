*comment ====================================================================================
*comment A HOUSE TO BE MOVED: the second radiant Hiring Hall job (Gilded Scales page). Hauling, Middle Ward only.
*comment
*comment The patron is a generated person (names.txt), the house is one of four, and three loads have to cross the lane to a handcart: a heavy one, an
*comment awkward one, a fragile one. Each load can be taken three ways (muscle, plan, finesse) with one check each. The loads can be done in any order
*comment and the player can leave between them; the job stays open until its term runs out (two days) and then undoes itself.
*comment
*comment Pay is by the hour worked, one copper bit an hour, plus two copper for each load delivered whole, plus a hot meal on the patron's back step. A failed
*comment load breaks something and earns no bonus; it never fails the job. Muscle takes one hour, plan two, finesse three, so faster work earns less and tires less.
*comment
*comment Rules this file follows: roll first, then the page's one time advance, so a cast-ahead Guidance (one minute) still counts; each load's result is
*comment stored by plain assignment (rj_haul_h1..3 hours, rj_haul_b1..3 the whole-load bonus), never +N, so a replay cannot double anything; the pay page and the meal page
*comment are separate pages because buy_tavern_item and the pay share the currency lock.
*comment
*comment Wired in: wall posting and signing in port_valen_civic_heights.txt (pv_ch_roll_read, pv_ch_haul_sign); reached through the posting hub
*comment (radiant_hub.txt rh_hub); compile.js lists the file. Not in *scene_list.
*comment
*comment Numbers (all tunable): DC 10 for the approach that suits the load, 12 for the middle one, 14 for the worst; 1 copper an hour; 2 copper bonus for each load delivered whole.
*comment ====================================================================================
