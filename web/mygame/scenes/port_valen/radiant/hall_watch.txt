*comment ====================================================================================
*comment THE NIGHT WATCH: the third radiant Hiring Hall job (Gilded Scales page). A night's watch over a Cargo Quay warehouse.
*comment
*comment The patron is a generated person (names.txt, docks mix). The watch signs on at dusk at the warehouse door and runs about nine hours in three stretches
*comment of three hours. Each stretch draws one event from a seeded table, and MOST of the table is a quiet night or a false alarm; only two events are real
*comment (a fire, a sneak thief) and only the thief is a fight. Phase 2, not built: the City Watch patrol with a warrant (its share of the table, rolls 90 to 99,
*comment plays as a quiet stretch until then).
*comment
*comment Pay is a copper bit for every hour of the watch (nine) plus two copper for each stretch held cleanly. A stretch that goes wrong loses its two copper bonus
*comment and some goods; it never fails the job and never takes the hourly pay away.
*comment
*comment Rules this file follows: roll first, then the page's one time advance, so a cast-ahead Guidance (one minute) still counts. A refresh replays a page from its start on
*comment top of that page's END state, so a page must not read a value it changes itself: each stretch's number is assigned as a constant at the top of its own page
*comment (rj_watch_stretch1..3), never counted up or derived from what the page marks, and each result is stored by assignment (rj_watch_held1..3). The event of each
*comment stretch is a seeded roll (a salt per stretch), so the same night always plays the same.
*comment
*comment Wired in: wall posting and signing in port_valen_civic_heights.txt (pv_ch_roll_read, pv_ch_watch_sign); reached through the posting hub (radiant_hub.txt rh_hub)
*comment from the Harbor Quayside, open at Dusk only; compile.js lists the file. Not in *scene_list.
*comment
*comment Event ids (rj_watch_ev): 0 quiet round, 1 shutter, 2 feral cat, 3 drunk or lost sailor, 4 fire, 5 sneak thief. Table of a 0-99 roll: 0-39 quiet, 40-49 shutter,
*comment 50-61 cat, 62-74 drunk, 75-82 fire, 83-89 thief, 90-99 reserved for the patrol (quiet for now).
*comment ====================================================================================
