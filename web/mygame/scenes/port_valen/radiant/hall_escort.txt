*comment THE ROAD (second piece, now driven by the route). A travel contract played as one chain of pages. The caravan sets the pace (es_pace, 30 minutes a mile) and the camp, so this is NOT the free
*comment overworld map and the player cannot wander off. The line walks the legs of Overworld.route(rj_escort_from, rj_escort_to) (overworld-data.js), so the same pages run in either direction and over
*comment any number of tiles. Nothing here names a tile: the text of the road comes from the tile fields walk, camp, depart and arrive.
*comment
*comment The day: the line rolls at 09:00 and walks leg by leg. After each leg it decides, from the clock, whether the next leg still ends by 20:00 (es_act / es_act2: "pay" at the last leg, "beat" for a halt
*comment with an event on a road, forest or hills tile, "go" for the next leg, "camp" to stop here). The camp is a night with supper, one three-hour watch for the player chosen by their place in the line
*comment (head 21:00, middle 00:00, rear 03:00), breakfast, and a 07:00 start. On the forward run (Port Valen to Timbermouth) that is: noon halt on the River Road, camp at Ashrun Mouth, Longshade Edge,

*comment quarters of a copper a mile (3 silver marks for 40 miles); some lost, but not all = a third of that; every beat lost = nothing. A beat that goes wrong never ends the job. An event is a seeded roll from
*comment the signing block (rj_escort_block + 5000, salt 60 + the beat number), so the same line always meets the same road.

*comment Replay rules (as hall_watch.txt): roll first, then the page's one time advance. A refresh replays a page on top of its END state, so (1) the leg a page walks is stored under a page lock
