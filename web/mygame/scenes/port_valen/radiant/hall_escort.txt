*comment ════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
*comment THE ROAD (second piece, now driven by the route and played in STRETCHES). A travel contract played as a short chain of pages. The caravan sets the pace (es_pace, 30 minutes a mile) and the camp, so this is NOT
*comment the free overworld map and the player cannot wander off. The caravan walks the legs of Overworld.route(rj_escort_from, rj_escort_to) (overworld-data.js), so the same pages run in either direction and over
*comment any route of up to Overworld.convoyMaxLegs (7) legs, about four days. Nothing here names a tile: the text of the road comes from the tile fields walk, camp, depart and arrive.
*comment
*comment A STRETCH is one page: the walk from where the caravan stands to its next stop, with the quiet legs in between folded into one summary line. The planning is a pure function (Overworld.convoyPlan, overworld-data.js):
*comment it walks legs until the next leg would end after 20:00 (a CAMP), a tile that meets a HALT EVENT, or the end of the road (PAY). A halt on a road, forest or hills tile meets an event with a chance set by the tile's
*comment danger (25, 50, 75 or 100 percent), at most one a day; which event comes from a table in the data file (HALT_EVENTS: kinds, weight by danger). The camp is one page for a quiet night (supper, a quiet watch, the morning)
*comment and a few pages for a disturbed one (the oxen spook, a sneak thief). The player stands ONE watch, chosen by their place in the line (head 21:00, middle 00:00, rear 03:00).
*comment
*comment BEATS are the halts and the nights, counted in order (rj_escort_cur). Goods lost are counted (rj_escort_lost), not slotted, so a road of any length works. Pay is Overworld.convoyFee: three quarters of a copper bit
*comment a mile if nothing is lost, the share of beats that held (never under a third) if some were, nothing if all were. A beat that goes wrong never ends the job. Events are seeded from the signing block
*comment (rj_escort_block + 5000), so the same caravan always meets the same road. Not every roll is a fight: a failed roll costs goods, time or both, and only a sneak thief at night (and later the armed bandits) can lead to blows.
*comment
*comment Replay rules (as hall_watch.txt): roll first, then the page's one time advance. A refresh replays a page on top of its END state, so (1) a stretch's plan and walk are done once under a page lock
*comment (es_leg_locked) and stored; (2) every page reads the decision about what follows (es_act, es_act2) before its own page_break and never after; (3) a beat is numbered and a loss counted under page locks.
