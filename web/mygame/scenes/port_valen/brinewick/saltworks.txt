[b]◈ THE SLIP MARKET[/b]
*line_break
The salt store's loading doors open onto the slip, and the slip is where Brinewick does its trading. Sacked salt stands in bays along the quay, stacked to a man's height and chalked with the day's weight, and wagons back up between the bays with their tailboards down. Fish racks line the seaward rail, and a tally-man with a wax tablet walks the quay beside a beam-scale. Behind the store the work path runs out between shallow beds of brine, walled with packed earth, to pan sheds open at the roof ridge to let their steam out. A packing rack under a long lean-to holds open sacks, wooden scoops and broad shovels stained white at the edges.

*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain rings on the shed roofs and breaks the smooth surface of every open bed.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Steam and fog mingle around the pan sheds, making the far beds fade into a pale blur.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow gathers on the low bed walls and turns to water where the sheds breathe heat.
*elseif ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  A few furnace windows glow beneath the pan sheds, and the bays stand bare under their tarpaulins with the salt store's doors shut above the black mud.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The work path remains soft at the edges, where boots and cart wheels have pressed it down beside the beds.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  A dry skin has formed on the path, but water still glints in every low print.

*if (bw_salt_first)
  Everything here is counted by the sack and the cartload, and the white dust of the pans lies on sleeves, boots and every rail top.
*elseif ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  The trading has ended for the day, though heat and steam still rise from the sheds.
*else
  The slip goes on in its measured way, with salt, fish and inland freight each moving through its own bay.
