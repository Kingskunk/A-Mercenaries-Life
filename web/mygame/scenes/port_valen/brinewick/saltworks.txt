[b]◈ THE SALTWORKS[/b]
*line_break
The work path runs between shallow beds of brine, each walled with packed earth and linked by narrow channels. Wooden gates divide one bed from the next. Beyond them stand the pan sheds, open at the roof ridge to let steam out, and the raised salt store with its loading doors facing the slip. A packing rack under a long lean-to holds open sacks, wooden scoops and broad shovels stained white at the edges.

*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain rings on the shed roofs and breaks the smooth surface of every open bed.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Steam and fog mingle around the pan sheds, making the far beds fade into a pale blur.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow gathers on the low bed walls and turns to water where the sheds breathe heat.
*elseif ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  A few furnace windows glow beneath the pan sheds, and the salt store's loading doors are shut above the black mud.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The work path remains soft at the edges, where boots and cart wheels have pressed it down beside the beds.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  A dry skin has formed on the path, but water still glints in every low print.

*if (bw_salt_first)
  The place is full of simple movements repeated carefully: brine guided from bed to bed, salt drawn from the pans, sacks filled and carried toward the raised store. The work leaves white dust on sleeves, boots and the rail tops.
*elseif ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  The open work has ended for the moment, though heat and steam still rise from the sheds.
*else
  The saltworks continues in measured stages, with water, heat, sacks and carts each moving through their own part of the ground.
