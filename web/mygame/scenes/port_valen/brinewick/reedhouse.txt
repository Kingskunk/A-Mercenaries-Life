The Reedhouse is a low inn with a stone lower wall and dark timber above, its roof weighted with bundles of reed against the wind. A wide hearth faces scrubbed plank tables and a short counter. An arch at the back opens on a raised yard with hitching rails, a covered trough and three stable stalls beneath a hay loft. The room smells of peat smoke, fish broth, damp wool and the clean sharpness of salt carried in from the flats.

[b]◈ YOUR ROOM: THE REEDHOUSE[/b]
*line_break
The room beneath the roof holds a plank bed, straw mattress, folded wool blanket, and a tin basin beside a jug of water. A shuttered window looks across the stable yard to the bank and the reeds beyond it. The room is plain and dry, with the smell of peat smoke caught in the boards.

*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  The stable-yard lamp shows as a narrow line beneath the shutter, and water makes a soft, uneven sound beyond the bank.
*elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
  Last light lies along the stable roof while the reeds beyond it turn black.
*else
  Daylight comes through the shutter gaps and falls across the foot of the bed.

*if (weather_severity >= 3)
  Wind worries the shutter and rattles the roof reeds overhead.
*elseif (weather = "Rain")
  Rain taps steadily above the bed and splashes in the yard below.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Cold clings to the window wall, though the room itself stays dry.
