*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ──
*label bw_place
Brinewick is built along the inside of an earthen bank, with the road on its higher side and the tidal flats spread out beyond the other. The Tide House stands on stout stone feet near the gate: a square building with a bell frame, narrow windows and tide marks cut into the post beside its stair. Below it, narrow paths run between low cottages, peat sheds and fenced yards where nets, barrels and oilskins dry on rails.

The saltworks lie on the flat side of the bank. Shallow square beds hold brine behind low walls, linked by straight channels and wooden gates. A line of dark pan sheds stands beyond them, each with a squat chimney, and the salt store rises behind on a base of stone with its loading doors above the mud. A narrow slip runs toward the tidal channel, where a small boat can come in when the water is high enough.

The Reedhouse stands near the road gate, its lower wall of stone and its upper walls of dark timber. Behind it, a small stable yard and hay shed stand on the highest ground in the village. The air carries peat smoke, wet reeds, salt and the sharp smell of boiling brine.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ──
*label bw_sky
*temp bw_dark false
*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *set bw_dark true
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain stipples the brine beds and runs along the channels toward the tide gate.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  Wind drives water hard across the flats, and every loose shutter and hanging rail has been tied down.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Fog lies across the beds and channels, hiding the far reeds and leaving only the nearer chimneys visible.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow catches in the reed tops and turns the bank paths to grey slush.
*elseif (weather = "Blizzard")
  Snow races low over the flats and fills the channels until their straight lines disappear.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (bw_dark)
    The flat water in the nearest beds catches starlight, and the salt store stands black against the sky.
  *elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
    Low light catches the wet beds and the channel gates, while the reeds beyond darken into one line.
  *else
    Clear light lies hard on the open flats, bright on the salt beds and the water between them.
*else
  A flat overcast sits low above the reeds and drains colour from the water and mud.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The paths along the bank remain soft and the lower yard gates stand above shallow water.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The packed earth is drying from the edges inward, though the channels still shine full.
*return
