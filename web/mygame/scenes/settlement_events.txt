*comment ====================================================================================
*comment SETTLEMENT EVENTS: shared, low-frequency incidents for towns and villages.
*comment
*comment Callers set se_settlement_id, se_settlement_kind, se_wedding_style, and event opt-ins, then
*comment *gosub_scene settlement_events se_roll after real local movement. This scene always returns.
*comment IDs are local declarations, never names in a central list. The wedding runs in any registered
*comment settlement once; event-specific scenes select broad style, never individual settlement names.
*comment ====================================================================================

*label se_roll
*temp se_event_roll 0

*if (se_settlement_id <= 0)
  *goto se_return
*if (not(se_wedding_enabled))
  *goto se_return
*if (settlement_event_last_day = campaign_day)
  *goto se_return
*if (weather_severity >= 3)
  *goto se_return
*if (street_life = "empty")
  *goto se_return
*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *goto se_return

*if ({("settlement_wedding_done_" & se_settlement_id)})
  *goto se_return

*rand se_event_roll 1 100
*if (se_event_roll > 2)
  *goto se_return

*set settlement_event_last_day campaign_day
*gosub_scene settlement_events/caught_in_wedding wedding_run

*label se_return
*comment Opt-ins are per-call configuration, never persistent state inherited by the next settlement.
*set se_wedding_enabled false
*return
