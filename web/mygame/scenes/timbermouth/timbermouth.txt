*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Constant geography and smell: no weather, no time of day, no people.
*label tm_place
*if (tm_first_hub)
  A paved square opens where the road from the river comes up out of the lowlands and meets three smaller roads: one from the forest and two from the farm country. Timbermouth has no wall. At the mouth of each road stands a pair of squared ash gateposts with rusted hooks where gates once hung; a squat stone watch post sits beside the lowland road, while the other roads simply run between their posts.

  The Split Pine inn stands at the road end of the square, a long house of dark timber with a split pine trunk set upright beside its door and an arch through to its yard. Across the paving, the Factor's Hall is the only stone building: two floors high behind broad steps, with the Gilded Scales' river-serpent banner over its door. Wells with stone curbs and a spring-fed trough stand in the middle of the square.

  On the forest side, Longshade ash stands in long ranks under open sheds, each stack marked with a grade and year. Beyond them is the Sawmill, a long open-sided building on the Ashrun's bank, its wheel set in a water channel and its log yard spread behind. The Ashrun comes loud over its ledges, a stony stream too shallow for boats but wide enough to bring loose logs down in the spring melt. A lane of low, open-fronted Workshops runs back from the square beside the mill.

  On the farm side, the Market opens through a yard gate with a beam scale hung above it. A general store stands beside that gate, its front hung with rope, lanterns and harness beneath a board reading [i]"MARLOW. General goods."[/i] The paving bears deep wheel ruts and old hoof marks, with hitching rails along every frontage. Timber houses lean shoulder to shoulder along the roads, their upper floors jutting out over the street. Cut pine, resin, wet stone, hay, hot iron and glue mix in the air. A signboard at the square's mouth reads [i]"Timbermouth"[/i].
*else
  The square still ties the forest road and farm roads to the lowland river road. The Split Pine faces the Factor's Hall across the wells and trough, while the Sawmill and Workshops lie toward the Ashrun and the Market and general store toward the farm country. Resin, wet stone and hay stay in the air.
*return

*rand st_c 1 2
*if (st_a = 1)
  The square is deserted. The carts stand under tarpaulins against the shed walls, and the shutters are up along every street.
*else
  Nothing moves on the paving but the water running between the stones, and the saw frame stands still under its streaming shed.
*if (st_b = 1)
  A single figure in an oilskin cape crosses between two doors and does not stop.
*else
  Smoke is torn from the chimneys as it leaves them, and a loose shutter bangs somewhere out of sight.
*if (st_c = 1)
  The watch have drawn into the post and shut its door, with a thread of smoke at its chimney.
*else
  A wagon horse stamps and blows in the Pine's stalls, and the sound carries across the square.
*return

*comment ── town life (slot B), shared by the working day and the market days: residents, not work ──
*label tm_st_life
*if (st_b = 1)
  Women with wooden pails wait their turn at the well, talking low, while a small boy tries to peer down the shaft until someone hauls him back by the collar.
*elseif (st_b = 2)
  A baker's boy cuts across the square with a tray of rolls balanced on his head, steering by the smell.
*elseif (st_b = 3)
  Children chase a hoop between the stacks until a man in a leather apron shouts them off.
*elseif (st_b = 4)
  A dog sleeps in the sun at the mouth of the Workshops' lane, and a hen picks her way across the paving as if she owned it.
*elseif (st_b = 5)
  A woman beats a rug over an upper sill, and a cloud of fine pale dust drifts down onto the street.
*elseif (st_b = 6)
  An old man sits on an upturned barrel beside the Market's gate, whittling a peg and watching everyone who passes.
*elseif (st_b = 7)
  A peddler with a tray of ribbons, needles and horn buttons works along the edge of the square, calling softly.
*else
  Two women argue about the price of eggs at the back of a farm cart, and neither is getting what she wants.
*return

*comment ── the watch and the draught animals (slot C), shared by the working day and the market days ──
*label tm_st_watch
*if (st_c = 1)
  Two of the watch, in grey coats with the river-serpent badge stitched on their sleeves, walk the square at an even pace, looking at everything.
*elseif (st_c = 2)
  One of the watch leans on the post at the road end with his staff, watching the wagons come and go.
*elseif (st_c = 3)
  Oxen stand yoked at the rails, chewing and patient, with a drover asleep on the shaft of the nearest cart.
*elseif (st_c = 4)
  A string of pack mules with empty panniers is led across the square by a boy with a switch.
*elseif (st_c = 5)
  A saddled horse stands at the Split Pine's rail with a traveller's pack hanging from the saddle horn.
*else
  One of the watch stops a carter whose wagon sits low on its axle, and walks slowly down its length counting sacks.
*return

*comment ── THE HOURS: the one place that decides what is open. Sets the tm_ok_* flags; every closure page calls it again, so they read the clock rather than a stale hub temp. ──
*label tm_hours
*comment Morning through Afternoon is the working day for the Workshops and the Factor's Hall; the Market (open air) and the General Store trade through Dusk. Hallowday is the rest day. The
*comment Market also closes at street_life "empty".
*set tm_ok_market false
*set tm_ok_store false
*set tm_ok_works false
*set tm_ok_hall false
*if (not(day_of_week = "Hallowday"))
  *if (((time_period = "Morning") or (time_period = "Midday")) or (time_period = "Afternoon"))
    *set tm_ok_works true
    *set tm_ok_hall true
  *if ((((time_period = "Morning") or (time_period = "Midday")) or (time_period = "Afternoon")) or (time_period = "Dusk"))
    *set tm_ok_store true
    *if (not(street_life = "empty"))
      *set tm_ok_market true
*comment The wagon lot is part of the Market (it follows the Market hours). The hub shows a shortcut to it only while a line to Port Valen is making up (its day of the week, which the prose never names) or the
*comment player holds a job that sets out from here, so nobody has to go through the Market page.
*set tm_lot_show false
*if (((rj_escort_stage = "active") and (rj_escort_from = "tm")) and (campaign_day <= rj_escort_until))
  *set tm_lot_show true
*if ((not(rj_escort_stage = "active")) and (day_of_week = "Tideday"))
  *set tm_lot_show true
*return

*comment ── CLOSURE PAGES: each costs no time, sets nothing, and returns here. They read the clock and the derived flags directly, so they play from any label. ──
*label tm_closed_market
*set tm_which "market"
*goto tm_closed

*label tm_closed_store
*set tm_which "store"
*goto tm_closed

*label tm_closed_works
*set tm_which "works"
*goto tm_closed

*label tm_closed_hall
*set tm_which "hall"
*goto tm_closed

*label tm_closed
*gosub_scene calendar update_hub_condition
*if (tm_which = "market")
  [b]◈ THE MARKET[/b]
*elseif (tm_which = "store")
  [b]◈ THE GENERAL STORE[/b]
*elseif (tm_which = "works")
  [b]◈ THE WOODWORKERS' WORKSHOPS[/b]
*else
  [b]◈ THE FACTOR'S HALL[/b]
*line_break
*if (day_of_week = "Hallowday")
  *if (tm_which = "market")
    The yard gates are chained, the grading boards turned to the wall, and the stalls folded with their awnings rolled up. A paper notice beneath a wooden hood beside the gate says that the market keeps the rest day.
  *elseif (tm_which = "store")
    The shutters are up and barred, and a card in the window says that the store keeps the rest day. The hooks outside have been taken in.
  *elseif (tm_which = "works")
    The workshop doors are shut and the benches inside are covered. Nobody works on the rest day.
  *else
    The doors of the hall are shut and the shutters closed. A paper notice beneath a wooden hood by the door says that it keeps the rest day.
*elseif ((street_life = "empty") and (tm_which = "market"))
  The weather has cleared the ground. Tarpaulins are lashed down hard over the stacks, the stalls are folded away, and the yard gate is shut and barred.
*elseif ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *if (tm_which = "market")
    The yard gate is chained for the night and the stalls are gone, leaving bare ground where loose straw turns in the wind. A lantern burns on a post beside the bays, and nothing else moves.
  *elseif (tm_which = "store")
    The shutters are up and a bar lies across the door. The coils and lanterns have been taken in, and only the empty hooks show beneath the board.
  *elseif (tm_which = "works")
    The workshops are barred and dark, and the smell of glue and fresh shavings hangs in the street outside.
  *else
    The doors are shut and a lantern burns at the foot of the steps. The windows are dark.
*else
  *comment Dusk: the workday is over for the two working places (the Market and the General Store trade through Dusk, so neither gets this page).
  *if (tm_which = "works")
    The benches are cleared and the doors pulled shut for the day, and the shavings are swept into heaps by the walls.
  *else
    The doors of the hall have been closed for the day, and the counter windows are shuttered.
*page_break Back to the square…
*goto timbermouth
