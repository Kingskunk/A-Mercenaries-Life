[b]◈ THE LANDING[/b]
*line_break
A trodden path runs down from the clearing to a long flat shelf of gravel on the stream's near bank, and the shelf is stacked with trunks from end to end: decks of bark-stripped logs laid in careful rows, ten and twelve high, each on a pair of skids, with their cut ends turned toward the water. Every end-grain carries a mark in paint, a circle, a double bar, a triangle, a cross in a ring, and each deck holds one mark. Beyond the decks the Ashrun runs loud over a series of grey stone ledges, narrow and fast and shallow, and a wooden chute of squared planks leads down toward its head. The air is cold and wet and smells of sap, bark and river stones.

*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain pocks the stream and runs down the skins of the stacked logs in dark streaks.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  The stream is brown and loud, and spray from the ledges flies across the gravel.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Mist lies on the water, and the far end of the decks fades to grey.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow clings to the logs and melts into the gravel, and the ledges are slick with ice.

*comment ── WEATHER MEMORY (calendar ground_state, strictly no people) ──
*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  After days of rain the Ashrun runs high and brown, and the lowest deck stands in a shallow of standing water.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The gravel is dark and the skids are slick from the last rain.

*if (lnd_roll = 1)
  A pair of men walk the length of a deck with long pikes, prodding the lowest logs and calling a number to a boy writing on a sheet of paper.
*elseif (lnd_roll = 2)
  A team of oxen drags a fresh trunk down the path on skids, and a woman with a paint-pot waits beside the deck for it, brush in hand.
*elseif (lnd_roll = 3)
  A half-elf man crouches at the ledges, watching the water run through the chute and reading the stream the way another man reads a face.
*else
  Two children skip stones down the shallows, and a heron stands on the far bank and ignores them.

*choice
  # Look over the decks.
    The decks are built with great care. Each log lies in its place with its ends square and its bark stripped, and every deck is wedged at both ends with a single chock cut to fit. The marks are painted fresh, the paint still bright on the newest logs and weathered grey on the oldest, and several decks have more than one coat, as though a mark had been repainted year after year.
    *page_break Step back from the logs…

  # Watch the stream run through the ledges.
    The Ashrun comes down through the stones in a series of short steps, each one a low white fall, and the water between them runs black and deep. A line of pegs has been driven along one bank, with a post at each of the falls and a rope wound on it. The stream is narrow enough to leap at its narrowest point and too shallow, over the ledges, for anything but a log to float.
    *page_break Turn back from the water…
