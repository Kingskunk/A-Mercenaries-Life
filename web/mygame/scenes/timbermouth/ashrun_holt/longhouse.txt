[b]◈ THE LONGHOUSE[/b]
*line_break
The Longhouse is a single hall of squared logs under a steep bark roof, with a deep porch along its front and a stone chimney at one end. The great ash stands a few paces from the porch, and its trunk is wound with ribbons, strips of cloth, knotted cords and little carved tokens hung from nails, faded and new, thick as a second bark. Inside the hall a long hearth runs down the middle between benches, a loom stands at one end under a window, and a board of pegs on the back wall holds a knot of cords at each, each tied differently and each hung where it belongs. The beams are black with smoke, and the air smells of resin, sheep fat and wet wool.

*if (weather_severity >= 3)
  The door has been dragged shut and barred, and the whole hall hums with the wind in the chimney. The hearth has been built up high, and the benches along both walls are full of people waiting out the weather with their work in their laps.
*elseif (weather = "Rain")
  Rain drums on the bark roof and runs from the eaves in a curtain along the porch, and a fire has been lit on the hearth.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Snow lies on the porch and the bark roof, and the hearth burns bright against the cold.

*label ah_ash
[b]◈ THE OLD ASH[/b]
*line_break
The tree is wider than the Longhouse porch is long, with a trunk that ten people could not circle, its bark furrowed deep enough to hide a hand and its lowest boughs thicker than the trunks of the ash in the woodpiles. Ribbons and cords flutter from every reachable branch, and the ground beneath it is bare earth, trodden hard, with a flat stone at its foot where small offerings lie: a whittled bird, a heel of bread, a cluster of acorns, a bent nail. No saw has ever touched it. The scars of an old fire show black at the base on one side, healed over now in a thick roll of bark.
*if (weather_severity >= 3)

*elseif (weather_wet)
  Rain drips off the ribbons in a steady patter, and the offerings on the stone are dark with wet.
