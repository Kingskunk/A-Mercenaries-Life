[b]◈ THE WORK YARDS[/b]
*line_break
The work track divides beyond the last houses. Downhill, the Log Yard spreads along a gravel shelf beside the Ashrun: bark-stripped trunks lie on skids in marked decks, their painted ends turned toward the water and its narrow grey ledges. Downwind, the Charcoal Ground is a black clearing of turf-covered mounds, cordwood stacks and thin blue smoke. The air holds wet bark, river stone, tar and ash.

*if (not(ah_work_open))
  *if (weather_severity >= 3)
    The work has stopped for the weather. The logs are chocked hard on their skids, and wet sacks lie over the charcoal mounds while everyone waits under cover.

*label ah_log_yard_shift
*comment Roll before the shift passes: Guidance and other short-lived check effects apply to the work.
*gosub_scene dice_engine roll_skill_check 11 "athletics" "" "" "" "" ""
*set hours_to_pass 4
*set minutes_to_pass 0
*set time_advance_call_id "ah_log_yard_shift"
*gosub_scene calendar advance_time
*gosub_scene calendar recalc_hp_from_neglect
*if (hp_current <= 0)
  *set death_cause "starvation"
  *if (neglect_damage_fatigue > neglect_damage_hunger)
    *set death_cause "exhaustion"
  *goto_scene death death_screen

*if (check_success)
  You take the hauling line with the ox team and keep each trunk moving cleanly from skid to deck. By midday the tally post has a neat new column of marks, and the yard caller waves you over without needing to ask who did the work.
  *set ah_work_pay 3
  *set ah_work_outcome "A LOG YARD SHIFT"
*else
  You spend the shift heaving wet timber into place while the line bites through your gloves. The work gets done, but your shoulders are shaking by the last deck, and the yard caller pays you as an untrained hand.
  *set ah_work_pay 2
  *set ah_work_outcome "A LOG YARD SHIFT"
  *goto ah_work_fatigue
*goto ah_work_resolve

*comment Roll before the shift passes: Guidance and other short-lived check effects apply to the work.

*if (check_success)
  You read the smoke as it comes through the vents and open or close each hole before the mound can turn hot or starve. The charcoal maker gives one approving nod when the burn settles into an even blue thread.
  *set ah_work_pay 3
  *set ah_work_outcome "A CHARCOAL SHIFT"
*else
  You mistake the colour of the smoke and spend the rest of the shift carrying wet earth, hauling cordwood, and fixing what the experienced hands catch before it goes wrong. By the end, everything you own smells of tar and soot.
  *set ah_work_pay 2
  *set ah_work_outcome "A CHARCOAL SHIFT"
  *goto ah_work_fatigue
*goto ah_work_resolve
