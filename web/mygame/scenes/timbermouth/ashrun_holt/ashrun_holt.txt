*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Constant geography and smell: no weather, time of day or people.
*label ah_place
The trees open around a clearing where the Ashrun runs over grey stones. An old ash grows in its middle, broad enough that its lowest limbs reach over the beaten ground. Strips of cloth and small knotted cords hang from the boughs.

Log houses stand apart around the clearing, roofed in peeled bark. There is no street: hard paths run between doorsteps, chopping blocks, woodpiles, and the stream. The Longhouse stands on the east side beneath the old ash, with a deep porch facing the clearing. Downstream, the bank has been cut into rough decks where logs are hauled from the water and stacked.

Wet bark, green needles, woodsmoke, and charcoal fill the air beneath the trees.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, matched to time_period; no people. The canopy softens it: rain arrives late and drips on after it stops. Closes with the ground's memory of recent weather.
*label ah_sky
*temp ah_dark false
*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *set ah_dark true
*if (weather = "Rain")
  The rain reaches the clearing as a soft roar high in the canopy, and falls in heavy drops from the leaves long after it has stopped on the open ground.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  The whole forest roars over the clearing, the great boughs thrash against the sky, and branches fall somewhere in the dark under the trees.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Mist lies in the clearing and hangs among the trunks, so that the houses come and go and the old ash vanishes into its own top.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow falls softly through the canopy and lies in patches on the open ground and on the bark shingles.
*elseif (weather = "Blizzard")
  Snow drives across the clearing in a freezing gale, and the forest groans beyond the houses.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (ah_dark)
    Starlight comes down through the gap over the clearing, and the trunks beyond it are solid black.
  *elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
    The low sun comes in level under the boughs and turns the trunks to columns of copper.
  *else
    Shafts of light slant down through the canopy and lie in bright patches on the ground.
*else
  *comment Overcast
  *if (ah_dark)
    A heavy, starless night lies over the clearing, and the forest is a darker dark on every side.
  *else
    The light under the canopy is green and dim, and the clearing is the only bright place.
