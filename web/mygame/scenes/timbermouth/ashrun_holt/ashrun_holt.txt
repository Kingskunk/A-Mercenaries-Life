*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Constant geography and smell: no weather, time of day or people.
*label ah_place
The trees open around a clearing where the Ashrun runs over grey stones. An old ash grows in its middle, broad enough that its lowest limbs reach over the beaten ground. Strips of cloth and small knotted cords hang from the boughs.

Log houses stand apart around the clearing, roofed in peeled bark. There is no street: hard paths run between doorsteps, chopping blocks, woodpiles, and the stream. The ground between the old ash and the porches is beaten flat and wide, and it is where the village does its trading. The Longhouse stands on the east side beneath the old ash, with a deep porch facing the clearing. The Ashrun House has a broad porch and a row of stable rails facing the ground. One work track follows the Ashrun downhill toward the log decks; another runs downwind toward the smoke of the charcoal ground. A third track leads past a fenced hide yard, where pale hides hang on frames.

Wet bark, green needles, woodsmoke, charcoal, and the sharp smell of bark water fill the air beneath the trees.
*return
