[b]◈ THE ASHRUN HOUSE[/b]
*line_break
The Ashrun House is a broad, two-storey log building with a deep porch facing the clearing and stable rails along one side. Inside, a long hearth warms scrubbed tables beneath smoke-darkened beams. Wet wool, fir smoke, onion broth and the clean sharp smell of split wood fill the room.

*if (inn_first)
  A square-shouldered woman at the counter looks up from a pot at the hearth. "Food's hot, rooms are two copper, and the rails are outside. Keep your ${feet_prose} clear of the passage and we'll get on." 
*elseif (time_period = "Night")
  Lamps burn over the counter and stair, and the last few tables keep their voices low beneath the sound of the river.
*elseif (weather_severity >= 3)
  The shutters are barred and the hearth burns high. Somebody has hung soaked cloaks from the beam above it.
*elseif (time_period = "Morning")
  Cutters and haulers eat quickly before the work paths fill, their boots lined beneath the nearest bench.
*else
  The room has the steady traffic of a place where people come in for warmth, news and a meal before returning to the trees.

*line_break
The room has a plank bed, straw mattress, wool blanket, washbasin and a shuttered window looking over the clearing. It is plain, clean and warm enough that the forest sounds beyond the wall seem far away.
