[b]◈ THE HIDE YARD[/b]
*line_break
A hard path runs downwind from the last houses to a low fence of split rails. Inside it, hides are stretched pale on wooden frames and hung over rails to dry. Three wide troughs stand beneath a lean-to, their water dark with soaked bark. At the far side, a roofed cutting shed holds two scrubbed tables, hooks in the beams, and a drain cut through the packed earth. A small smokehouse stands beyond it, its door blackened around the edges. The place smells of wet hide, woodsmoke, bark, and fresh-cut meat.

*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain rattles on the cutting shed roof and makes dark trails down the hides on their frames.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  The hides have been taken from the outer frames, and the loose doors of the smokehouse knock in the wind.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Mist settles among the frames, and smoke from the little house drifts low across the yard.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow clings to the hide frames and melts into the packed earth around the troughs.
*elseif (weather = "Blizzard")
  The outer frames stand bare in the blowing snow, and the cutting shed door has been shut against the gale.

*comment ── WEATHER MEMORY (calendar ground_state, strictly no people) ──
*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  After days of rain, the yard is slick underfoot and the drain runs brown toward the trees.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The packed earth is still dark and soft around the troughs.

*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *goto ah_hide_yard_night
*if (time_period = "Dusk")
  *goto ah_hide_yard_dusk

*if (hy_roll = 1)
  A woman braces a deer hide against a low beam and scrapes it clean with a two-handled blade.
*elseif (hy_roll = 2)
  Two hunters carry a dressed deer from the cutting shed toward the smokehouse, one at each end of the pole beneath it.
*elseif (hy_roll = 3)
  A man lifts a hide from a trough with a forked stick, lets the dark water drain from it, and hangs it on an empty frame.
*else
  A girl threads thin strips of meat onto a rack by the smokehouse door, working quickly to keep the flies off.

*choice
  # Look over the hide frames.
    The hides are stretched tight with cords through holes along their edges. Deer hides make the largest pale sheets; smaller skins are pegged in pairs beside them. Some are fresh and dark, some have dried almost white, and the oldest have been rubbed smooth enough to fold without cracking.
    *page_break Step back from the frames…
    *goto ah_hide_yard_main
  # Look into the cutting shed.
    The shed floor is packed earth washed clean around the drain. A deer hangs from the central hook, already skinned and opened, while wrapped joints of meat sit on a shelf beneath a window. A pile of clean bones waits beside the door for the dogs or the pot.
    *page_break Step back from the shed…
    *goto ah_hide_yard_main
  # Look at the smokehouse.
    A low fire smoulders on the hearth inside, feeding thin smoke through racks of meat hung high under the roof. The air is warm and sharp enough to sting your eyes. Bundles of dried strips wait on a shelf by the door, tied for carrying.
    *page_break Step back from the smokehouse…
    *goto ah_hide_yard_main
  # Walk back to the clearing.
    *goto_scene timbermouth/ashrun_holt/ashrun_holt ashrun_holt

*label ah_hide_yard_dusk
The last light catches on the pale hides as the yard is put away. The cutting tables have been scrubbed, the shed door stands open to dry, and a worker carries the loose hides in from the outer frames. Thin smoke keeps slipping from the little house at the far side.

*choice
  # Look over the hide frames.
    The empty frames stand in a row with their cords coiled neatly at the feet. A few thick hides remain stretched beneath the lean-to, where the damp night air will not reach them so easily.
    *page_break Step back from the frames…
    *goto ah_hide_yard_main
  # Look into the cutting shed.
    The tables have been washed clean and the hooks stand bare above them. Knives hang in a locked rack by the back wall, and a bucket of bark water sits beneath the drain to catch the last of the rinse.
    *page_break Step back from the shed…
    *goto ah_hide_yard_main
  # Look at the smokehouse.
    The fire inside has been banked low for the night. Meat hangs in dark rows above it, and the smoke gathers under the roof before finding its way out through a gap in the boards.
    *page_break Step back from the smokehouse…
    *goto ah_hide_yard_main
  # Walk back to the clearing.
    *goto_scene timbermouth/ashrun_holt/ashrun_holt ashrun_holt

*label ah_hide_yard_night
The gate hangs shut but not locked. A single lamp burns beneath the lean-to, throwing a dull shine across the troughs and the wet packed earth. The cutting shed is dark, its door barred from inside; only the smokehouse is still at work, breathing a thin thread of smoke into the trees.

*choice
  # Look over the yard.
    The outer frames are bare in the dark. Under the lean-to, the hides left to soak are only dark shapes in the troughs, and the lamp flame trembles each time the wind finds its way through the rails.
    *page_break Step back from the yard…
    *goto ah_hide_yard_main
  # Look at the cutting shed.
    The barred door gives nothing but the smell of clean wood, old blood, and damp earth. A dog lifts its head from the shelter of the steps, watches you for a moment, then puts its muzzle back on its paws.
    *page_break Step back from the shed…
    *goto ah_hide_yard_main
  # Look at the smokehouse.
    A low orange glow shows through a crack at the bottom of the door. The fire inside barely burns, but it keeps the smoke moving over the meat hung above it.
    *page_break Step back from the smokehouse…
    *goto ah_hide_yard_main
  # Walk back to the clearing.
    *goto_scene timbermouth/ashrun_holt/ashrun_holt ashrun_holt
