[b]◈ THE FACTOR'S HALL[/b]
*line_break
Inside the door the hall is long and cool, with a floor of grey flags and a counter running most of its length behind a brass rail, a grille at each end. A board of pinned notices hangs beside the door in a frame divided into three columns, and a schedule is lettered on a plate over the counter. Narrow windows run along the front, each with its own iron grille, and at the back of the hall a panelled door stands closed under a brass plate. The air smells of sealing wax, sand and cold stone.

*if (weather_severity >= 3)
  The doors are pulled to against the weather, and every lamp along the counter is lit.
*elseif (weather = "Rain")
  Rain ticks on the narrow windows, and the flags by the door are dark with wet boots.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Mist turns the windows to white slits, and lamps burn along the counter.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Slush is tracked across the flags from the door.

*comment ── WEATHER MEMORY (calendar ground_state, strictly no people) ──
*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  After days of rain, the flags by the door are slick with old mud, and a damp chill hangs over the hall.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The flags by the door are still dark from the last rain.

*if (hall_first)
  A grey-haired clerk in a black coat with river-serpent cuffs sits at the long end of the counter with a set of nested lead weights lined up beside his scale. "Deposits, letters, withdrawals," he says, without looking up from his page. At the near end, behind the other grille, a young clerk in the same cuffs has a dish of blank brass discs at one elbow and a silver stylus at the other. "Seals," she says, and nods at the dish.
*else
  The grey-haired clerk is working down a column with his pen, and the young clerk at the seals grille is polishing a brass blank on her sleeve.

*if (hall_roll = 1)
  Carters with satchels of sealed packets queue along the rail, and the scratch of the senior clerk's pen is the loudest sound in the hall.
*elseif (hall_roll = 2)
  Two drivers argue in low voices at the counter over a bill of carriage, while the young clerk waits beside them with a seal in her hand.
*else
  The hall is nearly empty. A woman counts coins into a leather purse at the rail, and a boy in livery sleeps on his feet beside the door.
