[b]◈ THE MARKET[/b]

A broad trading ground opens off the square, laid out in bays by low rails and chalk lines, and every bay holds a lot with its price chalked on a board beside it: a stack of seasoned ash planks with its grade painted in a white ring on the end, a heap of squared oak, a row of barrel staves bundled with withies, wheel sets stood on their edges in pairs. Wagons stand nose to tail along the middle, loaded with sacked grain and meal, rough barrels of salt, bars of iron bundled in straw, coal in sacks, crates of pottery and bales of hides and wool, each with a tally board tied to the tailboard. A beam-scale hangs from a timber frame at the yard gate, and the paving is worn into two shallow grooves where the loads come in. Farm stalls crowd the far edge under striped awnings, with hens in wicker crates complaining along the rail, and the whole ground smells of cut ash, hides, grain dust, coal smoke and wet straw.
