[b]◈ THE WOODWORKERS' WORKSHOPS[/b]
*line_break
A lane of low, open-fronted workshops runs back from the square beside the mill, each with its benches in the light of the doorway and its work hung up in the rafters. A wheelwright's front stands at the head of the lane under a painted board reading [i]"ROAN. Wheels, staves and shafts."[/i] Further down are a cooper's, with a row of unfinished barrels waiting beside a stack of staves, a wainwright's, where the bed of a wagon stands on trestles with its wheels still to be fitted, and a bowyer's, with bow staves hung to season and a rack of arrow shafts. Shavings lie ankle-deep and the air smells of glue, linseed and hot iron from the small forge at the lane's end.

*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain drums on the plank roofs, and the benches have been pulled back under the eaves.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  Wind rattles the shop shutters, and every bench has been dragged back from the doorways.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Mist drifts down the lane from the forest and softens the line of open fronts.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow melts on the shavings and turns them to a brown paste at the doorsteps.

*comment ── WEATHER MEMORY (calendar ground_state, strictly no people) ──
*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))

*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The shavings by the doorsteps are still dark from the last rain.

*if (lane_roll = 1)

*elseif (lane_roll = 2)

*elseif (lane_roll = 3)

*elseif (lane_roll = 4)

*else

The cooper works at a bench with a half-raised barrel clamped in an iron hoop before him, its staves splayed like the petals of a flower. He wets each stave with a rag, draws the hoop down, and checks the gaps with a thumbnail. A finished cask stands beside him on a trestle with its bung out, and the smell of fresh oak and char comes off it.

*label tm_works_bowyer
The bowyer works at a long bench under a window, with a stave of ash clamped in a tillering stick and a spokeshave in his hand. Bow staves hang from the rafters by cords, each with its ends sealed in wax and a chalk number on the belly, and a rack of arrow shafts stands by the door, split and trued but not yet fletched. A quiver of finished bows leans in one corner with the lane's mark burnt into every grip, and he sets them on Edda's counter at the head of the lane when they are done.
*page_break Step back out into the lane…

The wainwright's shed is wide enough to take a wagon, and one stands inside it on trestles, its bed framed in ash and its axle-tree laid across the bolsters, waiting for wheels. A plane runs along the edge of a tailboard and the curls of pale wood roll up off the blade and fall on the floor. A rack on the back wall holds shafts, cart poles and ladder frames in rows, and a pair of finished harrows lean against the doorpost.
