*comment ── THE BRIEFING: the one conversation, on the first knock ──
*label pd_first_talk

"I've taken your posting," you say. "Tell me about him."

"Merel Pellam." She shifts the girl to her other hip. "Aldis, my husband. He gathered plants. Roots, ferns, truffles, anything the Longshade grows that the apothecaries in Port Valen will pay for. Twice a season he went in and came out with a pack that smelled like a wet cellar. This time he took two hands from the lane, Marr and Joss, because the rare stuff grows deep."

"When did he go in?"

"Long enough ago that the Holt stopped asking, and so did I." Her chin lifts. "We held the funeral at the turn of the month. We buried his second-best coat, because there was nothing else."

"What do you want from me?"

"I'm not asking you to bring him back." Her voice stays level and her hand moves in the girl's hair. "I want to know how it ended. Where he is, and whether it was quick. The village and my sister put eight silver together for whoever tells me, and that's all there is. Ask in the Holt. He was seen going in."

She waits until you nod, and then she shuts the door softly.
*page_break Step back to the lane…
*goto_scene timbermouth/barleycross/lane bc_lane_lane
