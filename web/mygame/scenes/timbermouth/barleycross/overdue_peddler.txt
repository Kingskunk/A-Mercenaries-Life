"Merel Pellam." She shifts the girl to her other hip. "Aldis, my husband. A tinker. Pots, needles, ribbon. He's walked the round from Timbermouth through here to the Holt every three weeks for twenty years, and he's never been late."

"Who went with him?"

"Two hands from this lane for the forest stretch. Marr and Joss. That track has a rough name." Her mouth goes tight. "It saves a day when the road's wet, and Aldis never could pass up a saved day. The Holt says none of the three ever arrived."

"What do you want from me?"

"Word. And something of his that I can put in my hands." She looks down at the girl, whose face is pressed into her apron. "I can't carry a story to the children. The village put in what it could, and so did my sister. Eight silver, and it's all there is."

She waits until you nod, and then she shuts the door softly.

*comment ── REPORTING: the tag in her hand pays ──

"Merel," you say, and put the brass disc in her hand.

She turns it over. Her thumb runs across the row of small numbers on the back, then across the seal on the front, and stops. The girl looks up at her face.

"That's his," she says. "He wore it on the left, where he could reach it."

"I found them in the Longshade, in a clearing off the middle trails," you say. "All three. A big cat took them, and it took the camp first."

"Marr? Joss?"

"Both of them, with him," you say, as gently as you can.

Merel Pellam doesn't answer for a while. She looks past you down the lane, and the hand with the tag closes slowly on it. When she speaks, it is to the lane. "Thank you for going. I'd have gone myself, if there was anyone to leave them with."

She takes the girl in and comes back with a twist of cloth. She counts eight silver marks into your palm, one at a time, and closes your fingers over them. The brass disc goes into her apron pocket and her hand stays on it. "I'll keep this."
