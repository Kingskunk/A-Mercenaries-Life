*label pd_first_talk

"I've taken the posting," you say. "I'm looking for Merel Pellam."

"You have found her." She shifts the girl to her other hip and studies the Hall insignia. "My husband was Aldis. He gathered roots, ferns, truffles—whatever the Longshade grew that Port Valen's apothecaries would buy. Twice a season he came back with a pack that smelled like a wet cellar. This time he took two hands from the lane, Marr and Joss, because the rare things grow deep."

"When did he go in?"

"Long enough ago that the Holt stopped asking, and so did I." Her hand settles in the girl's hair. "We held the funeral at the turn of the month. We buried his second-best coat, because there was nothing else."

"What do you want from me?"

"I'm not asking you to bring him back." Her voice stays level. "I want to know where it ended, and whether it was quick. The village and my sister put eight silver together for whoever can tell me. That is all there is. Ask in the Holt; he was seen going in."

She waits until you nod, and then she shuts the door softly.

*comment ── THE NEWS: the dead found, and the pouch ──
*label pd_first_camp

"Are you Merel Pellam?" you ask. "I have taken the posting. I have also been in the Longshade."

The woman tightens her hold on the girl. "I am Merel. What did you find?"

"A camp, torn apart. Plant presses, drying frames, a pack dragged through the dirt."

For a moment she does not speak. Then she says, "The frames were Aldis's. He made them himself, bent the wood over the kettle until he scalded both hands." Her eyes stay on yours. "Did you find him? Marr? Joss?"

"Not yet."

"Then do not give me an ending you cannot prove." The words are controlled, but the girl has gone still against her. "Find where that trail went. If they are there, I need to know."

She closes the door without waiting for an answer.
