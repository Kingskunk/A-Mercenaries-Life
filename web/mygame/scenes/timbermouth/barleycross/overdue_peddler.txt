*if (pd_first)
  "I read your paper," you say. "Tell me about him."

  "Merel Pellam." She shifts the girl to her other hip. "Aldis, my husband. He gathered plants. Roots, ferns, truffles, anything the Longshade grows that the apothecaries in Port Valen will pay for. Twice a season he went in and came out with a pack that smelled like a wet cellar. This time he took two hands from the lane, Marr and Joss, because the rare stuff grows deep."

  "When did he go in?"

  "Long enough ago that the Holt stopped asking, and so did I." Her chin lifts. "We held the funeral at the turn of the month. We buried his second-best coat, because there was nothing else."

  "What do you want from me?"

  "I'm not asking you to bring him back." Her voice stays level and her hand moves in the girl's hair. "I want to know how it ended. Where he is, and whether it was quick. The village and my sister put eight silver together for whoever tells me, and that's all there is."

"I found where it ended," you say. "A camp deep in the Longshade, torn open by a big cat. All three of them are in a clearing near it."

Merel Pellam stands very still. The girl looks up at her face.

"Where?" she asks.

"Off the middle trails, a long way in. Marr and Joss are with him."

She looks past you down the lane for a while. When she speaks, it is to the lane. "Then he's somewhere I can't walk to," she says. "Thank you for going."

She takes the girl inside and comes back with a twist of cloth. She counts eight silver marks into your palm, one at a time, and closes your fingers over them.
