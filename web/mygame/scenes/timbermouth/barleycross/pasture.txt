[b]◈ THE COMMON PASTURE[/b]
*line_break
A track runs out past the last farmsteads to a wide grazing common on the low ground by the brook, enclosed with ditches and hurdles and dotted with pollard willows. Sheep and cattle graze it in loose groups, a few geese pick along the water, and nearer the track a set of strong rail pens stands in a row, each with a shed and a trough and a name burnt into its gatepost. The grass is cropped short and the whole place smells of wet earth, dung and trampled clover.

*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain hisses on the long grass, and the animals stand with their tails to the wind and their heads low.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  The animals have crowded together in the lee of the willows, and the hurdles shake in the gale.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Mist lies on the common so thickly that the animals are shapes and the willows are grey smears.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow has settled on the backs of the cattle, and the troughs have been broken free of ice.

*comment ── WEATHER MEMORY (calendar ground_state, strictly no people) ──
*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  After days of rain the common is a bog at every gate, and the sheep graze the ridges.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The track and the gateways are still dark and soft from the last rain.

*if (pas_roll = 1)
  A halfling boy rides a big plough horse bareback along the brook, with his short legs sticking straight out and his hands twisted in the mane.
*elseif (pas_roll = 2)
  A girl and a dog drive a small flock of sheep across the common toward a gap in the hurdles.
*elseif (pas_roll = 3)
  Two men stand at a rail with their arms folded, looking at a young mule in the pen and saying very little.
*else
  A woman walks a mare up and down the track on a long rein, watching the way she moves.

*choice
  # Look over the pens.
    Five pens stand in a row, each with a different name burnt into its gatepost: Tull, Ames, Reeve, Hobb, Garrow. In the first a pair of grey mules stand nose to tail swatting flies, in the second a heavy brown horse looks over the rail with a thick forelock hanging in its eyes, in the third three yearling colts jostle for the trough, and the last two hold only a goat each, on loan. Every animal has a hoof-brand and a chalk number on its flank.
    *page_break Step back from the rail…

  # Watch the animals on the common.
    The cattle graze in a loose line down the slope with the sun on their backs, and the sheep move together across the grass like one slow animal. The brook runs silver along the low edge, and a heron stands in it, waiting.
