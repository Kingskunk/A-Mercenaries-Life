*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Constant geography and smell: no weather, time of day or people.
*label bc_place
Four farm roads meet on Barleycross's wide grassy common. A stone well and muddy duck pond sit among the cart ruts; the timber Gathering Hall stands on the eastern edge. Behind it, a roofed hitching yard has two spare rail pens, a trough and a hay rack for road animals.

Some forty farmsteads run out along the cartways: long-roofed barns, mucked yards and cottages behind bramble hedges. Beyond them lie strips of barley and oats, dark rows of roots, and low pasture cut by drainage ditches and pollard willows.

At the southern edge, the brook turns the mill wheel beneath a low millhouse. Cured hay, hearth smoke, dung and ditch moss hang in the air.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, matched to time_period; no people. Closes with the ground's memory of recent weather.

*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain falls steadily on the thatch and the open fields, and the ditches run brown beside every lane.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  Wind lays the barley flat in long waves, and the rain drives across the common in sheets.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Ground mist lies over the fields and drowns the hedgerows, so that the farmsteads show only as darker shapes.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow lies thin on the thatch and the stubble, and the common is a grey slush.
*elseif (weather = "Blizzard")
  A freezing gale drives snow across the open fields and piles it against every hedge and barn wall.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (bc_dark)
    Starlight lies pale over the fields, and the hedgerows are black lines across them.
  *elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
    The low sun lays long shadows from the hedges across the stubble and turns the thatch to amber.
  *else
    Clear light lies on the fields, and the barley moves in the breeze.
*else
  *comment Overcast
  *if (bc_dark)
    A heavy, starless night lies over the fields, and the farms are dark but for a few banked fires.
  *else
    A flat grey overcast sits over the fields, and the air is heavy with the smell of the land.
