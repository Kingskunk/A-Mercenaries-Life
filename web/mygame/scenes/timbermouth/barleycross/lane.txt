[b]◈ THE LANE[/b]
*line_break
The lane runs south from the common, with a farmstead on either side every fifty paces or so. Some are large; some are little more than a cottage and shed. Barns, middens and gardens stand behind them, with long narrow fields beyond the hedges. Thatch hangs low over the doors, hay and barley ricks fill the yards, and cart wheels and hooves have cut deep ruts. By day, a few gates put out baskets or crates for passersby. The lane smells of dung, sweet hay, boiled roots and woodsmoke.

*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain drips from the thatch eaves and runs along the ruts, and every gate table has been dragged under a lean-to.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  Wind tears at the hedge and bangs the loft doors along the whole lane, and every table has been taken in.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Mist hides the far end of the lane, and the farmsteads show only as the nearest gate and a faint glow of fire.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow lies in the ruts, and every yard has a path trodden through it from the house to the barn.
*elseif (weather = "Blizzard")
  Snow drives down the lane in a freezing gale and piles against every gate, and every yard is shut up tight, with only a thread of smoke from each chimney.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))

*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The ruts are still dark and wet from the last rain.

*else
