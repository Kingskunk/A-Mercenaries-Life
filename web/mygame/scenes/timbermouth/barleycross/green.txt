[b]◈ THE COMMON AND THE GATHERING HALL[/b]
*line_break
The Gathering Hall is a single long room under a steep thatch, built of squared oak on a stone footing, with its door standing open on the common and a bench along its front. Inside are trestle tables stacked against one wall, a cold hearth at the far end, and a plank board holding papers beneath narrow wooden slats. A bell hangs in a little frame over the door, and the common spreads away below the bench, with its well-head, its pond and its notice post.

*if (weather_severity >= 3)
  The door of the Hall has been pulled to against the weather, and the wind shakes the thatch and moans in the rafters. A fire has been lit in the cold hearth, and the benches along the walls are crowded with people who had nowhere else to be.
*elseif (weather = "Rain")
  Rain drums on the thatch, and the bench on the porch is soaked, so everyone has moved inside.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow lies on the thatch and drips from the eaves, and a small fire has been lit in the hearth.

*comment ── WEATHER MEMORY (calendar ground_state, strictly no people) ──
*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  After days of rain the floor inside the door is trodden to mud, and the whole hall smells of wet wool.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The floor inside the door is still dark from the last rain.

*if (not(bc_head_seen))

  A halfling woman in a worn sheepskin waistcoat sits on the bench with her bare feet not quite reaching the ground, a folded packet of household papers across her knee and a pipe cold in her teeth, and she watches you cross the common the whole way. She is round, broad and brown-faced, with iron-grey curls cut short, and she hops down from the bench when you are close enough to speak to. "Wenna Hart. Village head, because nobody else would have it." She points with the pipe stem. "Business goes in the Hall. Idling goes by the pond. Both have kept people busy for years."
*else
