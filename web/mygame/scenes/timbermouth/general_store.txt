[b]◈ MARLOW'S GENERAL STORE[/b]
*line_break
A deep shop with a plank floor stands beside the Market's yard gate, under a board reading [i]"MARLOW. General goods, by weight and measure."[/i] Coils of rope hang from the rafters by thickness, lanterns and harness leather hang from hooks along the walls, and sacks of feed and kegs of nails stand in rows on the floor. A beam-scale with a tray of brass weights hangs over the counter, and the shelves behind it hold tin cans of lamp oil, jars of chalk, tied canvas rolls and bolts of waxed cloth. The shop smells of hemp, tar, lamp oil, leather and hay.

*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain runs down the front window in sheets, and a pool of water has spread under the door.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  Wind shakes the shop's shutters in their frames, and every lamp over the counter is lit.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  The window is a pale grey square, and lamps burn at the counter against the gloom.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Melting slush pools on the planks by the door, and a sack has been laid down to soak it up.

You walk the shelves. Rope hangs in coils by thickness, from cord thin enough for a bootlace to a line as thick as your wrist. Pitch-dipped torches stand in a barrel by the door, hooded lanterns and canvas tool rolls hang from hooks along the wall, a lute hangs from a nail beside the window, and paper twists of lye soap, jars of chalk and tin boxes of flint and steel line the counter. Tied canvas rolls and sheets of waxed cloth fill the lower racks, and a shelf by the door holds cloth-wrapped parcels of trail food.
