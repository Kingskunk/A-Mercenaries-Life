[b]◈ THE COOPERAGE BASIN[/b]
*line_break
Beneath a charred timber archway marked with a barrel-maker's cutting tool and reading [i]"Kaelen & Sons, Guild Coopers,"[/i] the lower end of the Middle Ward opens into a broad yard where the road descends to the harbor. High racks of split white-oak slats dry under open sheds, and the air smells of scorched wood, pine pitch, and wet horse straw.

In the center of the yard, Master Cooper Kaelen and his smiths heat barrel slats over open iron fire baskets, hammering red-hot hoops down over the wood with notched tools. Every strike rings through the terrace with a sharp, echoing *tink-tink-CLANG*. Stout freight wagons wait in the turning circle, their drivers lounging on cargo boxes and rolling knucklebones.
