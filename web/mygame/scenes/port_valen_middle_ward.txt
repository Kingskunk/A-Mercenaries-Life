The road climbs in turns through the salty mist from the docks, then levels out on the broad stone terraces of the Second Terrace. Between the lower docks and the tall limestone cliffs of Civic Heights, the Middle Ward is where the city's skilled workers live and work. Sturdy timber buildings lean over narrow lanes, their upper floors nearly touching beneath steep slate roofs, while their lower shutters open as shop counters. The air smells of cooled iron, brass dust, cedar sawdust, and warm caraway bread from the public ovens.

At the center of the square, an octagonal stone public fountain sends clear mountain water through four bronze lion-head spouts into deep basins. Narrow lanes branch from the crossroads toward the different craft quarters. Opposite the fountain, a broad chimney rises above a painted sign reading [i]"Mother Marda's Communal Oven,"[/i] sending sweet rye smoke into the air.
