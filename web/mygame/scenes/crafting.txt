*comment Crafting: one brew, made from the Inventory's Craft view (2026-10-23, the user's design; the view and its rules are in Resources & Quest/ALCHEMY_CRAFTING_REFERENCE.md).
*comment The panel checks the tools, picks the recipe from crafting-data.js and leaves the choice in craft_potion (a catalog id), craft_potion_name, craft_a, craft_b (counted ingredient ids), their names and craft_dc.
*comment It then runs choicescript_stats codex_craft_now, which gosubs craft_brew: a Herbalism check (the better of Nature or Medicine), 20 to 30 minutes either way, and then
*comment   a success: both ingredients and one empty vial are used up and the potion is added to the pack;
*comment   a failure: one of the two ingredients, picked at random, is lost, and the vial is kept.
*comment Output craft_ok and craft_text (one line, which the sidebar shows as a toast). No page lock of its own: it is run once per click by codex_craft_now, which ends at once, so there is no page to replay.
*label craft_brew
*set craft_text ""
*set craft_ok false
*if (combat_engaged)
  *set craft_text "You cannot brew in the middle of a fight."
  *return
*temp cb_have_a ({craft_a})
*temp cb_have_b ({craft_b})
*if (not(has_empty_potion_vial))
  *set craft_text "You have no empty vial to fill."
  *return
*if (cb_have_a < 1)
  *set craft_text "You have no ${craft_a_name} left."
  *return
*if (cb_have_b < 1)
  *set craft_text "You have no ${craft_b_name} left."
  *return
*comment THE BURNER'S FLAME (2026-10-23, the user): a brew over the burner burns one of its five uses (burner_fuel), whether it works or not; an empty burner takes a flask of lamp oil from the pack to fill (five uses), and with no flask the brew is refused before anything is spent. A flame cantrip lights it for nothing.
*temp cb_flame (((known_fire_bolt) or (known_prestidigitation)) or (known_control_flames))
*temp cb_poured false
*if ((craft_burner) and (not(cb_flame)))
  *if (burner_fuel <= 0)
    *gosub_scene equipment gear_count "lamp_oil"
    *if (gear_qty < 1)
      *set craft_text "Your burner has no oil."
      *return
    *gosub_scene equipment sell_gear "lamp_oil"
    *set burner_fuel 5
    *set cb_poured true
*gosub_scene dice_engine roll_skill_check craft_dc "nature" "" "medicine" "" "" ""
*set craft_ok check_success
*temp cb_minutes 25
*rand cb_minutes 20 30
*temp cb_lost 1
*rand cb_lost 1 2
*if ((craft_burner) and (not(cb_flame)))
  *set burner_fuel - 1
*if (craft_ok)
  *set {craft_a} (cb_have_a - 1)
  *set {craft_b} (cb_have_b - 1)
  *gosub_scene equipment sell_gear "empty_potion_vial"
  *gosub_scene equipment grant_gear craft_potion 1
  *set craft_text "🧪 Brewed ${craft_potion_name} (check ${check_total} against ${craft_dc}). ${cb_minutes} minutes."
  *comment The first success teaches the recipe (the Craft view's Recipes list reads recipe_<id>).
  *temp cb_flag ("recipe_" & craft_potion)
  *if (not({cb_flag}))
    *set {cb_flag} true
    *set craft_text "${craft_text} New recipe learned."
*else
  *if (cb_lost = 1)
    *set {craft_a} (cb_have_a - 1)
    *set craft_text "The brew fouls (check ${check_total} against ${craft_dc}) and the ${craft_a_name} is lost. Your vial is safe. ${cb_minutes} minutes."
  *else
    *set {craft_b} (cb_have_b - 1)
    *set craft_text "The brew fouls (check ${check_total} against ${craft_dc}) and the ${craft_b_name} is lost. Your vial is safe. ${cb_minutes} minutes."
*if (cb_poured)
  *set craft_text "${craft_text} You fill the burner from a flask of oil."
*set hours_to_pass 0
*set minutes_to_pass cb_minutes
*set time_advance_call_id "craft_brew_time"
*gosub_scene calendar advance_time
*return
