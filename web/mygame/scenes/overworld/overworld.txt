*comment THE OVERWORLD: where the player stands between towns, and how a journey is made. A TEST BUILD (2026-10-07): the road from Port Valen to Timbermouth, on foot, with an
*comment empty hook for events and nothing else.
*comment
*comment The tiles, the roads and the distances live in web/mygame/overworld-data.js (window.Overworld), read here through *script exactly as names.txt reads the name generator, so
*comment the map panel and this scene never disagree about a distance. The page does NOT list the tiles: the map panel (overworldmap.js) owns picking, with drag, zoom and a card
*comment for every tile, so the world can have fifty tiles or four hundred without this scene changing. It works like the trade panel: the panel writes the tile the player picked into
*comment ow_dest, then presses the ONE option below ("Travel to the tile you picked on the map"). Everything is still decided here: ow_travel asks the data file whether that tile
*comment exists and whether there is a road to it, and refuses anything else, so the panel can grant nothing the data does not allow. A scripted test sets ow_dest by hand.
*comment
*comment A journey is ONE page: ow_travel works out the route from where the player stands and walks it leg by leg (24 minutes a mile, so 20 miles is an 8-hour march): each leg is charged
*comment the exposure of the moment (calendar.txt calc_exposure: the weather changes while you walk, so a leg after midnight pays the new day's weather) and moves the clock once, trail
*comment rations are eaten for you at the hunger point (calendar.txt advance_time), and one summary line is printed before the new overworld page follows on the same page with the new tile.
*comment Nothing stops the player at each tile. The player stops by picking a nearer tile first and camping there: ow_camp is an 8-hour sleep on any tile that is not a town, which clears
*comment fatigue. A camp counts as a shared bunk for resolve_sleep (so the wool blanket's +1 HP works), the oilcloth halves its rain, a flint and tinderbox (or the cantrips Fire Bolt and Prestidigitation) halves its cold. Roadside inns and
*comment road events stop a journey on the tile they happen on (see ow_event below and travel_events.txt).
*comment
*comment A replay (a refresh, or opening Stats and returning) reruns a page from its start on top of its END state, so ow_travel must not read a value it changes itself. The route is
*comment worked out once under a page_id lock (ow_trip_locked): whether the trip is valid, the tile the player stood on, the miles and the minutes are stored then, and a replayed
*comment page reads them back instead of working them out again from a position that has already moved. The WHOLE walk (every leg's exposure and clock) sits inside that lock: advance_time's
*comment own replay guard remembers only the last call on a page, so a second call per page cannot rely on it. The camp page has the same shape under ow_camp_locked.
*comment
*comment Not in *scene_list: compile.js lists it with verifyFileName. The map panel shows only while ow_on is set.
