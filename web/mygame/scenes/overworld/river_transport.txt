[b]◈ THE GREY RIVER TRANSPORT: DOWNRIVER[/b]
*line_break

The berth clerk marks your passage, and you cross the loading plank onto a broad forty-foot freight barge. A wagon has been chocked between stacks of bound timber, with passengers and their baggage settled beneath an oiled-canvas awning amid the working cargo.

*if (has_wagon)
  Your wagon is rolled aboard over the reinforced freight ramp, its wheels chocked and its team secured among the draft animals near the middle of the deck.
*elseif (has_horse)
  ${horse_name} follows you up the broad livestock ramp and is tied among the draft animals near the middle of the deck, where the hull moves least. The transport hands treat ${horse_obj} as ordinary freight with opinions.
*else
  Behind you, handlers lead draft animals aboard by the broad livestock ramp while the last wheels are chained against the deck chocks.

The crew casts off below the weir and lets the current take the laden hull south. Alderford's wharves disappear behind the bends; the gorge closes around the river, then slowly opens toward the tidal reach and the masts of Port Valen.

[b]◈ THE GREY RIVER TRANSPORT: UPRIVER[/b]
*line_break

The passage clerk adds your name to the upriver manifest, and you board a broad forty-foot freight barge among traders, bundled cargo and a wagon chained over its axles.

*if (has_wagon)
  Your wagon is rolled aboard over the reinforced freight ramp, its wheels chained over the axles and its team secured where the hull moves least.
*elseif (has_horse)
  ${horse_name} is led up the livestock ramp and tied among the draft animals near the middle of the deck. There is room enough for ${horse_obj} to shift ${horse_pos} footing when the towline draws taut.
*else
  The last draft team comes aboard behind you, iron-shod wheels sounding across the loading planks before the ramps are hauled in.

Going north is slower work. Tow teams take the bank where the gorge allows it; elsewhere the deck crew lean into long poles and haul against fixed lines while the Grey presses steadily south beneath the hull. Port Valen's masts sink behind the bends, replaced by limestone walls, wooded banks and the long pull toward Alderford's weir.
