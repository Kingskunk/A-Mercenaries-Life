*label aly_tavern_entry
*set scenery_subplace "generic_tavern_inn"

The heavy oak door swings shut against the damp chill of the Valen Reach, cutting off the whistle of the evening wind. Inside, the air smells of peat smoke, boiled greens, and stale small beer. 

A handful of locals—timber-haulers with grime under their nails, a peddler counting lead tokens, and a pair of turnpike guards—huddle over their trenchers in low, guarded conversation.

Across the room, sitting at a corner table with a clear line of sight to the entrance, is an elf woman. She wears travel-stained leathers and a moss-green tunic, her pale golden hair braided with plain leather cord. She is idly peeling an apple with a small folding knife.

*comment ── PAPER DOLL VISUAL ASSESSMENT ──
As you step across the threshold, her hazel eyes flick up from the fruit. Her gaze sweeps over you with an assessor's speed. She notes the mud of the road clinging to your ${feet_prose}, the fit and weight of your ${armor_prose}, and the telltale presence of your ${weapon_prose}.

Her gaze lingers for a brief second on the travel-wear of your ${cloak_prose} before her face blooms into a radiant, innocent smile.

She tips her cup of cider toward you in a polite, wordless tavern greeting, waiting to see what you do next.

*choice
  # Approach boldly and take a seat at her table.
    *set aly_approach "bold"
    You cross the grit-dusted boards and slide onto the bench across from her. 

    "See anything you like?" you ask with a smirk. "I'm ${name}."

    Alyndra doesn’t flinch. Instead, her smile tilts into something conspiratorial and amused as she sets her knife down.

    "Bold of you, ${name}," she says, leaning forward on her elbows and resting her chin in her palms. Her eyes trace your ${weapon_prose} with playful curiosity. "And perhaps I did. A woman spends three days sharing a room with carters who smell of wet mule, and in walks someone with salt on their boots and a sense of purpose."

    Alyndra blinks, looking slightly sheepish, though her warm smile never wavers. She sets her knife down on the platter and gestures to the empty bench across from her.

    "Guilty," she laughs softly, her voice light and melodic. "No offense intended, ${name}. Out on the Reach, a woman traveling alone learns to watch the door closely. Won't you sit? I'd welcome company with better manners than the bailiffs."

      When she catches you watching her, she tilts her head, smiles warmly, and raises her cider cup again in an unmistakable invitation.

    "I'm ${name}," you say, nodding to her.

    "A pleasure, ${name}," she answers, setting her apple knife aside. "I'm Alyndra."

Alyndra slides a clean slice of apple across the wooden platter toward you.

"You have the cut of someone who knows the rough road, ${name}," she says, resting her elbows on the pine table. "What brings someone with a ${weapon_prose} out into the Reach? Looking for coin, or just trying to get across the gap without paying Baron Karr's bailiffs?"

*choice
  # "Looking for coin and work. The Reach roads usually have both."
    "They certainly do," she smiles, her eyes brightening. "Though out here, most work ends with an axe in your hand or a shovel in the ditch. Real coin takes a bit more initiative."
    *goto aly_her_story

  # "Knowledge and old ground. The Reach has plenty of history buried in it."
    "More than most people realize," she agrees, leaning in slightly. "Old Imperial forts, collapsed watchposts, ruins that the turnpike carters just walk right past because they never look up from the mud."
    *goto aly_her_story

  # "My business is my own. What's yours, Alyndra?"
    She laughs softly, entirely unfazed by your bluntness. "Fair enough. A cautious tongue keeps a head on its shoulders out here."
    *goto aly_her_story

*label aly_her_story
"As for me," Alyndra continues, her voice softening into a wistful, reflective cadence. "Misfortune turned into wanderlust, I suppose. My parents were wood elves, modest merchants. Sickness took them when I was young, and the local lord burned our steading fearing plague. A kind tavernkeep took me in and hid my heritage behind an apron."

She gives a light, self-deprecating shrug of one shoulder.

"I spent years wiping beer grease and watching sellswords ride off toward the horizon. One morning I took my parents' old horse, bought a hunting knife, and rode out. Better to freeze on the high trails than die an old maid with dishwater hands. Now I scout paths, trade gear, and search old ruins for treasure."

*choice
  # Listen closely to her tragic tale.@{show_stat_hints  [Insight DC 13]|}

    *else
      Her grief sounds quiet and genuine—the common tragedy of an orphan girl who chose the danger of the open road over a life of scrubbing boards.
    *goto aly_topic_pitch

  # "Is that why you're watching strangers? Found something out there you can't tackle alone?"
    *set aly_asked_watching true

    "Guilty as charged," she murmurs. "You're sharp. Sharper than the usual carters. It's rare to find someone out here who understands how dangerous this road is for a woman alone."

*comment ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
*comment SCENE 1, PART 3: THE PITCH & THE HANDSHAKE

Alyndra leans across the table, her hazel eyes gleaming with suppressed excitement.

"Three weeks ago, following a wash-out along the coastal bluffs, I found a collapsed archway beneath the roots of a tree. Worked stone—Imperial granite, smooth as glass. Inside is a sealed vault door with interlocking bronze rings and arcane glyphs."

She watches your face carefully.

"I didn't dare crack it alone. The coastal bluffs have wolves and tide hazards, and warding seals are beyond me. But with someone of your talents? We take the smuggler's cut through the bluffs—halves the travel time to the coast. We reach the vault, bypass the seals, and split whatever Imperial relics are inside straight down the middle. Equal shares."

*label aly_deal_menu

*choice

    *goto aly_deal_menu

  # Decline the venture; you travel alone.
    *goto aly_refuse_warning

She beams, her relief looking completely unfeigned. "Splendid! It's a deal, then."

Alyndra extends her slender right hand across the pine boards to seal the contract.

*else

She pulls her hand back with an eager bounce and rises from the bench.

"First light, then! Get whatever rest you can tonight, ${name}. The climb through the limestone fins is steep, and tomorrow we find out what the Empire left behind."

She slips gracefully through the common room toward the guest loft, leaving you with an empty trencher and the low hum of the tavern.
