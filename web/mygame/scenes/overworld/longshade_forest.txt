*comment THE LONGSHADE FOREST: the second dungeon (2026-10-14). A *goto_scene-only scene like port_valen_silt_conduits.txt, NOT in *scene_list, listed in compile.js. Everything here is a LAYOUT ONLY: 38 places
*comment (15 rooms, 21 trail junctions and 2 mouths) numbered in the order a walker from the south mouth meets them, with placeholder text and an empty encounter table. The user will name the rooms and
*comment junctions and fill them in a piece at a time. The layout is lsf_ in web/mygame/dungeons/longshade_forest.js and tools/check_dungeon_map.js compares it with this file.
*comment
*comment TWO MOUTHS (the user's call): the SOUTH mouth (lsf_x1) opens on The Longshade (overworld tile lf) and the EAST mouth (lsf_x2) on West Longshade (ua); the overworld option "Go into the Longshade forest." on either tile
*comment starts the run at that mouth. A mouth leaves the dungeon onto ITS OWN tile, so walking in at one and out at the other is a way between the two tiles. dg_dev_entry (the Developer menu's entries) sends every exit back to the Developer menu.
*comment
*comment LIGHT BY THE DAY (the user's call): dg_daylit is set before the run begins, and dungeon.txt dg_dark_check then reads the dungeon as lit by day and at dusk, and dark at night, before dawn and in a storm (weather
*comment severity 3 or more). By day nothing burns and nothing is asked; when the light fails during a run (dg_fell_dark) the player is offered a light, as when a torch dies.
*comment HOW A MOVE RUNS is as in the Silt-Gate channels: an option sets dg_dest and dg_dest_hall and goes to lsf_move, which calls dungeon.txt dg_step (10 minutes, one step of light, a 40% encounter roll on a trail
*comment junction step). The table is empty for now, so no step ever finds anything.
