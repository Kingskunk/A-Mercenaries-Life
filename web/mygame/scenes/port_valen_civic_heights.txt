The road from the Middle Ward ends at a broad plaza of cut limestone, and the buildings change from timber to stone. The Council Hall stands at its head, tall-windowed and long-winged, with a bronze balance-scale taller than a man hung above its central doors and the words [i]"The Free City of Port Valen"[/i] carved beneath it. Its two wings run down either side of the plaza, and every door along them is carved with the city's three-masted seal. Painted boards mark public windows under deep eaves, where people stand holding cargo papers and tax notices, and beside the windows stand sloped pine desks where men in ink-stained cuffs write out sheets for people at their elbows. A gate signed [i]Port Watch[/i] stands beside the west wing, and its yard is crowded with crates and rolled papers.

Two tax windows face the plaza from the east wing. The long counter is reached by a wide flight of worn steps and draws a long queue of people in plain coats. The other is a side window with a private door, where clerks in river-serpent colors hand in leather cases and take out stamped receipts.

Facing the Council Hall across the plaza stands a tall stone house, the tallest on the plaza after the Hall itself, its bronze-banded doors hung with brass balance-scales and a gilded river-serpent coiled along the lintel.

The cathedral rises above the Council Hall's roofline on an older foundation, its bells carrying out over the roofs of the lower city. Kitchens with iron soup-kettles cluster around its steps, and thin, tired people in mended clothes queue there, within sight of the Council Hall and a long way from its side window.
