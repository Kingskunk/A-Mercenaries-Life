[b]◈ CIVIC HEIGHTS & CATHEDRAL DISTRICT[/b]
*line_break
[b][⏳ Civic Heights Hour: ${clock_time} (${time_period}) | Hit Points: ${hp_current}/${hp_max}${hub_condition_str}][/b]

The road from the Middle Ward ends at a broad plaza of cut limestone, and the buildings change from timber to stone. The Council Hall stands at its head, tall-windowed, with a bronze balance-scale taller than a man hung above its doors and the words [i]"The Free City of Port Valen"[/i] carved beneath it. Around the plaza stand the tax hall, the records house, and the magistrates' courts, each door carved with the city's three-masted seal. Painted boards mark the public windows, with deep eaves above them for the petitioners who come with cargo papers, tax notices, and residency tokens. Beside the windows stand sloped pine desks where public scriveners write petitions and work contracts for anyone who cannot write their own. The Port Watch headquarters stands beside the courts, its yard crowded with seized goods and complaint scrolls.

The tax hall keeps two windows. The long counter, for householders and small traders, is reached by a wide flight of worn steps. The other is a side window with a private door, where clerks in river-serpent colors hand in leather cases and take out stamped receipts.

The cathedral rises above the courts on an older foundation, its bells carrying over every district. Charity kitchens cluster around its steps, and the sick, the displaced, and the people with nowhere else to ask for help come there, within sight of the Council Hall and a long way from its side window.

[b]◈ THE RECORDS HOUSE: THE OPEN ROLL[/b]
*line_break
Inside the arch, a long hall of pale stone runs under a barrel roof. Wooden frames line both walls from floor to ceiling, each holding one posted sheet, sealed by the patron and stamped with the city's three-masted seal. At the far end, three windows in a low counter are marked [i]Postings[/i], [i]Claims[/i], and [i]Seals[/i]. Along the side wall a narrow desk is kept for clerks in river-serpent colors, who hand in bundles of contracts that are already sealed.
