*label apply_ability_score_range
*comment Ability scores, one score for one slot: rolls each of the six for a type-library enemy, feeding
*comment initiative and the derived combat-bonus/AC/HP labels below. The manual per-slot override wins if set
*comment (nonzero, see startup.txt); otherwise this rolls within [aasr_min, aasr_max], the calling type's own
*comment default range. Called six times (once per score) from both branches of every apply_type_X below: slot 1
*comment passes the bare "combat_enemy_" prefix (the same bare names fight_setup later snapshots into
*comment combat_enemy1_*), slot 2-and-beyond passes that type's own apt_p prefix directly. Resets its own override
*comment to 0 once consumed (fight_cleanup also resets every score and override as a backstop, so a hand-authored
*comment fight that never touches ability scores can't inherit a leftover roll from an earlier fight).
*params aasr_prefix aasr_field aasr_min aasr_max
*comment Shares combat_roll_initiative's own whole-fight lock (combat_init_locked/locked_combat_init_page_id,
*comment this file, 2026-09-30) rather than a lock of its own: called many times per fight (once per ability score
*comment per slot) from apply_enemy_type/apply_ally_type, always BEFORE combat_roll_initiative in the same
*comment fight_setup call, on the same first page -- one shared "has this fight's whole setup phase already run

*comment must skip both together. Unlocked, a same-page refresh (fight entry through the first real pause, however

*comment STR/DEX cascading into a different combat_enemy_atk_bonus/dmg_bonus (apply_derived_combat_bonuses) even
*comment though nothing about the fight's actual events changed. The one-time manual-override reset below is safe

*comment already reflects.
*if (not(combat_init_locked)) or (not(locked_combat_init_page_id = page_id))
  *temp aasr_ov_name ((aasr_prefix & aasr_field) & "_override")

*label apply_derived_combat_bonuses
*comment A "ranged" weapon_type always attacks off DEX; everything else uses whichever of STR/DEX rolled higher
*comment (the same rule the player's own finesse weapons use), so a DEX-leaning melee type needs no extra flag.
*comment atk_bonus = key ability mod + combat_lib_prof_bonus (flat 2 -- every type in either the enemy or ally
*comment library is early, low-tier content). dmg_bonus = key ability mod alone: RAW, proficiency never adds to
*comment damage, matching how the player's own combat_player_dmg_bonus is already just str_mod/dex_mod with no
*comment separate prof term. Shared by both apply_enemy_type and apply_ally_type (2026-09-28) -- prefix-generic
*comment already, nothing enemy-specific here except the constant it used to hardcode by name, not by parameter.
*comment Shares combat_roll_initiative's own whole-fight lock (combat_init_locked/locked_combat_init_page_id,
*comment 2026-09-30) -- same reasoning as apply_ability_score_range's own lock (its *create comment, startup.txt,
*comment has the full explanation): called only once per slot, always during the same one-time fight_setup phase.
*comment Reads/writes the SHARED sm_mod/sm_mod_str scratch (score_to_mod) alongside every other caller in the
*comment whole game, not just combat -- an unlocked replay recomputing it here on a page that spans a whole
*comment surprise round (no real pause in between) clobbers atk_bonus/dmg_bonus with a fresh mod even though the
*comment ability score it reads is itself already correctly preserved, since RECOMPUTING is not free of side
*comment effects when the computation itself writes through a shared global.
*params adcb_prefix
*if (not(combat_init_locked)) or (not(locked_combat_init_page_id = page_id))
  *temp adcb_str {(adcb_prefix & "strength")}
  *temp adcb_dex {(adcb_prefix & "dexterity")}
  *gosub_scene startup score_to_mod adcb_str
  *temp adcb_str_mod sm_mod
  *gosub_scene startup score_to_mod adcb_dex
  *temp adcb_dex_mod sm_mod
  *temp adcb_key_mod adcb_str_mod
  *if ({(adcb_prefix & "weapon_type")} = "ranged")
    *set adcb_key_mod adcb_dex_mod
  *elseif (adcb_dex_mod > adcb_str_mod)
    *set adcb_key_mod adcb_dex_mod
  *set {(adcb_prefix & "atk_bonus")} (adcb_key_mod + combat_lib_prof_bonus)
  *set {(adcb_prefix & "dmg_bonus")} adcb_key_mod
*return

*label apply_derived_ac
*comment ada_base_ac is the type's own default chassis number (armor/natural toughness), not a final answer --
*comment {prefix}base_ac_override (0 = "use the default") can raise or lower it per fight while still letting the
*comment slot's own rolled DEX apply on top, instead of hand-computing a final AC. No armor-tier DEX cap like the
*comment player's medium/heavy armor: nothing in this library needs that yet, and the final ac_override
*comment (apply_slot_stat_override) still covers a DEX-blind heavy fighter if one ever shows up.
*params ada_prefix ada_base_ac
*temp ada_ov_name ((ada_prefix & "base_ac") & "_override")

*label apply_derived_hp
*comment adh_hit_dice_count/adh_hit_die_max are the type's own defaults, not a final answer -- the per-slot
*comment overrides (0 = "use the default") can make one instance tougher or squishier while still letting its own
*comment rolled CON apply on top. Same formula startup.txt's hp_max uses. Clamped to a floor of 1 so a
*comment single-hit-die type with a very low CON can never come out dead on arrival.
*params adh_prefix adh_hit_dice_count adh_hit_die_max

  *set adh_hit_die_max adh_hdm_ov

  *set adh_hp 1
*set {(adh_prefix & "hp")} adh_hp
*set {(adh_prefix & "max_hp")} adh_hp
*return
