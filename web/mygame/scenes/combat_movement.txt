*label combat_initialize_shared_lane

*label combat_get_high_ground_attack_modifier
*comment Shared elevation query for ranged weapon and spell ATTACK rolls. The lane coordinate, not an enemy type,
*comment decides elevation: a combatant on any "high_ground" terrain slot gains +2 against a target not on high
*comment ground, while a low attacker suffers -2 against an elevated target. If both (or neither) are elevated,
*comment the modifier is 0. Saving throws intentionally never call this label.
*params cghgam_attacker_kind cghgam_attacker_slot cghgam_defender_kind cghgam_defender_slot
*set combat_high_ground_attack_bonus 0
*set combat_high_ground_attack_label ""
*temp cghgam_attacker_position combat_lane_player_position
*temp cghgam_defender_position combat_lane_player_position
*if (cghgam_attacker_kind = "enemy")
  *set cghgam_attacker_position {(("combat_enemy" & cghgam_attacker_slot) & "_position")}
*if (cghgam_attacker_kind = "ally")
  *set cghgam_attacker_position {(("combat_ally" & cghgam_attacker_slot) & "_position")}
*if (cghgam_defender_kind = "enemy")
  *set cghgam_defender_position {(("combat_enemy" & cghgam_defender_slot) & "_position")}
*if (cghgam_defender_kind = "ally")
  *set cghgam_defender_position {(("combat_ally" & cghgam_defender_slot) & "_position")}
*temp cghgam_attacker_high false
*temp cghgam_defender_high false
*temp cghgam_i 1
*label combat_get_high_ground_attack_modifier_loop
*if (cghgam_i <= combat_terrain_slot_cap)
  *temp cghgam_type {(("combat_terrain" & cghgam_i) & "_type")}
  *temp cghgam_position {(("combat_terrain" & cghgam_i) & "_position")}
  *if (cghgam_type = "high_ground")
    *if (cghgam_attacker_position = cghgam_position)
      *set cghgam_attacker_high true
    *if (cghgam_defender_position = cghgam_position)
      *set cghgam_defender_high true
  *set cghgam_i + 1
  *goto combat_get_high_ground_attack_modifier_loop
*if ((cghgam_attacker_high) and (not(cghgam_defender_high)))
  *set combat_high_ground_attack_bonus 2
  *set combat_high_ground_attack_label " +2 (High Ground)"
*if ((cghgam_defender_high) and (not(cghgam_attacker_high)))
  *set combat_high_ground_attack_bonus -2
  *set combat_high_ground_attack_label " -2 (Target on High Ground)"
*return

*label fight_pick_target
*comment Kill priority (2026-09-28): the lowest-HP living slot, not the lowest slot number -- finishes off
*comment whichever enemy is already wounded instead of always working strictly front-to-back. Shared by the
*comment player's own HUD-fallback targeting AND every ally's turn (combat_next_turn), so both get smarter from
*comment one change, not duplicated per caller -- the first piece of a wider smarter-AI pass (see
*comment GAMEPLAY_MECHANICS_RULES.md). A HUD-clicked target (combat_player_chosen_target, sticky until it dies --
*comment see startup.txt) still wins over it whenever one is set and still alive, checked first, below.
*set combat_active_target 0
*if (combat_player_chosen_target > 0)
  *temp fpt_chosen_hp {(("combat_enemy" & combat_player_chosen_target) & "_hp")}
  *if ((combat_player_chosen_target <= combat_enemy_count) and (fpt_chosen_hp > 0))
    *set combat_active_target combat_player_chosen_target
  *else
    *comment The chosen target just died (or the fight shrank under it) -- clear it so the HUD stops
    *comment highlighting a dead slot, and fall through to the ordinary auto-pick below.
    *set combat_player_chosen_target 0
*if (combat_active_target = 0)
  *comment Scans every slot rather than stopping at the first living one (the old front-to-back pick's
  *comment shortcut) -- the lowest-HP slot can be anywhere in the order, so every living slot needs comparing.
  *temp fpt_i 1
  *temp fpt_best_hp 0
  *label fight_pick_target_loop
  *if (fpt_i <= combat_enemy_count)
    *temp fpt_hp {(("combat_enemy" & fpt_i) & "_hp")}
    *if (fpt_hp > 0)
      *if ((combat_active_target = 0) or (fpt_hp < fpt_best_hp))
        *set combat_active_target fpt_i
        *set fpt_best_hp fpt_hp
    *set fpt_i + 1
    *goto fight_pick_target_loop
*if (combat_active_target > 0)
  *gosub_scene combat fight_sync_target_in
*return

*label fight_pick_nearest_target
*comment Movement targeting (2026-09-28): the NEAREST living enemy that's actually eligible for this direction,
*comment not kill-priority -- Push Forward/Fall Back are about closing or opening a specific gap, so "who's
*comment already closest" is the sensible default, not "who's most wounded" (fight_pick_target above, used for
*comment attacks and ally targeting instead). "push" wants the nearest enemy with range > 0 (nothing to gain
*comment closing on one already Engaged); "fallback" wants the nearest with range < 3 (nothing to gain opening on
*comment one already at Long Range) -- an enemy Engaged with you is a valid Fall Back candidate but never a valid
*comment Push Forward one, so different enemies can legitimately win depending on direction. A HUD-clicked target
*comment (combat_player_chosen_target, sticky until it dies) still wins over the auto-pick whenever it's alive
*comment AND eligible for this direction; alive-but-ineligible falls through to the ordinary scan below rather
*comment than silently doing nothing (the click stays sticky for its original purpose, attacks, either way).
*params fpnt_direction
*set combat_active_target 0
*if (combat_player_chosen_target > 0)
  *temp fpnt_chosen_hp {(("combat_enemy" & combat_player_chosen_target) & "_hp")}
  *if ((combat_player_chosen_target > combat_enemy_count) or (fpnt_chosen_hp <= 0))
    *comment Dead, or the fight shrank under the click -- clear it for the same reason fight_pick_target
    *comment does, so the HUD stops highlighting a slot that no longer holds anything.
    *set combat_player_chosen_target 0
  *else
    *comment Alive and in range. Eligibility is checked here but must NEVER clear the click (2026-09-28).
    *comment **This branch used to be written as an inner *if for eligibility with the *else bound to THAT
    *comment if**, so being ineligible for one movement direction silently wiped combat_player_chosen_target
    *comment -- meaning "click the swordsman, then Push forward" forgot who you were attacking, and your next
    *comment swing reverted to kill-priority. The header comment above already promised the opposite ("the
    *comment click stays sticky for its original purpose, attacks, either way"); the code just didn't do it.
    *comment Inverting the test puts the *else back on the liveness check, which is the only thing that
    *comment should ever clear a click. It became materially more reachable once the movement buttons started
    *comment advertising a from/to tier, which invites exactly that click-then-move sequence.
    *temp fpnt_chosen_range {(("combat_enemy" & combat_player_chosen_target) & "_range")}
    *if (((fpnt_direction = "push") and (fpnt_chosen_range > 0)) or ((fpnt_direction = "fallback") and (fpnt_chosen_range < 3)))
      *set combat_active_target combat_player_chosen_target
*if (combat_active_target = 0)
  *temp fpnt_i 1
  *temp fpnt_best_range 0
  *label fight_pick_nearest_target_loop
  *if (fpnt_i <= combat_enemy_count)
    *temp fpnt_hp {(("combat_enemy" & fpnt_i) & "_hp")}
    *if (fpnt_hp > 0)
      *temp fpnt_range {(("combat_enemy" & fpnt_i) & "_range")}
      *temp fpnt_ok false
      *if ((fpnt_direction = "push") and (fpnt_range > 0))
        *set fpnt_ok true

*label combat_lane_move_enemy_relative_to_player
