*comment REUSABLE ENGINE PRIMITIVE: CURRENCY MATH
*comment Adds or spends currency_add_amount (in copper-equivalent units: 10c = 1s, 10s = 1g).
*comment ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
*label currency_add
*temp ca_total ((((gold * 100) + (silver * 10)) + copper) + currency_add_amount)
*if (ca_total < 0)
  *set ca_total 0
*set gold (ca_total - (ca_total modulo 100)) / 100
*temp ca_remainder (ca_total - (gold * 100))
*set silver (ca_remainder - (ca_remainder modulo 10)) / 10
*set copper (ca_remainder - (silver * 10))
*return
