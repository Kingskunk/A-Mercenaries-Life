At the western end of Langston, the lane narrows between close-set timber-framed homes and their fenced gardens. Tomlin's cottage is a sound single-storey house with fresh whitewash and well-kept clay tiles, much like its neighbours. Its windows, however, are almost hidden behind flowerboxes bursting with colour. Vines and flowering shrubs crowd the frontage, and trellises run toward a substantial garden behind the house.

The place is beautiful, carefully maintained, and impossible to mistake for anyone else's home.
