*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Fixed geography only. The long description prints once. Nothing here changes with the season, so the grass and the thatch are never given a colour other than the plain one.
*label bm_place
*if (bm_first_hub)
  Broadmead lies on a broad meadow of damp green grass under a long, bare down, with a shallow stream winding through it and a humped stone bridge across the water. The village runs along one rutted lane beside the stream. Its older houses are dark timber and grey fieldstone under thick thatch, and the newer ones wear slate. Fences of every age divide the grass. Close to the houses, tall post-and-rail fences stand around the best meadow, a brand cut deep into every post. Beyond them, older dry-stone walls and thorn hedges wander up the slopes, and open common runs out past them on the down.

  At the head of the lane a long, low hall of timber and stone sits beside a square of hurdle pens, with rows of weathered boards under its eaves, each burned with a different mark. Across the stream a grey stone lodge and a slate-roofed stable range stand apart from the village, swept and square, a tall blue banner with a golden horse hanging over the lodge door. A low, black-thatched inn faces the lane near the bridge, a halter hanging crooked from its bracket. Below the village the meadow narrows into a long fold of trodden ground, ringed with sheep rails and open-sided barns, and a weathered standing stone rises from a low mound at its far end. Wool, hay, wet earth and wood smoke hang over everything.
*else
  Broadmead's rutted lane runs beside the stream, with the hall and its hurdle pens at the head, the inn near the bridge, the grey lodge across the water and the trodden fair ground in the fold below. The fences run off over the meadow, new rail beside old stone.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, followed by the ground's recent-weather state.
*label bm_sky
*temp bm_dark false
*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *set bm_dark true
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain runs from the thatch in strings and fills the ruts in the lane, and the stream below the bridge has turned brown.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  Wind sweeps down the bare down and flattens the meadow grass. Rain drives sideways along the lane, the thatch hisses, and every fence rattles.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Fog lies thick along the stream and hides the meadow, leaving the nearest fence posts as dark stakes in the white.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow settles on the thatch and along the tops of the walls, and the down above the village has faded to white.
*elseif (weather = "Blizzard")
  Driven snow hides the down, the stream and the far end of the lane. The nearest fence post is the farthest thing you can see.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (bm_dark)
    Starlight lies pale on the meadow, and the long down is a black line against the stars.
  *elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
    The last light turns the wet grass to copper and lays long shadows from the fence posts across the meadow.
  *else
    Pale sun lights the down and the damp meadow, and every fence post throws a sharp shadow.
*else
  *if (bm_dark)
    A starless sky presses down on the village, and the stream is only a sound in the dark.
  *else
    Low grey cloud lies over the down and darkens the thatch, and the wet grass looks very green beneath it.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Brown water stands in the ruts of the lane, and the meadow squelches underfoot.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The lane is dark and slick from the last rain, and the ruts hold shallow water.
*return
