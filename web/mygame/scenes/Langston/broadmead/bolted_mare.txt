[b]◈ THE STABLES[/b]

Sandy brown hair sticks to his damp forehead above anxious hazel eyes, and he wears a coarse wool shirt under a scuffed leather apron. He tenses as you stop beside him, his knuckles whitening on the wooden handle.

"Easy," you tell him. "The sergeant sent me to find the spotted horse, not to call you to account. Tell me how she got loose at the water."

His shoulders drop a fraction. "Myles. Myles Buckman," he says, wiping his brow with a forearm. "I'm from Broadmead, born on the commons, only taken on here this season. If she's hurt or lost for good, the sergeant will let me go before market day."

He lowers his hands, glancing at the empty stall. "She spooks easily at raised sticks or banging wood, mind. Come up low on her left shoulder and talk steady to her neck instead of grabbing at her nose, and she settles quick enough."

Clean ink at the Hall beats cut fences next month. Give the gate guards your name whenever you have business inside this wall."
  *elseif (bm_mare_how = "law")
    You report that a shepherd called Thistle had penned her on the ridge and you brought her out without paying his demand.

    Marren sets four silver marks into your hand. "Your full pay, as promised," she tells you evenly. "The shepherds won't smile at you in the lane for it, though you brought the Countess's animal home unharmed. The troopers at the gate will know your face whenever you need to step inside."
  *elseif (bm_mare_how = "paid")
    You explain that Thistle held her in the village pound on the ridge and you paid his customary fee out of your own purse to bring her down without trouble.

    Marren lifts her scarred eyebrow a fraction, then presses all four coins into your palm. "Three clear for your walk, then, and no bad blood left behind on the hill," she answers. "The guards will let you pass whenever you have reason to call here."
  *elseif (bm_mare_how = "recovered")
    You report that Thistle had penned her on the ridge, and when the confrontation spooked Magpie over the crumbling pound wall, you caught her on the slope before she reached the loose rock.

    Marren brushes stone dust from Magpie's knees and counts three silver marks into your palm instead of four. "Three for bringing the Countess's mare home unharmed," she tells you evenly. "One mark goes up to Mardred's counter to mend the tumbled pound stones before the shepherds take it out on her fences. Give the gate guards your name whenever you have business inside this wall."
  *else

    Marren's grey eyes cool a notch. "Bruised shepherds mean cut fence rails before the month is out," she notes flatly. She lays three silver coins into your hand instead of four. "Three for bringing her home whole, less one for the trouble left on our border. That ends our arrangement."
