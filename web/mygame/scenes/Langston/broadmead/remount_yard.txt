*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Fixed geography only. The long description prints once. Nothing here changes with the season or the hour.
*label bm_ry_place
*if (ry_first)
  Across the stream from the village the ground rises a little, and on it stands the one building in Broadmead that looks as if it was built to a plan. The lodge is a long grey stone house of two storeys under a slate roof, and a tall navy banner hangs over its door, the cloth edged in narrow gold and bearing a single golden horse in profile, one foreleg raised. A stable range runs beside it, its split half-doors painted and shut in even rows. Next to the stables a smithy bay stands open to the yard, with an anvil on a stump and a dull glow in the forge, and next to that a tack room open at the front shows saddles on racks and bridles in lines. The yard between them is raked gravel, swept to the edges. Beyond the buildings a gate opens onto the muster field, where a picket line runs along one side and a straw quintain post stands at the far end.
*else
  The yard is as before: the grey lodge under its banner, the stable range with its shut half-doors, the open smithy bay and tack room, and the raked gravel running out to the gate of the muster field.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, followed by the ground's recent-weather state.
*label bm_ry_sky
*temp ry_dark false
*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *set ry_dark true
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain runs off the slate in clean lines and beads on the gravel, and the banner hangs dark and heavy.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  Wind drives rain across the open yard and snaps the banner out stiff from its pole. Shutters bang on the stable range, and the forge glow shows red in the smithy bay.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Fog fills the yard and softens the stable range to a grey bar, and the banner hangs limp above the door.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow lies on the slate and along the top rail of the picket line, and the gravel has been swept clear in a path from the lodge door to the stables.
*elseif (weather = "Blizzard")
  Driven snow hides the muster field and the far end of the stable range. The banner is a dark shape above the lodge door.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (ry_dark)
    Starlight lies on the slate, and the banner is a dark square above the door.
  *elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
    The last light turns the grey stone gold and lays the shadows of the picket posts long across the gravel.
  *else
    Bright light shows every line of the raked gravel and picks out the gold of the horse on the banner.
*else
  *if (ry_dark)
    A starless sky lies over the yard, and the lodge is a dark mass with a few lit windows.
  *else
    Flat grey light lies over the yard and makes the slate and stone look even greyer.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Puddles stand along the edges of the gravel, and the paths to the stables have been scraped clear of mud.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The gravel is dark from the last rain, and the raked lines hold small beads of water.
*return
