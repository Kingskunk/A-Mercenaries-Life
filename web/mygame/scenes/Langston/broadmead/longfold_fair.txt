*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Fixed geography only. The long description prints once. Nothing here changes with the season or the hour.
*label bm_fair_place
*if (fr_first)
  Below the village the meadow narrows into a long fold, and the fair ground fills it from end to end. Timber hurdles and sheep rails divide the trodden earth into pens, with bare lanes worn between them. At the near end a wool hall stands open on its sides, fleeces and bound bales hanging from its rafters, and a weigh-master in a leather apron works a big iron-framed scale on a timber platform beneath them. Beside it a low stone hall holds dairy stalls and long shelves of cheese wheels, and steps beside its door lead down to a cool undercroft. Two long open sheds, where the shearing is done, stand along one side of the ground. A long stone trough runs down the middle of the fold, fed by a pipe from the stream. At the far end, on a low turf mound, a weathered standing stone rises from a trodden ring, its sun disc worn almost smooth and tufts of wool tied to it with string. It smells of wool grease, milk, dung, hay and wet earth.
*else
  The fair ground is as before: the trodden lanes between the hurdle pens, the open wool hall with its scale, the stone dairy hall, the long trough and the standing stone on its mound at the far end.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, then the ground's memory of recent weather. The fair is shut at night and in dangerous weather, so those cases never print here.
*label bm_fair_sky
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain drums on the wool hall's roof and runs off the sheds in strings, and the lanes between the pens churn to mud.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Fog fills the fold and turns the far pens to grey shapes. The standing stone is a pale shadow at the end of the ground.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow slides off the shed roofs and melts to slush in the lanes, and the standing stone wears a white cap.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (time_period = "Dusk")
    The last light lies low along the fold and turns the trodden lanes and the fleeces gold.
  *else
    Bright light runs the length of the fold and throws sharp shadows from the hurdles across the earth.
*else
  Flat grey light lies over the fold and shows every churned patch in the lanes.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The lanes are churned to deep mud, and water stands in every hoofprint.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The trodden earth is dark and slick in the lanes from the last rain.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 3: STREET ── Who is about and what is laid out. The stock line follows the season; the rest follows the day and the clock. The fair is busiest on Marketday and thin on the rest day.
*label bm_fair_street
*if (current_season = "Spring")
  Shearers kneel over ewes in the long sheds, and bound fleeces pile up at the wool hall door.
*elseif (current_season = "Summer")
  Women carry pails from the dairy stalls, and new cheese wheels sit pressed under stone slabs on the shelves.
*elseif (current_season = "Autumn")
  Drovers pen culled sheep along the rails, and dust and bleating rise over the lanes.
*else
  Few pens are filled. Stored fleeces hang in the wool hall, and carters stack cheeses in straw beside the dairy hall.
*if (time_period = "Dusk")
  Dealers are tying their pens shut and rolling the last cheeses onto carts, and the weigh-master has begun to chalk the day's weights on a board.
  *return
*if (day_of_week = "Marketday")
  The ground is packed. Pens are full to the rails, carts nose in beside the trough, and dealers call lots across the noise of sheep, while the scale on its platform is never still.
  *return
*if (day_of_week = "Hallowday")
  The work stops for the rest day. Only a few pens stay open, and families in their better clothes stand quietly around the mound at the far end.
  *return
*if (fr_vignette = 1)
  A dealer runs a hand down a fleece, then nods to the weigh-master.
*elseif (fr_vignette = 2)
  The weigh-master calls a weight across the hall, and a cart pulls off the platform.
*elseif (fr_vignette = 3)
  A dog holds a pen of ewes against the gate while its owner counts them under his breath.
*elseif (fr_vignette = 4)
  A woman turns a cheese wheel on its shelf and thumps it once with a knuckle.
*elseif (fr_vignette = 5)
  Boys water a pony at the long trough while an older man watches the pony's feet.
*else
  A buyer pins a twist of wool to a tally stick and walks on to the next pen.
*return
