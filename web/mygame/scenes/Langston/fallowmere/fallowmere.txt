*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Fixed geography only. The long description prints once. Nothing here changes with the season, so the thatch, the hedges and the strips are never given a colour other than the plain one.
*label fm_place
*if (fm_first_hub)
  Fallowmere lies on level farmland, round a wide green with a shallow pond in the middle of it. Reeds crowd the banks, and a flock of grey geese picks along the water's edge. At the near side of the green a covered well stands under its own small roof of shingle. Low cottages of whitewashed plaster and dark timber ring the green under deep thatch, each with its brick chimney, its kitchen garden and its wattle fence. Barns and haystacks stand between the houses, and a cart waits in the lane with its shafts on the ground. Beyond the roofs, hedged strips of wheat, barley and beans run flat to the horizon.

  A long, low house under a deep thatch stands at the head of the lane, with a covered bench beneath a lean-to beside its door. Down by the pond a few open trestles stand near a heavy scale on a plank platform. Across the lane a long, low inn opens a wide arch to its cart yard and hangs a painted grey goose from an iron bracket over its door. Hay, wet reeds and wood smoke hang over everything.
*else
  Fallowmere's green lies round its pond, with the covered well at the near side, the long thatched house and its bench at the head of the lane, the trestles and the scale down by the water, and the goose sign across the lane. The strips run off flat on every side.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, followed by the ground's recent-weather state.
*label fm_sky
*temp fm_dark false
*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *set fm_dark true
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain dimples the pond and runs from the thatch in strings, and the ruts in the lane fill with brown water.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  Wind flattens the reeds and drives rain across the green. The pond heaves brown, the thatch hisses, and every shutter bangs.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Fog lies on the pond and hides the far strips, leaving the nearest cottages as grey shapes.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow settles on the thatch and on the roof of the well, and the strips beyond the hedges have faded to white.
*elseif (weather = "Blizzard")
  Driven snow hides the pond and the far end of the lane. The well roof is the farthest thing in sight.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (fm_dark)
    Starlight lies on the pond, and the strips beyond the hedges are dark silvery shapes.
  *elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
    The last light turns the pond to copper and lays long shadows from the hedges across the green.
  *else
    Soft light lies on the green, and the pond holds the thatched roofs upside down.
*else
  *if (fm_dark)
    A starless sky presses down on the village, and the pond is a black patch ringed with a few lit windows.
  *else
    Flat grey light lies over the green and makes the thatch look darker.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Brown water stands in the ruts of the lane, and the green squelches underfoot.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The lane is dark and slick from the last rain, and the ruts hold shallow water.
*return
