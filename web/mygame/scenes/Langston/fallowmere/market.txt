*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Fixed geography only. The long description prints once. Nothing here changes with the season or the hour.
*label fm_market_place
*if (mk_first)
  The market stands on the trodden grass of the green, between the pond and the lane. In the middle a heavy two-pan scale hangs from a timber frame on a plank platform, and round it stand a few trestle tables under canvas awnings, their legs sunk into the turf. The tables carry sacks of grain with their mouths rolled down, baskets of dried beans and greens, coils of rope and bundles of tool handles, and a barrel stands by the nearest post. Reeds and grey water lie behind the stalls, and the thatched cottages close the far side of the green. It smells of grain dust, wet reeds and hemp.
*else
  The market is as before: the trestles under their awnings, the heavy scale on its platform, the barrel by the near post, and the pond and reeds behind.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, then the ground's memory of recent weather. The market is shut at night and in dangerous weather, so those cases never print here.
*label fm_market_sky
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain drums on the canvas awnings and dimples the pond, and the grass between the trestles is trodden to mud.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Fog lies on the pond and softens the far stalls to grey shapes.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow slides off the awnings and melts to slush between the trestles.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (time_period = "Dusk")
    The last light lies low across the pond and turns the canvas and the sacks gold.
  *else
    Soft light lies across the green, and the pond throws a wavering shine up under the awnings.
*else
  Flat grey light lies on the awnings and the water.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The grass between the trestles is churned to mud, and water stands in every footprint.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The grass is dark and slick between the trestles from the last rain.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 3: STREET ── Who is about and what is laid out. The scale-keeper is always at the platform in market hours. The market is crowded on the weekly market day and thin on the others.
*label fm_market_street
*if (time_period = "Dusk")
  Sellers stack their trestles against the barrel and tie canvas over the sacks, and the scale-keeper locks the brass weights back in their box.
  *return
*if (day_of_week = "Marketday")
  The green is crowded. Carts nose in beside the pond, geese shriek along the bank, and sellers call their goods across one another while the scale on its platform is never still.
  *return
*if (time_period = "Morning")
  Only a few tables are laid out yet. A stout woman in a canvas apron unlocks a box of brass weights at the foot of the scale platform, and two sellers shake out their awnings.
  *return
*if (mk_vignette = 1)
  A stout woman in a canvas apron weighs a sack of beans on the scale, nods, and chalks the weight on the sack.
*elseif (mk_vignette = 2)
  A man haggles over a coil of rope, walks off, and comes back.
*elseif (mk_vignette = 3)
  A girl sells eggs from a basket set on an upturned crate and counts coppers into her apron.
*elseif (mk_vignette = 4)
  The scale-keeper checks the two pans against a set of brass weights, then sets them swinging again.
*elseif (mk_vignette = 5)
  A carter loads sacks onto his cart beside the platform while the scale-keeper calls out the weights.
*else
  A goose escapes its basket, and two boys chase it round the barrel.
*return
