Voice: fast looping sentences that double back and correct themselves ("I mean", "no, sorry", "well"), "Right" and "Sorry" often, he asks leave before a blunt remark. NEVER an em dash (the game's prose avoids them). KEEP THE LOOPS SHORT: at most one long tangled sentence per reply, then a short plain one.
*comment He flushes and thanks twice at a compliment. Backstory (roleplay sheet): eldest son of the previous head, a year learning to survey in Langston, stayed when his father died and the rolls turned out to be in the two of them. He keeps the Hall's Contract Board (fm_hall_board).

  You step up to the plank counter. He drops the cord, catches it on his wrist and winds it back round his hand.

"What can you tell me about Fallowmere?" you ask.

"Well, we feed Langston." He says it like an apology. "Wheat, barley, beans, some greens. Fewer turnips this season, the ditch flooded, I'm sorry about that, I should have had it cleared." He tugs the cord. "It's dull, if I may say so. But if a strip is out by half an ell, the miller argues with the carter, and nobody eats in the city."

"So you keep the strips straight?"

"I do. It isn't hard. It's just, ah, endless."
*goto fm_hl_talk_menu

*label fm_hl_talk_post
*set fm_hl_asked_post true
*set minutes_to_pass 5
*set time_advance_call_id "fm_hl_talk_post"
*gosub_scene calendar advance_time
"How did you come to be village head?" you ask.

"My father was the head before me." He ducks his head. "A great booming man. Everyone listened to him." The cord winds round two fingers. "I spent a year in Langston learning to survey. I meant to draw maps, the high ridges and the rivers, proper charts. Then he died, and when the elders opened the chest, every field roll was in his hand, or mine from when I was a boy. They asked me to see out the autumn rent."

"And then?"

"Then it was spring sowing." A short laugh. "I never quite left."
*goto fm_hl_talk_menu

*label fm_hl_talk_duties
*set fm_hl_asked_duties true
*set minutes_to_pass 5
*set time_advance_call_id "fm_hl_talk_duties"
*gosub_scene calendar advance_time
"What does a village head do?" you ask.

"Rolls, mostly." He pats the shelf behind him. "Who farms which strip, who sold what to whom, who owes the road, the ditch, the carting. If two neighbours disagree about a boundary, they wait on the bench outside, and I go and measure." He holds up the cord. "The moot says what it thinks. Then I give the ruling under House Gault's seal, and answer for it at the heads' meeting in Langston Hall."

"How does that go?"

"I'm told I'm behind." He winces. "I am, though."
*goto fm_hl_talk_menu

*label fm_hl_talk_supplies
*set fm_hl_asked_supplies true
*set minutes_to_pass 5
*set time_advance_call_id "fm_hl_talk_supplies"
*gosub_scene calendar advance_time
"Where can I buy supplies for the road?" you ask.

"The green, by the pond." He points the cord at the door. "There are trestles on market days. Dried beans, coarse meal, rope, greens. Nothing rare, I'm afraid." He pauses. "For anything finer you want Langston's market. And the Gander across the lane will feed you, Ione's pottage is, well, hearty."
*goto fm_hl_talk_menu

*label fm_hl_talk_praise
*set fm_hl_praised true
*set minutes_to_pass 5
*set time_advance_call_id "fm_hl_talk_praise"

"The village is lucky to have you," you say.

Aubrey Fenn freezes with the cord half wound, and the colour climbs to the tips of his ears. "Oh. Well, that's, that's remarkably generous, truly." He rubs the back of his neck with an ink-stained hand. "Thank you. Thank you. Most people only speak up when their ditch overflows." He clears his throat and straightens a roll that did not need it. "Right. What else can I do for you?"
