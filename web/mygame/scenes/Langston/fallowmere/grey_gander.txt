"Where can a traveller earn some coin?" you ask.

"Nowhere, unless you want to swing a scythe." Ione turns the mug a quarter turn. "Reapers get two coppers a day and blisters."

"And for someone who doesn't want blisters?"

"Hall's got a board by the study door. Courier work, small. Aubrey Fenn keeps it." She nods toward the long thatched house at the head of the lane. "He'll tell you in twenty minutes what I've told you in two." A pause. "Hm. Anything bigger is Langston Hall."

*label fm_gg_talk_self
*set fm_gg_asked_self true

"How did you come to run the Gander?" you ask.

"Potgirl," Ione says. "The old landlord took me in when I was small. My father was a reaper, and a winter chest took him twenty years back." She works the rag round the rim. "The old man kept the taps ten years more. Then he left me the house, the cask and no debts."

"And your mother?"

She does not answer at once. "Passing trade," she says, and slides the dish of beans a hand's width closer to you.
*goto fm_gg_talk_menu

"What are the dice and the slate on the shelf for?" you ask.

"Carters bring them down after dark." Ione does not look at the shelf. "Small stakes, one pot. The slate says who owes what." She wipes the brass of the tap dry. "Nobody loses money until the sun's behind the barn."
