*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Fixed geography only. The long description prints once.
*label lg_place
*if (lg_first_hub)
  Langston stands close within its stone wall. Two- and three-storey timber-framed houses crowd the cobbled streets, their upper floors jutting over shopfronts, covered passages and narrow side lanes. The main street opens into a broad market square built around a weathered stone fountain. Covered stalls and wagon bays occupy the gateward side, while the surrounding buildings rise in uneven rows of white plaster, dark beams and steep tiled roofs.

  A wide flight of shallow steps climbs from the square toward the older part of town. The town hall stands there in plain dressed stone, smaller than the buildings packed around it but heavier in its construction. Beyond it, a tall chapel tower marks the upper skyline. From the higher lanes, the roofs fall away toward the wall and the open Western Vale beyond: broad pasture and cultivated fields, with the old forest lying along the distant southern horizon.

  Hemp fibre, horse sweat, hearth smoke and fresh bread mingle in the square. Wheel rims have worn pale tracks across the cobbles between the gate, the fountain and the market bays. Langston may serve the farms surrounding it, but within the walls it feels like a compact trading town rather than a country village.
*else
  The market square remains Langston's centre: the stone fountain below the stepped upper streets, the gate and wagon bays to one side, and the chapel tower above a dense field of timbered roofs. Traffic from the Western Vale road keeps pale wheel marks on the cobbles.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, followed by the ground's recent-weather state.
*label lg_sky
*temp lg_dark false
*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *set lg_dark true
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain darkens the timber frames and rattles on the tiled roofs, running down the stepped lanes toward the gate drains.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  Wind drives rain across the square and sends water spilling from the roof gutters in sheets. The chapel tower disappears whenever the weather closes in.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Mist fills the lower streets and softens the upper storeys, leaving the chapel tower suspended above the roofs.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow gathers along the rooflines and stair edges while grey slush fills the wheel tracks around the fountain.
*elseif (weather = "Blizzard")
  Driven snow sweeps between the buildings and hides the far side of the square behind a shifting white curtain.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (lg_dark)
    Clear starlight outlines the chapel tower and steep roofs while lanterns burn along the main street and around the fountain.
  *elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
    The last light catches the upper windows and chapel tower while the square below settles into shadow.
  *else
    Clear light falls across the pale cobbles, white plaster and dark timber fronts around the square.
*else
  *if (lg_dark)
    A starless sky hangs above the roofs, leaving the upper streets to window light and the town's lanterns.
  *else
    Flat grey light lies over the roofs and brings out the dark lines of every timbered frontage.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Water still runs from the upper lanes after days of rain, and the wheel tracks around the square remain full.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The cobbles are still dark from the last rain, with shallow water gathered around the fountain curb.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 3: STREET ── Current town activity. Murder-state unease supersedes ordinary traffic.
*label lg_street
*if (street_life = "empty")
  *gosub lg_st_empty
*elseif ((wol_quest_stage = "offered") or (wol_quest_stage = "active"))
  *gosub lg_st_uneasy
*elseif (time_period = "Pre-Dawn")
  *gosub lg_st_predawn
*elseif (time_period = "Night")
  *gosub lg_st_night
*elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
  *gosub lg_st_dusk
*elseif (day_of_week = "Hallowday")
  *gosub lg_st_rest
*elseif (day_of_week = "Marketday")
  *gosub lg_st_market
*else
  *gosub lg_st_day
*return

*label lg_st_day
Wagons pass between the gate and market bays while porters shift wool bales, small casks and bound hemp from tailboards to covered scales. Townspeople cross the square around them with baskets, tools and folded account sheets, and Watch officers keep the fountain lane clear enough for through traffic.
*return

*label lg_st_market
The market square is packed with wagons. Hemp bales and wool bundles stand in counted rows beneath the covered bays, small wine casks wait under canvas, and drovers argue over space at the watering trough while clerks carry tallies between the scales and surrounding counting rooms.
*return

*label lg_st_rest
Most shutters remain closed for the rest day, and families cross the square in their better clothes as the chapel bell sounds above the roofs. A few food sellers work beneath the covered bays, serving people who have come in from the surrounding farms.
*return

*label lg_st_dusk
Porters rope the last wagon covers while stallholders carry boxes beneath the market awnings. Watch officers narrow the gate traffic to one lane, and lamplighters move outward from the square as upper windows begin to glow.
*return

*label lg_st_night
The market bays are shuttered and the fountain carries louder than the remaining voices. Pairs of Watch officers walk between the gate and upper steps while light and muted conversation escape from a few late-closing public rooms.
*return

*label lg_st_predawn
The town is dark except for gate lanterns, watch lamps and the first glow from bakehouse windows. A few wagons wait outside the market bays while their drivers sleep beneath the carts or tend quiet teams.
*return

*label lg_st_empty
The market awnings have been tied down and the square left almost bare. Shutters cover the lower windows, and Watch officers shelter beneath the gate arch and in the stepped passages while the weather moves through the town.
*return

*label lg_st_uneasy
People still cross the square, but few linger. Conversations fall quiet when strangers pass, doors close earlier than their owners intend, and Watch officers patrol in pairs between the gate, market bays and upper streets while Langston waits for word about the killing.
*return
