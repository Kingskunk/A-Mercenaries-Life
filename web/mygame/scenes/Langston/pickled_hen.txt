*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Permanent common-room geography and sensory character.
*label lg_hen_place
*if (hen_first)
  The Pickled Hen occupies a broad timber-framed house just off the market square. Its painted sign shows a white hen perched on the rim of a green preserving crock. Inside, a long counter faces rows of close-set tables and benches. A wide hearth fills the back wall, with an iron cooking pot on its crane and strings of onions and herbs drying from the beam above it. A narrow stair beside the counter climbs to the rooms on the upper floor. The house smells of bread, broth, lamp oil and beeswax rubbed into old boards.
*else
  The Pickled Hen is warm behind its painted sign, with the hearth at the back, close-set tables across the common room and the narrow stair to the rooms beside the counter.
*return
