*label wol_ch2_doorstep
You knock. Muffled voices and the sound of heavy furniture shifting answer from within. After a few seconds they stop. You lift your hand to knock again, but the door opens beneath your knuckles.

Tomlin and a young woman stand on the other side. Both have mussed hair and red faces. Tomlin speaks first.

"I saw you at Maybelline's service, didn't I? What—what can I do for you?"

You tell him Mayor Anathram hired you to investigate Maybelline's death.

"If you'll excuse me," the woman says, "I'd best be going."

"Don't forget the medicine," Tomlin says, taking up a sachet of dried herbs and cutting a glance toward you. "For your aunt."

"I'd nearly forgotten," she says. She accepts it gingerly, thanks him, and hurries down the street. Tomlin watches her go, plainly deciding what to say next.

When you confirm it, he steps aside. "Please. Come in. I'm sure you have many questions."
