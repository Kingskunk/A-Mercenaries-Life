*comment ====================================================================================
*comment ROI'S GROVE: the forest tile south-west of Langston (overworld-data.js "rg").
*comment Reached from the map's "Go into the grove" button (overworld.txt). While the Wolves of Langston is active and Chapter 3 is not complete, the button hands the player to the
*comment Chapter 3 file (wolves_of_langston_chapter_3.txt: the silent wood, the wolf cold open, the stirges, the grove and Roi), whose resume point picks up from the saved checkpoint. This file owns the place itself: the track
*comment and trees (rg_place), the sky under the canopy (rg_sky, which Chapter 3 reuses) and the grove. This is an open-world place that can be entered at any hour in any weather.
*comment ====================================================================================

*label rg_arrive
*set scenery_subplace "rois_grove"
*gosub_scene calendar update_hub_condition
*if ((wol_quest_stage = "active") and ((wol_chapter_2_complete) and (not(wol_chapter_3_complete))))
  *goto_scene Langston/wolves_of_langston_chapter_3 wol_ch3_start
*goto rg_grove

*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── The track and the trees, always true.
*label rg_place
Past the last field the track turns into the trees. It is two ruts with a strip of grass between them, then a footpath where the roots crowd in. The trunks are old and broad, and the air smells of leaf litter and wet bark.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and the light under the canopy, closing with the ground's memory of recent rain.
*label rg_sky
*if (weather_severity >= 3)
  *if (weather_cold)
    Snow drives between the trunks and the canopy roars overhead.
  *else
    Wind thrashes the canopy and rain comes through it in heavy gusts.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Mist lies between the trunks, and the far end of the track dissolves into grey.
*elseif (weather = "Rain")
  Rain patters through the leaves and drips from every branch.
*elseif (weather = "Sleet")
  Sleet ticks on the leaves and bounces off the roots.
*elseif (weather = "Snow")
  Snow sifts down between the branches and thins to a few flakes by the time it reaches the ground.
*elseif ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *if (weather = "Clear")
    Moonlight finds the track in a few pale patches, and the rest is black trunks.
  *else
    The canopy shuts out the sky, and the track is a paler strip in the dark.
*elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
  The light is low and orange under the trees, and the shadows between the trunks have run together.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  Sunlight falls through the leaves in moving patches.
*else
  Grey light filters down through the canopy and lays no shadows.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The ruts hold standing water from the last long rain.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The ground is soft underfoot from recent rain.
*return

*comment ── THE GROVE ── Every entry after Chapter 3 opens on the sky layer, then the clearing, then Roi (rg_roi). Roi has no conversation menu yet; the grove is a look-in.
*label rg_grove
*set scenery_subplace "rois_grove"
*gosub rg_sky

*label rg_grove_edge
The old trees stand back from a clear patch of ground, and the track runs out into it. A rock overhang shelters a bed of hides and a ring of blackened stones.
*gosub rg_roi
*page_break Walk back to the track…
*goto_scene overworld/overworld ow_here

*comment ── WHERE ROI IS TODAY ── Sets roi_where from the campaign day alone, so a day reads the same on every look and after a reload. h cycles through 0..15 (5 is coprime to 16, so the away days are scattered, never in a run):
*comment h under 4 is a wandering day (one in four); h = 0 is a market day (one in sixteen: he walks to Langston and back). Used by rg_roi here and by the market square in langston.txt (lg_roi_sighting). replay-ok: sets a display variable.
*label rg_roi_where
*temp roi_h (((campaign_day * 5) + 2) modulo 16)
*set roi_where "grove"
*if (roi_h < 4)
  *set roi_where "wandering"
*if (roi_h = 0)
  *set roi_where "market"
*return

*comment ── LAYER 3: ROI'S DAY ── Where the druid is and what he is doing, by the hour; his own hours, invented (the source gives him none). Wet or stormy weather sends him under the overhang through the day, and the night finds him asleep whatever
*comment the sky does. Every line is a continuous activity (no one-off gestures), so a return on any day reads true. Roi is cured on every path to this label (rg_arrive sends the player to Chapter 3 until it is complete). replay-ok: display only.
*label rg_roi
*gosub rg_roi_where
*if (roi_where != "grove")
  Ash lies grey and cold in the fire ring, the hides are folded flat on the rock shelf, and the hook where the bark basket hangs at the edge of the overhang is bare. A narrow trail of bent ferns leads off between the trunks.
  *return
*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  Roi lies curled in his bed of hides beneath the overhang, one arm flung over a bundle of dried stems. His breath comes slow and even, and the stones of the fire ring give off a last dull red.
  *return
*if ((weather_severity >= 3) or weather_wet)
  *if (time_period = "Dusk")
    Roi sits close to the fire under the overhang with the hides pulled round his shoulders, feeding it split sticks one at a time.
  *else
    Roi sits under the overhang with a heap of bark across his knees, tearing it into long strips and twisting them into cord. A pot of something green ticks on the coals beside him.
  *return
*if (time_period = "Morning")
  Roi crouches at the edge of the clearing among the saplings, one palm flat against a trunk. A pot of something green steams on the fire behind him.
*elseif (time_period = "Midday")
  Roi sits cross-legged on the flat rock at the centre of the clearing with leaves spread over his knees, sorting them into bundles by size and tying each with a twist of grass.
*elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
  Roi kneels at the fire ring under the overhang, banking the coals with ash and laying the day's bundles along the rock wall to dry.
*else
  Roi works his way along the edge of the clearing with a bark basket on his arm, stopping at the roots of the older trunks to turn over the leaf litter.
*if (time_period != "Dusk")
  He lifts a hand as you stop at the edge of the trees, and returns to his work.
*return
