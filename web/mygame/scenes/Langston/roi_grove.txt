*comment ROI'S GROVE: the forest tile south-west of Langston (overworld-data.js "rg").
*comment Reached from the map's "Go into the grove" button (overworld.txt). The first entry plays the wolf cold open: something follows the player along the track and shows itself
*comment on a bank, a forest version of the night scene on the inn roof in the source (Chapter 2, sections 60-96): the hunched shape with the outstretched arm, "He's mine!", the laugh,
*comment the claw marks and the hair that crumbles into a shimmer. There is no fight and no sleep. After it, the player reaches the grove's edge. Roi's meeting is the next piece.
*comment This is an open-world scene: it can be entered at any hour in any weather, so every layer (place, sky, light on the shape) branches on time_period and weather.
*comment ====================================================================================

*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── The track and the trees, always true.
*label rg_place
Past the last field the track turns into the trees. It is two ruts with a strip of grass between them, then a footpath where the roots crowd in. The trunks are old and broad, and the air smells of leaf litter and wet bark.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and the light under the canopy, closing with the ground's memory of recent rain.
*label rg_sky
*if (weather_severity >= 3)
  *if (weather_cold)
    Snow drives between the trunks and the canopy roars overhead.
  *else
    Wind thrashes the canopy and rain comes through it in heavy gusts.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Mist lies between the trunks, and the far end of the track dissolves into grey.
*elseif (weather = "Rain")
  Rain patters through the leaves and drips from every branch.
*elseif (weather = "Sleet")
  Sleet ticks on the leaves and bounces off the roots.
*elseif (weather = "Snow")
  Snow sifts down between the branches and thins to a few flakes by the time it reaches the ground.
*elseif ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *if (weather = "Clear")
    Moonlight finds the track in a few pale patches, and the rest is black trunks.
  *else
    The canopy shuts out the sky, and the track is a paler strip in the dark.
*elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
  The light is low and orange under the trees, and the shadows between the trunks have run together.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  Sunlight falls through the leaves in moving patches.
*else
  Grey light filters down through the canopy and lays no shadows.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The ruts hold standing water from the last long rain.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The ground is soft underfoot from recent rain.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 3: THE LIGHT ON THE SHAPE ── How the same figure looks in each hour and weather. Never names the sun at night or a moon under cloud.
*label rg_shape_light
*if (weather_severity >= 3)
  The weather tears at the branches, and each time they thrash apart you see it again, outlined and still.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Mist hangs between you and the bank, and the shape stands half inside it.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Snow sifts down on its shoulders and lies there without melting.
*elseif (weather = "Rain")
  Rain runs down the bank and over the shape, and it does not shake the water off.
*elseif ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *if (weather = "Clear")
    The moon has found a gap in the canopy and rims the shape in silver.
  *else
    There is no moon to see it by, only a darker outline against the dark trunks.
*elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
  The last low light runs along its outline like the edge of a coal.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  Sunlight through the leaves rims it in gold and leaves its face in shadow.
*else
  The flat grey light shows its outline and nothing of its face.
*return

*label rg_cold_open
*gosub rg_place
*gosub rg_sky

You have walked some way along it when you notice that the wood is following you. A branch sways that the wind did not move. A patch of leaf litter ahead of you is pressed flat, and when you reach it the print is gone. Twice, something keeps pace with you off to your left, always just out of sight. Each time you stop and turn, the trees are only trees.

Then the track bends round a bank of earth and roots, and you stop for a third time.

Something stands on top of the bank ten paces ahead: a hunched, hairy shape, bigger than any dog and too upright for a wolf. One long arm is stretched toward you, and it waits there as if you ought to take what it is offering.

*gosub rg_shape_light
Its eyes burn gold.

*choice
  # Call out to it.
    *set wol_wolf_choice "called_out"
    You call out and ask what it wants. The arm lowers, and the shape keeps its powerful body close to the top of the bank, bristling with hair that may be hide or may be its own.

    "He's mine!" it snarls, with the force of a whip cracking.

    A low growl rolls through the trees, and the shape drops back over the bank. It does not show itself again.
    *goto rg_after_wolf
  # Shout for help.
    *set wol_wolf_choice "called_for_help"
    You shout for anyone who can hear you. The golden eyes narrow, and you brace for it to come down the bank. Instead its shoulders start to shake. It is laughing at you.

    It slips back over the bank while your own voice dies among the trunks. Nobody answers.
    *goto rg_after_wolf
  # Go after it.@{show_stat_hints  [Athletics or Survival DC 12]|}
    *set wol_wolf_choice "gave_chase"
    *goto rg_climb

*comment After a call, the player can still go up the bank or walk on; after a chase the climb is the first thing they do.
*label rg_after_wolf
*choice
  # Climb the bank to where it stood.@{show_stat_hints  [Athletics or Survival DC 12]|}
    *goto rg_climb
  # Walk on toward the grove.
    *goto rg_walk_on
  # Stay where you are and watch the trees for a while.
    The wood settles around you. Small sounds come back one at a time: dripping, creaking, leaves shifting, and nothing else moves among the trunks. When you have waited long enough to be sure, you let out your breath.
    *goto rg_walk_on

You go on along the track. Whatever it is has marked out this ground, and the feeling of being watched stays between your shoulder blades until the trees thin ahead and open onto the grove.
