*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── The temple itself, always true.
*label tst_place
The sun temple is easy to find, since it towers over every other building in Langston and pierces the sky on the eastern edge of town. Unlike the timber walls and whitewash around it, the temple is built of limestone, scoured smooth by centuries of rain and wind. It stood here long before the rest of Langston, and it will likely stand long after Langston is lost to history.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light on the limestone, closing with the ground's memory of recent rain.
*label tst_sky
*if (weather_severity >= 3)
  *if (weather_cold)
    Snow drives against the pale stone, and the spire vanishes into the storm overhead.
  *else
    Rain lashes the limestone in sheets, and the spire is lost in low cloud.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Fog wraps the lower walls, and the spire rises out of it like a pale finger.
*elseif (weather = "Rain")
  Rain darkens the limestone to a wet grey and runs from the spire's sunburst in thin streams.
*elseif (weather = "Sleet")
  Sleet ticks against the stone and gathers in the carved joints of the walls.
*elseif (weather = "Snow")
  Snow settles on the courtyard's tile roof and clings in the hollows of the carving.
*elseif ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *if (weather = "Clear")
    The limestone is pale under the stars, and the spire cuts a clean line across them.
  *else
    The limestone is a ghostly grey in the dark, and the spire fades into the cloud above.
*elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
  The low light turns the limestone gold, and the spire throws a long shadow across the courtyard.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  The limestone shines in the sun, and the spire's sunburst throws back a point of light.
*else
  Grey light lies flat across the limestone, and the spire stands pale against the cloud.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Standing water fills the low joints of the courtyard paving.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The paving is dark from recent rain.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 3: THE COURTYARD ── The altar and the seats around it, constant.
*label tst_courtyard
The courtyard in front of the temple holds an altar left open to the sky, surrounded by theater-style seating under a tile roof that offers shade or shelter from the rain. The top of the altar carries the same sunburst symbol as the spire.
*return
