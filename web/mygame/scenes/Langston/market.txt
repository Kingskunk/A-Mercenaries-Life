*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Fixed geography only. The long description prints once. Nothing here changes with the season or the hour.
*label lg_market_place
*if (mk_first)
  The market bays fill the gateward side of the square, a cobbled ground strewn with straw and pale with wheel tracks. Long open-sided sheds under deep tiled roofs and rows of canvas-covered stalls stand round a stone well-house with a small roof of its own. Beside it a tall timber frame carries a pair of scale pans on chains, each wide enough to take a sack at a time. Wagon bays open between the sheds, barrels, crates and baskets stand against every post, and sacks lie in counted rows. Narrow doors in the buildings round the ground lead to counting rooms, and above the roofs the chapel spire stands over the timbered upper storeys. The ground smells of straw, wool grease, hemp and wet stone.
*else
  The market bays are as before: the long sheds and canvas stalls round the well-house, the hanging scale on its timber frame, the wagon bays and the counted rows of sacks and barrels under the chapel spire.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, then the ground's memory of recent weather. The market is shut at night and in dangerous weather, so those cases never print here.
*label lg_market_sky
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain drums on the shed roofs and runs off the canvas in strings, and the cobbles between the bays shine.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Mist fills the bays and softens the far sheds, leaving the chapel spire standing over the roofs.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow gathers on the canvas and slides in lumps from the shed roofs, and grey slush fills the wheel tracks.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (time_period = "Dusk")
    The last light lies low across the cobbles and turns the straw and the canvas gold.
  *else
    Clear light falls through the open sheds and lies across the cobbles and the hanging scale.
*else
  Flat grey light lies over the sheds and the pale cobbles.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Water stands in the wheel tracks between the bays, and the straw is trodden to a wet brown mat.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The cobbles are dark from the last rain, and the straw clings to them in wet strands.
*return
