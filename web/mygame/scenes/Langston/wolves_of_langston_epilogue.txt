*if (weather_wet)
  Awnings are cranked out over the stalls, and neighbors argue over prices beneath them while the rain runs off the edges.

People still recognize you. Some offer thanks, some stare, and others go out of their way to behave as though nothing unusual happened. Langston has not forgotten the wolf in its square, but it has not stopped living because of her either.

The young clerk from the Hall catches your attention near the entrance.

"Mayor Anathram asked me to tell you that she and Roi have an account of everything that followed," she says. "She said there's no hurry. They'll see you whenever you have the time."

Mayor Anathram receives you in her office.
*if (weather_wet)
  Rain ticks against the shutters.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Fog presses pale against the window glass.
*elseif (time_period = "Morning")
  Morning light lies across her desk.
*elseif (time_period = "Midday")

*else
  Afternoon light slants across the floorboards.

Roi sits in the armchair beside her desk, and Buttercup has settled wherever she is the greatest inconvenience to everyone else.

You take the chair across from the desk and tell them you are listening.

"The murder of Maybelline is solved," the mayor says. "Solaine is in custody, her curse is broken, and the town has begun putting its affairs back in order. You deserve to know what followed."

Roi nods. "Healing, testimony, repairs, and a great deal of paperwork. Quiet work, mostly, though no less necessary."

The mayor opens a folder of reports from the militia, the temple and the people caring for the wounded.

"There are several matters you deserve to hear about," she says.

*label wol_ep_tomlin
*set wol_ep_asked_tomlin true
You ask how Tomlin and the wounded militia members are recovering.

"Tomlin lives," Roi says. "He's awake, eating, complaining about the food, and trying to convince everyone that he can walk farther than the length of his room."

The mayor's mouth tightens in what might be relief. "The militia members injured in the square are expected to recover as well. Their families have them under close watch, which may be a harsher sentence than bed rest."

"Tomlin's at his cottage," Roi says, "under orders to stay off his feet. A few days yet before he's trusted with a pot."
*if (wol_ch6_tomlin_tended)

  "He knows who kept him alive," Roi adds. "Once he's steady enough for visitors, I expect he'll want to thank you himself."
*else

  "He knows you ran in after him," Roi adds. "Once he's steady enough for visitors, I expect he'll want to thank you himself."

*goto wol_ep_menu

*label wol_ep_solaine
*set wol_ep_asked_solaine true
You ask what will happen to Solaine.

"Solaine remains human," the mayor says. "The Silverstar broke the curse. It did not erase what she chose to do while she carried it."

"The cell's been repaired and reinforced," Roi says. "Two militia members stand watch, with shifts arranged so no exhausted guard is ever left alone. She's been given plain clothes, food, water and treatment for the wounds she took below the temple."

He rubs his beard. "Sometimes she answers questions. Sometimes she prays. Sometimes she just sits there. She hasn't changed again, and she hasn't tried to run."

Mayor Anathram has sent a full report to the Church of the True Sun and to its Order of Radiant Light, but she has not handed Solaine over because the Church claims her as one of its own. "Langston will hear what the Church proposes," she says, "and Solaine will answer for Maybelline's murder."

*goto wol_ep_menu

*label wol_ep_temple
*set wol_ep_asked_temple true
You ask whether Langston is safe without its True Sun cleric.

"Veyra's ward didn't vanish the moment Solaine was removed," Roi says. "It's weakened, not gone. The Church of the True Sun assures us that a new caretaker from the Order of Radiant Light can arrive before that becomes dangerous."

"Until then, the temple and its mortuary are watched," the mayor says. "Roi has organized the militia and several volunteers into shifts, and every body awaiting burial has someone beside it."

Roi nods. "The Order has acknowledged the mayor's message and confirmed that a new caretaker is already on the road."

"We are taking precautions," Mayor Anathram says. "If the Church keeps its word, the ward will have a keeper well before it fails. If it does not, Langston will decide what must be done."

*goto wol_ep_menu

*label wol_ep_maybelline
*set wol_ep_asked_maybelline true
You ask about Maybelline's family and the town's dead.

"I've told Maybelline's parents that her killer was found and taken," the mayor says. "Roi has offered to speak with them about her burial and any rites they still want observed. They haven't asked you to carry another message or to reopen the investigation."

"They've lost a daughter," Roi says. "Answers don't make that loss smaller. But they know the town didn't forget her, and they know the person responsible will answer for it."

He looks toward the window. "Fresh flowers keep showing up at her grave. Some came from her parents, some from people in town, and some from people who leave only the flowers. The colors brighten the raw earth, and more appear whenever the old stems begin to wilt."

"You are welcome to visit," the mayor says. "That is remembrance, not an obligation."

*goto wol_ep_menu

*comment The close (source 366), then the hub. wol_ep_seen only means this conversation was heard. One time advance, on the page that ends it.
*label wol_ep_end
*set wol_ep_seen true
When there is nothing more you wish to ask, Mayor Anathram closes the folder.

"Then the account is settled," she says. "Langston remembers what you did."

Roi pushes himself out of the armchair. "You'll find me at the grove, usually."

The mayor has opened the next folder on her desk before you reach the door. Buttercup follows you as far as the threshold, then returns to her mat at a word from the mayor.

You step back into the market square.
*if (weather_wet)
  Rain drums on the stall awnings, and the ordinary noise of Langston closes around you.
*else
  A hawker is arguing over the price of onions, the well rope creaks, and the ordinary noise of Langston closes around you.
