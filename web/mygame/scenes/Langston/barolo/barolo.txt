*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Fixed geography only. The long description prints once. Nothing here changes with the season, so the vines are never given a colour.
*label ba_place
*if (ba_first_hub)
  Barolo climbs a low pale hill, one of the few real slopes in the Vale. Vines cover the sunward side in tight rows, and small orchards, hedges and white beehives break them up wherever the ground flattens. Beaten-earth lanes run between stone walls, and the village's houses of pale stone and red tile gather along the one that climbs the middle of the hill.

  Where the road from Langston comes in, a stone arch spans the cart way beside a small lodge and a cellar with an iron-banded door. A painted shield hangs above the arch, and casks wait beneath it to be looked over. Past the arch the lane opens into a paved yard under tiled canopies, with a raised scale at its centre. Across from it a broad stone barn stands open to a cart yard, with heavy wooden wine presses showing in the dark inside. A two-storey inn of stone and timber faces the lane behind a covered carriage arch, a red-painted barrel stave hanging over its door.

  Three ways leave the village among the vines. One climbs in walled steps toward an old stone house on the high ground. A wide lane worn by wheels runs toward a busy yard of stacked casks. A narrow lower lane threads between small plots and working courtyards. From the top of the hill the Vale spreads out below, and manor roofs show here and there among the long rows. The air smells of warm earth, beeswax, wood smoke and old wine soaking into oak.
*else
  Barolo's main lane climbs between pale walls, with the paved yard and the arch below it and the inn across from the press barn. The vine rows run away on every side, and the three ways out lead off among them under the manor roofs on the hill.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, followed by the ground's recent-weather state.
*label ba_sky
*temp ba_dark false
*if ((time_period = "Night") or (time_period = "Pre-Dawn"))
  *set ba_dark true
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain darkens the pale walls and drums on the tiled roofs, and brown water runs down the furrows between the vine rows.
*elseif (weather = "Storm")
  Wind shoves across the open slope and drives rain sideways through the vines. Roof tiles clatter, and the hedges lash against the walls.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Fog fills the lanes and hides the slope below the village, leaving the nearest vine rows as dark lines in the white.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow gathers on the tiles and the tops of the walls, and the vine rows run down the white slope in dark stripes.
*elseif (weather = "Blizzard")
  Driven snow blots out the slope and the far end of the lane. The nearest wall is the farthest thing you can see.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (ba_dark)
    Starlight lies pale on the walls, and the vine rows run down the hillside as dark bars.
  *elseif (time_period = "Dusk")
    The last light turns the pale walls gold and sends long shadows down between the vine rows.
  *else
    Bright light lies across the slope, warm on the stone and the red tiles, and every vine row throws a thin shadow.
*else
  *if (ba_dark)
    A starless sky presses down on the hill, and the vine rows are lost in the dark.
  *else
    Flat grey light lies over the slope and brings out the pale stone of the walls.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Water still runs in the furrows between the vine rows, and the lanes are soft with mud.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The lane stones are dark from the last rain, and the furrows hold shallow water.
*return
