*comment ── LAYER 1: PLACE ── Fixed geography only. The long description prints once.
*label ba_mk_place
*if (mk_first)
  The market fills a broad paved yard where the village lane meets the road from the arch. Tiled canopies on stone posts shelter long tables along three sides, and the paving between them has been worn smooth and pale by carts and boots. At the middle a raised stone platform holds a big iron scale beneath a canopy painted with House Gault's shield, and a clerk's counter with folding shutters stands beside it. Small casks with wooden taps sit in rows on the nearer tables, among baskets of fruit, jars of honey and pale blocks of beeswax. A stone trough fed by a pipe waits where the carts pull up. It smells of fruit, beeswax, wine and warm stone.
*else
  The paved yard is as before: the tiled canopies along its sides, the scale on its platform under the painted shield, and the rows of small casks, fruit and honey on the tables.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 2: SKY ── Weather and light only, then the ground's memory of recent weather. The market is shut at night and in dangerous weather, so those cases never print here.
*label ba_mk_sky
*if (weather = "Rain")
  Rain drums on the tiled canopies and runs off their edges in strings, darkening the paving between them.
*elseif (weather = "Fog")
  Fog fills the yard and softens the far canopies to grey shapes.
*elseif ((weather = "Snow") or (weather = "Sleet"))
  Wet snow slides off the canopy tiles and melts to slush on the paving.
*elseif (weather = "Clear")
  *if (time_period = "Dusk")
    The last light lies low across the yard and turns the pale paving gold.
  *else
    Bright light falls across the paving, and the canopies throw sharp shadows over the tables.
*else
  Flat grey light lies over the yard and shows every worn dip in the paving.

*if ((ground_state = "sodden") and (not(weather_wet)))
  Water still stands in the dips of the paving, and the carts have left muddy tracks across it.
*elseif ((ground_state = "wet") and (not(weather_wet)))
  The paving is dark in the hollows from the last rain.
*return

*comment ── LAYER 3: STREET ── Who is about and what is laid out. The stock line follows the season; the rest follows the day and the clock.
*label ba_mk_street
*if (current_season = "Spring")
  Last year's apples and dried pears fill the fruit baskets, and the first honey of the year stands in small jars.
*elseif (current_season = "Summer")
  Baskets of cherries, plums and early pears share the tables with fresh honeycomb.
*elseif (current_season = "Autumn")
  Baskets of apples, pears, plums and grapes crowd the tables, and the honey jars are new and dark.
*else
  Stored apples and dried fruit fill the baskets, and the honey jars stand in neat rows beside the beeswax.
*if (time_period = "Dusk")
  Sellers are untying canopy ropes and rolling the last small casks toward their carts, and the clerk has begun folding the shutters over the counter.
  *return
*if (day_of_week = "Marketday")
  The yard is packed. Carts nose in beside the trough, buyers pass tin cups from hand to hand, and sellers call their lots over the noise of the scale.
  *return
*if (day_of_week = "Hallowday")
  Only a few tables are laid out for the rest day, and the buyers who come move slowly from one to the next.
  *return
