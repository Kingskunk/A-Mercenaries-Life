*comment Night and Pre-Dawn. Hours spent inside the dungeon go through advance_time as always, so the tide turns by itself as the run goes on.
*comment
*comment A DUNGEON'S OWN PART lives in its own scene, not here: pvsc_table (its encounter rows, set into the dg_trow_ slots), pvsc_respawn (what a refill clears) and pvsc_forget_map (the

*comment Speaking with a beast (2026-10-02): what casting Speak with Animals would cost this character, for an encounter that offers it. dg_swa_check reports it and dg_swa_cast pays it, so an

*comment MAGIC_AND_ABILITIES_REFERENCE.md). A Bard, Ranger or Warlock pays one slot from their own pool. Outputs: dg_swa_can, dg_swa_cost ("none", "free", "ritual", "slot") and dg_swa_why (what
