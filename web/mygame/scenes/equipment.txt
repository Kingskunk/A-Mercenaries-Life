*comment *gosub_scene equipment format_copper <amount>   (output gear_sale_desc, e.g. "3 Silver Marks, 5 Copper Bits")
*label format_copper
*params fc_amount
*temp fc_silver ((fc_amount - (fc_amount modulo 10)) / 10)
*temp fc_copper (fc_amount modulo 10)
*temp fc_s_str ""
*temp fc_c_str ""
*if (fc_silver = 1)
  *set fc_s_str "1 Silver Mark"
*elseif (fc_silver > 1)
  *set fc_s_str (fc_silver & " Silver Marks")
*if (fc_copper = 1)
  *set fc_c_str "1 Copper Bit"
*elseif (fc_copper > 1)
  *set fc_c_str (fc_copper & " Copper Bits")
*if ((fc_silver > 0) and (fc_copper > 0))
  *set gear_sale_desc ((fc_s_str & ", ") & fc_c_str)
*elseif (fc_silver > 0)
  *set gear_sale_desc fc_s_str
*elseif (fc_copper > 0)
  *set gear_sale_desc fc_c_str
*else
  *set gear_sale_desc "nothing"
*return

*comment *gosub_scene equipment gear_unit_value <item_id> <"smith"|"pawn">   (output gear_unit_copper: what ONE
*comment unit fetches from that buyer; 0 means this buyer declines it)
*comment Prices come from the catalog's sell fields (gear_info): a percentage of retail per buyer, an optional fixed
*comment pawn value, and an optional per-piece smith price for a counted item.
*label gear_unit_value
*params guv_item guv_buyer
*gosub gear_info guv_item
*temp guv_pct gear_pct_pawn
*if (guv_buyer = "smith")
  *set guv_pct gear_pct_smith
*set gear_unit_copper (((gear_retail * guv_pct) - ((gear_retail * guv_pct) modulo 100)) / 100)
*if ((guv_buyer = "pawn") and (gear_fixed_pawn > 0))
  *set gear_unit_copper gear_fixed_pawn
*if ((guv_buyer = "smith") and (gear_piece_smith > 0))
  *set gear_unit_copper gear_piece_smith
*return

*comment CONSUMABLES: USE AT WILL
*comment *gosub_scene equipment use_consumable <item_id>   (input use_in_combat true/false)
*comment One routine for every way of using a consumable: the combat menu, the dossier's satchel, and the inventory
*comment panel's Use button (which opens the dossier at codex_use_do). Outputs use_result "ok" | "full" (a heal
*comment while unhurt, nothing consumed) | "limit" (an out-of-combat heal on a day that already had one) | "none",
*comment plus use_text and use_banner. Healing works freely in combat; out of combat it shares the once-a-day
*comment instant-heal limit with the hedge-doctor (last_mundane_treatment_day), so a stack of phials cannot undo
*comment the slow-healing rule. Boons have no limit and go through apply_unique_buff. The whole effect runs inside a

*label use_consumable
*params uc_item
*if (not(use_locked)) or (not(locked_use_page_id = page_id))
  *set use_result "none"
  *set use_banner ""
  *set use_text ""
  *gosub gear_count uc_item
  *if ((gear_qty > 0) and (gear_kind = "consumable"))
    *set use_text gear_use_text
    *set use_result "ok"
    *if (gear_effect = "heal")
      *if (hp_current >= hp_max)
        *set use_result "full"
        *set use_text "You are not hurt enough for it to mend anything, so you leave the stopper in."
      *elseif ((not(use_in_combat)) and (last_mundane_treatment_day = campaign_day))
        *set use_result "limit"
        *set use_text "You have already had a wound seen to today. A body only takes so much mending in a day, and another dose now would be wasted."
      *else
        *temp uc_sum gear_heal_bonus
        *temp uc_i 0
        *temp uc_die 0
        *label uc_heal_loop
        *if (uc_i < gear_heal_n)
          *rand uc_die 1 gear_heal_sides
          *set uc_sum (uc_sum + uc_die)
          *set uc_i (uc_i + 1)
          *goto uc_heal_loop
        *set hp_current (hp_current + uc_sum)
        *if (hp_current > hp_max)
          *set hp_current hp_max
        *if (not(use_in_combat))
          *set last_mundane_treatment_day campaign_day
        *set use_banner (((((("🧪 " & gear_title) & ": Restored +") & uc_sum) & " HP | HP: ") & hp_current) & (" / " & hp_max))

      *set unique_buff_input_name gear_eff_name
      *set unique_buff_input_desc gear_eff_desc
      *set unique_buff_input_stat gear_eff_stat
      *set unique_buff_input_bonus gear_eff_bonus
      *set unique_buff_input_minutes gear_eff_minutes
      *gosub_scene startup apply_unique_buff
      *set use_banner ((("✨ " & gear_eff_name) & ": ") & gear_eff_summary)
    *if (use_result = "ok")
      *set gear_flag_var ("has_" & uc_item)
      *set gear_spare_var ("spare_" & uc_item)
      *temp uc_spare {gear_spare_var}
      *if (uc_spare > 0)
        *set {gear_spare_var} (uc_spare - 1)
      *else
        *set {gear_flag_var} false
  *set use_locked true
  *set locked_use_page_id page_id
*return

*comment *gosub_scene equipment shop_cart_sell <item_id>   (one sell line; shop_buyer says who is buying)
