*comment ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
*comment SHARED SMALL-COMBAT ENGINE
*comment All dice math (attack rolls, damage, spell slots, temp HP, locks)
*comment lives in startup.txt -- this file just wires those primitives into
*comment a playable round-by-round loop and owns the mechanical shell every
*comment fight shares (the choice skeleton, the enemy-turn resolution).
*comment
*comment Calling convention: a scene starts a fight with
*comment   *gosub_scene combat fight_<name>
*comment and gets back combat_outcome set to "victory", "rescued", or
*comment "fled" once it returns. Nothing in here prints start-of-fight or
*comment end-of-fight prose -- that's deliberate: the kill/retreat and
*comment whatever NPC's reaction to it read as one continuous narrative beat
*comment when the calling quest scene writes both together, instead of a
*comment jarring cut between this file's text and theirs.
*comment
*comment Adding a new fight: write a new *label fight_<name> block below
*comment that sets the enemy's stats (combat_enemy_*) and its bespoke
*comment flavor lines (combat_enemy_epithet, combat_flavor_weapon_hit/miss,
*comment combat_flavor_cantrip_hit/miss, combat_flavor_enemy_hit/miss, and
*comment optionally combat_flavor_dissonant_hit/miss), then *gosub
*comment fight_setup and *goto fight_round_hub. Everything else -- every
*comment spell/ability option, the enemy's attack resolution, retreat/rescue
*comment handling -- is already shared and needs no duplication. The eight
*comment ability options below (cantrips aside) deliberately avoid any
*comment enemy-specific wording, since they describe the PLAYER's own
*comment casting motion, not the creature -- keep new ones that way too, so
*comment they never need a per-fight override.
*comment ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

*label fight_setup
*comment Resets every ephemeral combat flag so encounter N+1 never inherits
*comment leftover state from encounter N (or a Shield/Hex left armed from a
*comment fight the player fled).
*set hex_active false
*set agathys_active false
*set temp_hp 0
*set shield_armed false
*set combat_agathys_triggered false
*set combat_agathys_shattered false
*set combat_outcome ""
*return

*label fight_cleanup
*set hex_active false
*set agathys_active false
*set temp_hp 0
*set shield_armed false
*set combat_agathys_triggered false
*set combat_agathys_shattered false
*return

*label fight_victory
*set combat_enemy_hp 0
*set combat_outcome "victory"
*gosub fight_cleanup
*return

*label fight_rescued
*set hp_current 1
*set combat_outcome "rescued"
*gosub fight_cleanup
*return

*label fight_fled
*set combat_outcome "fled"
*gosub fight_cleanup
*return

*comment ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
*comment THE ROUND HUB (shared choice skeleton)
*comment ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

*label fight_round_hub
*set combat_enemy_disadvantage false
*gosub_scene startup detect_attack_cantrip

[b][⚔️ Round ${combat_round} — ${combat_enemy_name}: HP ${combat_enemy_hp}/${combat_enemy_max_hp} (AC ${combat_enemy_ac}) | You: HP ${hp_current}/${hp_max}@{(temp_hp > 0)  (+${temp_hp} Temp HP)|} (AC ${armor_class})][/b]
*if (shield_armed)
  [b][✨ Shield Armed — will trigger on the next hit][/b]
*if (hex_active)
  [b][🔮 Hex Active — +1d6 Necrotic Damage on all hits][/b]
*if (agathys_active)
  [b][❄️ Armor of Agathys Active — ${temp_hp} Temp HP | 5 Cold Damage Retaliation][/b]

*choice
  # Attack with your ${weapon}.
    *if (weapon_type = "ranged")
      *set check_stat "dex"
    *else
      *if (weapon_type = "finesse")
        *comment Finesse (RAW): the wielder's choice of STR or DEX, whichever
        *comment is higher -- not a blanket DEX weapon.
        *if (str_mod > dex_mod)
          *set check_stat "str"
        *else
          *set check_stat "dex"
      *else
        *set check_stat "str"
    *set check_dc combat_enemy_ac
    *set check_skill "Melee Attack"
    *gosub_scene startup roll_d20_check
    *if (check_success)
      *if (weapon_type = "finesse")
        *set combat_player_dmg_dice_sides 4
        *comment Matches the STR-or-higher check above -- 5e uses the same
        *comment ability for a finesse weapon's attack and its damage.
        *if (str_mod > dex_mod)
          *set combat_player_dmg_bonus str_mod
        *else
          *set combat_player_dmg_bonus dex_mod
      *else
        *if (weapon_type = "ranged")
          *set combat_player_dmg_dice_sides 6
          *set combat_player_dmg_bonus dex_mod
        *else
          *if (weapon_type = "two_handed")
            *set combat_player_dmg_dice_sides 12
            *set combat_player_dmg_bonus str_mod
          *else
            *set combat_player_dmg_dice_sides 8
            *set combat_player_dmg_bonus str_mod
      *gosub_scene startup resolve_player_weapon_hit
      *if (combat_sneak_attack_triggered)
        You strike from an opening it never saw coming, driving your ${weapon} into an exposed weak point for a vicious, precise finishing blow.
        [b][🗡️ Sneak Attack | 🩸 Hit: ${combat_player_dmg_dealt} damage][/b]
      *else
        ${combat_flavor_weapon_hit}
        [b][🩸 Hit: ${combat_player_dmg_dealt} damage][/b]
    *else
      ${combat_flavor_weapon_miss}
      [b][💨 Miss][/b]
    *goto fight_check_enemy_hp

  *if (has_attack_cantrip)
    # Channel ${attack_cantrip_name} at ${combat_enemy_epithet}.
      *set check_stat attack_cantrip_stat
      *set check_dc combat_enemy_ac
      *set check_skill attack_cantrip_name
      *gosub_scene startup roll_d20_check
      *if (check_success)
        *set combat_player_dmg_dice_sides attack_cantrip_dice_sides
        *set combat_player_dmg_bonus 0
        *gosub_scene startup resolve_player_hit
        ${combat_flavor_cantrip_hit}
        [b][🔥 Hit: ${combat_player_dmg_dealt} damage][/b]
      *else
        ${combat_flavor_cantrip_miss}
        [b][💨 Miss][/b]
      *goto fight_check_enemy_hp

  *if (((character_class = "wizard") and (wizard_spell = "magic_missile")) and (wizard_spell_slots > 0))
    # [Spell: Magic Missile] Unleash three darts of force that strike unerringly, bypassing its hide entirely.
      *gosub_scene startup resolve_magic_missile
      You speak three crisp syllables and snap your fingers forward. Three glowing, pale-blue darts of crackling arcane force burst from your fingertips, curving unerringly through the air to slam home.
      [b][✨ Magic Missile: Automatic Hit | ${combat_player_dmg_dealt} damage][/b]
      *goto fight_check_enemy_hp

  *if ((((warlock_spell = "hex") and (warlock_spell_slots > 0)) or ((race_cantrip_2 = "hex") and (not(hexblood_hex_used)))) and (not(hex_active)))
    # [Spell: Hex] Lay a baleful eldritch curse upon ${combat_enemy_epithet}, marking it to suffer deeper wounds.
      *gosub_scene startup resolve_cast_hex
      You whisper a guttural syllable of ancient ruin. Dark, spectral runes flare briefly across ${combat_enemy_epithet}, tying its fate to your will—every strike you land will tear through it with extra necrotic fury!
      [b][🔮 Hex Active — +1d6 Necrotic Damage on all hits][/b]
      *goto fight_round_hub

  *if (((warlock_spell = "armor_of_agathys") and (warlock_spell_slots > 0)) and (not(agathys_active)))
    # [Spell: Armor of Agathys] Sheathe yourself in spectral frost that shields you and punishes melee strikes.
      *gosub_scene startup resolve_cast_agathys
      A brittle casing of translucent, jagged rime coats your leather harness and shoulders, radiating bone-chilling cold. Any creature foolish enough to strike you will feel the frost shatter against them.
      [b][🛡️ Armor of Agathys: +5 Temp HP | 5 Cold Damage Retaliation on Hits][/b]
      *goto fight_enemy_turn

  *if (((((character_class = "wizard") and (wizard_spell = "false_life")) and (wizard_spell_slots > 0)) or (((character_class = "warlock") and (warlock_spell = "false_life")) and (warlock_spell_slots > 0))) and (temp_hp = 0))
    # [Spell: False Life] Weave a necromantic shroud over your flesh for a protective buffer of vitality.
      *gosub_scene startup resolve_cast_false_life
      You murmur a dark necromantic rite. A numb, leathery vitality spreads beneath your skin, insulating your muscles against immediate harm.
      [b][✨ False Life: +${combat_cast_result} Temporary Hit Points][/b]
      *goto fight_enemy_turn

  *if (((bard_spell = "dissonant_whispers") or (bard_spell_2 = "dissonant_whispers")) and (bard_spell_slots > 0))
    # [Spell: Dissonant Whispers] Utter a hideous, discordant psychic phrase that wracks its mind.
      *set check_stat "cha"
      *set check_dc 12
      *set check_skill "Dissonant Whispers"
      *gosub_scene startup roll_d20_check
      *gosub_scene startup resolve_dissonant_whispers
      *if (check_success)
        ${combat_flavor_dissonant_hit}
        [b][🧠 Dissonant Whispers: Failed Save | ${combat_player_dmg_dealt} Psychic Damage][/b]
      *else
        ${combat_flavor_dissonant_miss}
        [b][🧠 Dissonant Whispers: Saved | Half Damage: ${combat_player_dmg_dealt} Psychic Damage][/b]
      *goto fight_check_enemy_hp

  *if ((((bard_spell = "healing_word") or (bard_spell_2 = "healing_word")) and (bard_spell_slots > 0)) and (hp_current < hp_max))
    # [Spell: Healing Word] Speak a resonant, curative syllable to knit your gashes and steady your footing.
      *gosub_scene startup resolve_cast_healing_word
      A sharp, ringing cadence leaves your lips. Warmth rushes through your marrow, knitting shut the worst of your wounds.
      [b][🩸 Healing Word: Restored ${combat_cast_result} HP | HP: ${hp_current}/${hp_max}][/b]
      *goto fight_round_hub

  # Brace and watch its strikes, giving ground instead of pressing the attack.
    *set combat_enemy_disadvantage true
    You plant your feet and hold your ground, tracking its movement instead of swinging blind.
    *goto fight_enemy_turn

  *if ((((character_class = "wizard") and (wizard_spell = "shield")) and (wizard_spell_slots > 0)) and (not(shield_armed)))
    # [Spell: Shield] Hold the barrier-spell coiled and ready, primed to snap into place the instant something connects.
      *set shield_armed true
      You murmur the first half of the somatic phrase under your breath, holding the working coiled and ready at your fingertips, costing you nothing to keep it there.
      [b][✨ Shield Readied — will trigger automatically on the next hit][/b]
      *goto fight_round_hub

  *if ((character_class = "fighter") and (fighter_second_wind_uses > 0))
    # [Second Wind] Grit your teeth through the pain and the fear, and keep going.
      *gosub_scene startup second_wind
      A surge of stubborn, practiced discipline steadies your breathing and closes the worst of your scrapes.
      [b][🩸 Hit Points: ${hp_current} / ${hp_max}][/b]
      *goto fight_enemy_turn

  *if (((character_class = "barbarian") and (not(is_raging))) and (barbarian_rage_uses > 0))
    # [Rage] Let the fury take you.
      *gosub_scene startup activate_rage
      A hot, roaring pressure floods your chest, dulling the pain and the fear to nothing at all.
      *goto fight_enemy_turn

  # Break off and disengage.
    *goto fight_fled

*label fight_check_enemy_hp
*if (combat_enemy_hp <= 0)
  *goto fight_victory
*goto fight_enemy_turn

*label fight_enemy_turn
*gosub_scene startup resolve_enemy_attack
*if (combat_shield_triggered)
  *if (combat_enemy_hit_success)
    A shimmering lattice of force flares to life around you on pure reflex, but the blow crashes through it anyway, striking home regardless.
    [b][✨ Shield Broken Through | 🩸 ${combat_enemy_name} Hits: ${combat_enemy_dmg_dealt} damage | HP: ${hp_current} / ${hp_max}][/b]
  *else
    A shimmering lattice of force flares to life around you on pure reflex, deflecting the strike a hair's breadth away.
    [b][✨ Shield Holds | 💨 ${combat_enemy_name} Misses][/b]
*else
  *if (combat_enemy_hit_success)
    ${combat_flavor_enemy_hit}
    *if (combat_agathys_triggered)
      *if (combat_agathys_shattered)
        As the blow lands, your frost-sheathe shatters violently in a burst of crystalline rime, driving shards of biting cold deep into ${combat_enemy_epithet}!
        [b][❄️ Armor of Agathys: 5 Cold Damage dealt to ${combat_enemy_name} | Frost Armor Shattered][/b]
      *else
        As the blow lands, the rime holding across your harness pulses with bitter frost, biting into ${combat_enemy_epithet} while your armor holds!
        [b][❄️ Armor of Agathys: 5 Cold Damage dealt to ${combat_enemy_name} | ${temp_hp} Temp HP remaining][/b]
    [b][🩸 ${combat_enemy_name} Hits: ${combat_enemy_dmg_dealt} damage | HP: ${hp_current} / ${hp_max}][/b]
  *else
    ${combat_flavor_enemy_miss}
    [b][💨 ${combat_enemy_name} Misses][/b]
*if (combat_enemy_hp <= 0)
  *goto fight_victory
*if (hp_current <= 1)
  *goto fight_rescued
*if (not(stat_bump_locked)) or (not(locked_stat_bump_page_id = choice_page_id))
  *set combat_round + 1
  *set stat_bump_locked true
  *set locked_stat_bump_page_id choice_page_id
*page_break Hold your ground...
*goto fight_round_hub

*label fight_silt_lurker
*comment Used by alderford.txt's Maura cellar-nuisance quest.
*set combat_enemy_name "Silt-Lurker Lacedon"
*set combat_enemy_hp 12
*set combat_enemy_max_hp 12
*set combat_enemy_ac 12
*set combat_enemy_atk_bonus 4
*set combat_enemy_dmg_dice_sides 6
*set combat_enemy_dmg_bonus 2
*set combat_round 1
*set combat_enemy_epithet "the creature"
*set combat_flavor_weapon_hit "You drive your ${weapon} home. It bites deep, and the creature recoils with a wet, furious hiss."
*set combat_flavor_weapon_miss "Your ${weapon} scrapes off wet scale and old chitin, throwing you half a step off balance in the current."
*set combat_flavor_cantrip_hit "The working leaves your fingertips and strikes true, searing across wet scale in the dark."
*set combat_flavor_cantrip_miss "The working goes wide, scattering harmlessly into the black water."
*set combat_flavor_enemy_hit "The thing surges out of the dark water in a wet lunge, ragged claws raking for your ribs."
*set combat_flavor_enemy_miss "It lunges out of the water, but you're already moving, and the claws rake nothing but torchlit air."
*set combat_flavor_dissonant_hit "You whisper a jarring, dissonant phrase into the shadows. The sound bypasses its ears and ruptures straight into its rotting brain—the beast screeches in agony, clawing wildly at its own skull!"
*set combat_flavor_dissonant_miss "The phrase reverberates through the drain, but the creature recoils just enough to weather the worst of the psychic assault."
*gosub fight_setup
*goto fight_round_hub
