*comment lives in startup.txt -- this file just wires those primitives into
*comment a playable round-by-round loop and owns the mechanical shell every

*comment

*comment when the calling quest scene writes both together, instead of a
*comment jarring cut between this file's text and theirs.
*comment

*comment that sets the enemy's stats (combat_enemy_*), combat_enemy_epithet,

*comment   combat_flavor_weapon_hit_ranged_1/2/3, _miss_ranged_1/2/3
*comment     (player, ranged -- needs its own wording, not melee text with
*comment      ${weapon} swapped in, which reads wrong for a bow/crossbow)
*comment   combat_flavor_enemy_hit_1/2/3, _miss_1/2/3         (enemy, slot 1
