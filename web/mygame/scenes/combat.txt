*label apply_slot_stat_override
*comment One slot's hp/ac/atk override, applied and reset, via name indirection instead of a hand-copied block
*comment per slot (see fight_setup, which calls this for every slot from 2 to combat_enemy_slot_cap; slot 1 keeps
*comment its own block there unchanged, since slot 1's override applies to the plain combat_enemy_* names, not a
*comment numbered slot, until fight_setup snapshots it into combat_enemy1_* afterward).
*params aso_slot
*temp aso_p (("combat_enemy" & aso_slot) & "_")
*temp aso_hp_ov {(aso_p & "hp_override")}
*temp aso_ac_ov {(aso_p & "ac_override")}
*temp aso_atk_ov {(aso_p & "atk_override")}
*temp aso_delta 0
*if (not(aso_hp_ov = 999))
  *set {(aso_p & "hp")} aso_hp_ov
  *set {(aso_p & "max_hp")} aso_hp_ov
*if (not(aso_ac_ov = 999))
  *set {(aso_p & "ac")} aso_ac_ov
*if (not(aso_atk_ov = 999))
  *if ({(aso_p & "move2_chance")} > 0)
    *set aso_delta aso_atk_ov
    *set aso_delta - {(aso_p & "atk_bonus")}
    *set {(aso_p & "move2_atk_bonus")} ({(aso_p & "move2_atk_bonus")} + aso_delta)
  *set {(aso_p & "atk_bonus")} aso_atk_ov

*set {(aso_p & "ac_override")} 999
*set {(aso_p & "atk_override")} 999

*label apply_ability_score_range
*comment Ability scores (2026-09-27, groundwork for initiative rolls and later saving throws): one score for one
*comment slot. The manual per-slot override wins if set (nonzero, see startup.txt); otherwise this rolls within
*comment [aasr_min, aasr_max], the calling type's own default range. Called six times (once per score) from BOTH
*comment branches of every apply_type_X below: slot 1 passes the bare "combat_enemy_" prefix (the same bare names
*comment fight_setup later snapshots into combat_enemy1_*, mirroring how hp/ac/atk_bonus already work for slot 1),
*comment slot 2-and-beyond passes that type's own apt_p prefix directly, no separate slot-1-vs-N code path needed.
*comment Resets its own override to 0 once consumed (fight_cleanup also resets every score and override as a
*comment backstop, same treatment as the opt-in condition/movepool fields, so a hand-authored fight that never
*comment touches ability scores at all can't inherit a leftover roll from an earlier type-library fight).
*params aasr_prefix aasr_field aasr_min aasr_max
*temp aasr_ov_name ((aasr_prefix & aasr_field) & "_override")
*temp aasr_target (aasr_prefix & aasr_field)
*temp aasr_ov {(aasr_ov_name)}
*if (aasr_ov > 0)
  *set {(aasr_target)} aasr_ov
*else
  *temp aasr_roll aasr_min
  *rand aasr_roll aasr_min aasr_max
  *set {(aasr_target)} aasr_roll
*set {(aasr_ov_name)} 0
*return

*label cri_is_better_than_best
*comment Compares initiative candidate cri_ibt_a against candidate cri_ibt_b (params: 1-indexed positions into
*comment the combat_init_* scratch arrays, startup.txt) for combat_roll_initiative's selection sort below. Output
*comment cri_ibt_result: true if A should be placed ahead of B. Tie-break order, all deterministic so it's
*comment testable: higher initiative roll, then higher raw DEX score (5e's own tiebreak), then player over ally
*comment over enemy, then ascending slot number.
*params cri_ibt_a cri_ibt_b
*temp cri_ibt_a_score {("combat_init_score_" & cri_ibt_a)}
*temp cri_ibt_b_score {("combat_init_score_" & cri_ibt_b)}
*set cri_ibt_result false
*if (cri_ibt_a_score > cri_ibt_b_score)
  *set cri_ibt_result true
*elseif (cri_ibt_a_score = cri_ibt_b_score)
  *temp cri_ibt_a_dex {("combat_init_dex_" & cri_ibt_a)}
  *temp cri_ibt_b_dex {("combat_init_dex_" & cri_ibt_b)}
  *if (cri_ibt_a_dex > cri_ibt_b_dex)
    *set cri_ibt_result true
  *elseif (cri_ibt_a_dex = cri_ibt_b_dex)
    *temp cri_ibt_a_kind {("combat_init_kind_" & cri_ibt_a)}
    *temp cri_ibt_b_kind {("combat_init_kind_" & cri_ibt_b)}
    *temp cri_ibt_a_rank 1
    *temp cri_ibt_b_rank 1
    *if (cri_ibt_a_kind = "player")
      *set cri_ibt_a_rank 3
    *elseif (cri_ibt_a_kind = "ally")
      *set cri_ibt_a_rank 2
    *if (cri_ibt_b_kind = "player")
      *set cri_ibt_b_rank 3
    *elseif (cri_ibt_b_kind = "ally")
      *set cri_ibt_b_rank 2
    *if (cri_ibt_a_rank > cri_ibt_b_rank)
      *set cri_ibt_result true
    *elseif (cri_ibt_a_rank = cri_ibt_b_rank)
      *temp cri_ibt_a_slot {("combat_init_slot_" & cri_ibt_a)}

*label combat_roll_initiative

*comment persistent *create'd storage, not fresh *temps, so they don't reset themselves between calls.

*comment Selection sort, descending: repeatedly scan every not-yet-placed candidate for the best remaining one
*comment (cri_is_better_than_best above), place it, mark it used. Simpler and safer than an insertion sort's
*comment shifting step, and with at most 11 candidates the O(n^2) cost is nothing.
*temp cri_placed 0
*label cri_sort_loop

  *temp cri_best 0
  *temp cri_k 1
  *label cri_scan_loop

    *if (not({("combat_init_used_" & cri_k)}))
      *if (cri_best = 0)
        *set cri_best cri_k
      *else

          *set cri_best cri_k
    *set cri_k + 1
    *goto cri_scan_loop

  *set {("combat_turn_kind_" & cri_placed)} {("combat_init_kind_" & cri_best)}
  *set {("combat_turn_slot_" & cri_placed)} {("combat_init_slot_" & cri_best)}
  *set {("combat_init_used_" & cri_best)} true
  *goto cri_sort_loop
*set combat_turn_count combat_init_count

*label fight_library
*comment THE PLUG-IN ENTRY (2026-09-26, widened to 6 slots 2026-09-27). A scene that needs a fight, and has no
*comment fight_<name> label of its own, sets
*comment   combat_lib_1 (and optionally combat_lib_2 through combat_lib_6): a library type id for each slot, and
*comment   combat_is_nonlethal true if the brawl should end in surrender rather than death (default false),
*comment   plus, if it wants its own names, combat_enemy_name_override and combat_enemy_epithet_override for slot 1
*comment   and the combat_enemy2_ / _3_ / _4_ / _5_ / _6_ versions for the others,
*comment then *gosub_scene combat fight_library and reads combat_outcome afterwards like any fight. Call anything
*comment humanoid by naming it, with no other change: a black knight with a sword and a bandit archer is
*comment   *set combat_lib_1 "swordsman"   *set combat_enemy_name_override "Black Knight"
*comment   *set combat_enemy_epithet_override "the black knight"
*comment   *set combat_lib_2 "archer"      *set combat_enemy2_name_override "Bandit Archer"
*comment   *set combat_enemy2_epithet_override "the bandit archer"
*comment (a proper name works too: epithet "Captain Vane"). To make one fighter tougher or weaker than its type, set any
*comment of combat_enemy_hp_override, combat_enemy_ac_override and combat_enemy_atk_override (hit points, the number the
*comment player must beat to hit it, and its to-hit bonus; combat_enemy2_ through combat_enemy6_ for the other slots). Leave one
*comment alone and that number stays the type's own. Captain Vane, a swordsman with 24 HP, AC 14 and +5 to hit:
*comment   *set combat_enemy_hp_override 24   *set combat_enemy_ac_override 14   *set combat_enemy_atk_override 5
*comment The number of enemies is the number of combat_lib_ slots filled. Slot 1 decides the player's lines: creatures
*comment (lacedon, mire_rat, beast) use apply_creature_weapon_flavor, everything else apply_generic_weapon_flavor; both
*comment switch on the weapon's damage type. No prose is written per fight.
*comment Human types by weapon: swordsman, axeman, knifeman, maceman, spearman, hookman (hooked pole), archer, slinger,
*comment and guard (plain mace, no special move). Creatures: lacedon, mire_rat, and beast, a generic natural-attack monster
*comment (wolf, boar, bear, giant spider, ghoul) that becomes whatever you name it:
*comment   *set combat_lib_1 "beast"   *set combat_enemy_name_override "Dire Wolf"   *set combat_enemy_epithet_override "the dire wolf"
*comment   *set combat_enemy_hp_override 14   *set combat_enemy_atk_override 4
*comment combat_lib_* is cleared by fight_cleanup.
*set combat_enemy_count 1
*set combat_apply_slot 1
*set combat_apply_type combat_lib_1
*gosub apply_enemy_type
*comment Slot 1 is mandatory; slots 2 and beyond are however many combat_lib_N the caller filled (up to
*comment combat_enemy_slot_cap), checked via a bounded loop and indirection instead of one hand-written *if per
*comment slot. Widening the slot cap needs no change here.
*temp flb_i 2
*label fight_library_slot_loop
*if (flb_i <= combat_enemy_slot_cap)
  *temp flb_lib {("combat_lib_" & flb_i)}
  *if (not(flb_lib = ""))
    *set combat_enemy_count flb_i
    *set combat_apply_slot flb_i
    *set combat_apply_type flb_lib
    *gosub apply_enemy_type
  *set flb_i + 1
  *goto fight_library_slot_loop
*set combat_round 1
*if ((combat_lib_1 = "lacedon") or ((combat_lib_1 = "mire_rat") or (combat_lib_1 = "beast")))
  *gosub apply_creature_weapon_flavor
*else
  *gosub apply_generic_weapon_flavor
*gosub fight_setup
*goto fight_round_hub
