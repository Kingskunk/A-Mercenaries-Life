*label apply_ally_roster
*comment Called from fight_library, before fight_setup. Applies every filled combat_ally_lib_N (1..
*comment combat_ally_slot_cap) via apply_ally_type, the same bounded-loop-plus-indirection pattern fight_library's
*comment own slot loop uses for enemies, and sets combat_ally_count to the highest filled slot. A fight with no
*comment allies at all (combat_ally_lib_1 through _4 all "") leaves combat_ally_count at 0, same as ever.
*set combat_ally_count 0
*temp aar_i 1
*label apply_ally_roster_loop
*if (aar_i <= combat_ally_slot_cap)
  *temp aar_lib {("combat_ally_lib_" & aar_i)}
  *if (not(aar_lib = ""))
    *set combat_ally_count aar_i
    *set combat_apply_ally_slot aar_i
    *set combat_apply_ally_type aar_lib
    *gosub apply_ally_type
  *set aar_i + 1
  *goto apply_ally_roster_loop
*return

*label apply_ally_type
*if (combat_apply_ally_type = "archer")
  *gosub apply_allytype_archer
*if (combat_apply_ally_type = "guard")
  *gosub apply_allytype_guard
*temp aat_p (("combat_ally" & combat_apply_ally_slot) & "_")
*temp aat_name_ov {(aat_p & "name_override")}
*temp aat_epi_ov {(aat_p & "epithet_override")}
*if (not(aat_name_ov = ""))
  *set {(aat_p & "name")} aat_name_ov
  *set {(aat_p & "name_override")} ""
*if (not(aat_epi_ov = ""))

*return

*label fight_library
*comment THE PLUG-IN ENTRY. A scene that needs a fight, and has no fight_<name> label of its own, sets
*comment   combat_lib_1 (and optionally combat_lib_2 through combat_lib_6): a library type id for each slot, and
*comment   combat_is_nonlethal true if the brawl should end in surrender rather than death (default false),
*comment   plus, if it wants its own names, combat_enemy_name_override and combat_enemy_epithet_override for slot 1
*comment   and the combat_enemy2_ / _3_ / _4_ / _5_ / _6_ versions for the others,
*comment then *gosub_scene combat fight_library and reads combat_outcome afterwards like any fight. Call anything
*comment humanoid by naming it, with no other change: a black knight with a sword and a bandit archer is
*comment   *set combat_lib_1 "swordsman"   *set combat_enemy_name_override "Black Knight"
*comment   *set combat_enemy_epithet_override "the black knight"
*comment   *set combat_lib_2 "archer"      *set combat_enemy2_name_override "Bandit Archer"
*comment   *set combat_enemy2_epithet_override "the bandit archer"
*comment (a proper name works too: epithet "Captain Vane"). To make one fighter tougher or weaker than its type, set any
*comment of combat_enemy_hp_override, combat_enemy_ac_override and combat_enemy_atk_override (hit points, the number the
*comment player must beat to hit it, and its to-hit bonus; combat_enemy2_ through combat_enemy6_ for the other slots). Leave one
*comment alone and that number stays the type's own. Captain Vane, a swordsman with 24 HP, AC 14 and +5 to hit:
*comment   *set combat_enemy_hp_override 24   *set combat_enemy_ac_override 14   *set combat_enemy_atk_override 5
*comment The number of enemies is the number of combat_lib_ slots filled. Slot 1 decides the player's lines: creatures
*comment (lacedon, mire_rat, beast) use apply_creature_weapon_flavor, everything else apply_generic_weapon_flavor; both
*comment switch on the weapon's damage type. No prose is written per fight.
*comment Human types by weapon: swordsman, axeman, knifeman, maceman, spearman, hookman (hooked pole), archer, slinger,
*comment and guard (plain mace, no special move). Creatures: lacedon, mire_rat, and beast, a generic natural-attack monster
*comment (wolf, boar, bear, giant spider, ghoul) that becomes whatever you name it:
*comment   *set combat_lib_1 "beast"   *set combat_enemy_name_override "Dire Wolf"   *set combat_enemy_epithet_override "the dire wolf"
*comment   *set combat_enemy_hp_override 14   *set combat_enemy_atk_override 4
*comment combat_lib_* is cleared by fight_cleanup.
*set combat_enemy_count 1
*set combat_apply_slot 1
*set combat_apply_type combat_lib_1
*gosub apply_enemy_type
*comment Slot 1 is mandatory; slots 2 and beyond are however many combat_lib_N the caller filled (up to
*comment combat_enemy_slot_cap), checked via a bounded loop and indirection instead of one hand-written *if per
*comment slot. Widening the slot cap needs no change here.
*temp flb_i 2
*label fight_library_slot_loop

    *set combat_enemy_count flb_i
    *set combat_apply_slot flb_i
    *set combat_apply_type flb_lib
    *gosub apply_enemy_type
  *set flb_i + 1
  *goto fight_library_slot_loop
*set combat_round 1
*if ((combat_lib_1 = "lacedon") or ((combat_lib_1 = "mire_rat") or (combat_lib_1 = "beast")))
  *gosub apply_creature_weapon_flavor
*else
  *gosub apply_generic_weapon_flavor
