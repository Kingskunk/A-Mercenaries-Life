# The Carrion Company — Story & Narrative Ground Rules

These rules govern all scene writing, character interactions, narrative pacing, and ChoiceScript mechanics for *The Carrion Company*.

---

## 1. Strict Subjective POV (Zero Omniscient Leaks)
* **Second-Person Subjective Anchor ("You"):** The narration is strictly limited to what the protagonist directly sees, hears, smells, feels, or recalls from his selected background.
* **No Authorial Knowledge:** Never name a distant location, enemy plan, faction title, or officer's secret past in the prose unless an NPC speaks it, a scout reports it, or the protagonist reads/observes it in-universe.
* **No Telepathic NPC Insight:** Never state what an NPC is thinking or feeling in omniscient terms. Describe only their physical tells—voice pitch, posture, clenched jaws, avoidance of eye contact, or nervous habits.
* **Progressive Codex Discovery:** Factions, locations, and historical lore stay locked (`[??? UNDISCOVERED]`) until an in-world dialogue or event organically reveals them.

---

## 2. Choice Design & Agency
* **Distinct Tactical Archetypes:** Choices should consistently reward different approaches:
  * **Grit:** Physical endurance, shield-work, frontline intimidation, pain tolerance.
  * **Cunning:** Flanking, spotting ambushes, sleight-of-hand, reading tells, exploiting terrain.
  * **Allure / Presence:** De-escalation, command presence, morale, gallows humor, piercing dialogue.
  * **Ruthless vs. Compassionate:** Pragmatic survival and cold efficiency vs. solidarity, mercy, and retaining humanity.
* **Fair, Telegraphed Checks:** Stat requirements should match established character progression and offer meaningful narrative divergence on both success and failure.

---

## 3. Technical & Formatting Standards
* **Responsive Typography:** Use native ChoiceScript text styling and uniform 48-character dividers (`━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━`) rather than fragile fixed-width ASCII boxes.
* **Automated Verification:** Every chapter must compile cleanly and pass both ChoiceScript `Quicktest` and `Randomtest` with 0 errors.
