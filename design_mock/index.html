The Carrion Compound
⏳ Port Valen · Hour 10:30 (Midday) Hit Points 7/7 Hunger Fed Fatigue Rested

The iron-banded oak gates open onto the Carrion Company's walled garrison grounds: a sprawling compound of log barracks, packed mud, and pitched canvas crowded behind the city's seawall. The black raven banner snaps from the central watchtower above the stores awning. The air smells of coal smoke, damp wool, horse lather, and forge slag, and the packed earth is churned with wagon ruts and hobnailed boot prints.

Pale daylight breaks cleanly over the post, drying the wagon ruts and casting sharp silhouettes of the parapet guards over the muddy ground.

The compound runs with the loud, disciplined friction of a company of mercenaries. Armorers hammer bent rivets over bench anvils, baggage carters grease dry wagon axles, and file corporals shout duty rosters across the packed clay. The yard is lined with inspection gear: mail shirts scrubbed in sand-barrels, sharpened axes racked on timber trestles, and corporals checking harness buckles down the ranks.

Near the cookhouse, a camp dog trots proudly through the mud with a gnawed mutton knuckle in its jaws, dodging a thrown turnip from an irritated cook.
